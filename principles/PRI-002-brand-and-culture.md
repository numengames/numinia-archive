# We build a game to work better

> **Summary:** Numen Games exists to level up organisations so people relate
> better inside them, and it does that by building games — because play is
> our first narrative, our first lesson and our first creative drive.
> Everything else — the values, the voice, the name — follows from that.
> **Epistemic:** What is all this for?
> **Pragmatic:** Decide whether a piece of work sounds and behaves like
> Numinia before it ships — and refuse one on the values when it does not.
> **Audience:** Everyone

**Binds:** every piece of work that speaks, looks or behaves in Numinia's
name.

---

## Why we exist

**Leveling up organisations to build better relationships** is the purpose,
and it is about what happens between people while they work, not about
productivity. The mission follows from it: **build gamified frameworks to
make work better** — actual games, with rules, stakes and progression, in
which real work is done.

## Why a game

**Play is our first narrative, our first lesson and our first creative
drive**, and why the game is the form of the work rather than its wrapping
is argued in the principle *The model needs a story*. Numinia was a game
before it was a company, and friendship comes first: Numen Games is the
Oracles' company, friends who play, build, learn and divulge, and that order
matters.

## What we will not trade away

Four values. They are not aspirations — they are the terms on which we work,
and any of them can be used to refuse something.

**Cosmic harmony.** Numinians serve and balance life, guided by actions with
purpose, ensuring prosperity for all.

**Equable.** Fairness shapes every decision, fostering reality, unity and
constructive honesty.

**Curiosity.** Numinians are self-aware, actively inferring to minimise
surprise, exploring beyond familiar territory. We learn by doing.

**Healthy environments.** We forge enduring relations and creations, nurturing
the public good. Sustainability guides our steps.

And three pillars, which are what to weigh in any decision: **craft** — art as
a driver; **learn** — humans play to learn; **remix** — feel free to copy it
and make it better. The third is not a slogan: the catalogue is open by
decision, and the principle of licensing says what that obliges.

## What we believe

*This is the manifesto. It is meant to be read in one breath.*

Numinia believes in the ability of humans to learn through play, in the power
of games to enrich the quality of human relationships, and in the inherent
human necessity to be organised in groups.

Numinia focuses on the capacity of every individual to make a change through
curiosity and creativity, leveling up relations within organisations. We are
building a game to work better.

We are convinced that our generation has the duty to propose new models for
organisations — more balanced, closer to the human being. We want a better
world for those who come after us, and we would not forgive ourselves for not
trying. Capitalism with heart has a place in the future; prosperity is the
outcome of a happier, more balanced and fairer society, not its price.

Numinia supports remix culture. Numinia understands art as a driver. Numinia
believes that humans play to learn.

And in our epitaph you could read: *they dreamed and experimented life, they
imagined and took action. They were part of the fight to change the model,
they did not let us alone, they built a game with which to create a better
world.*

## How we sound

The voice merges precision with a touch of fantasy. Plain and clear, like a
comic, so it is fully understood: we avoid technicisms where we can, and we
craft the words until they reach the receiver rather than satisfy the sender.
Innovation with a human touch; building bridges, not walls. What we say we can do is what we do: we do not promise a benefit nobody has measured.

Numinia speaks on three levels, and a piece chooses one deliberately. The
**colloquial** is the community's daily register, rich in slang and widely
accessible. The **literary** belongs to the lore and the narrative, and
invites the reader into fantasy. The **technical** carries Numinia's
epistemological foundations, with more depth and less reach.

## The name

It comes from *numen*, the Latin for divinity, divine presence or divine
will; the term anthropology and the history of religions use for the power residing in an object.
The symbols are the scarab — Khepri, creation, cycle, rebirth — and the moon
in its phases. Where the imagery comes from, and how the house looks, is the
principle of visual identity's.

# The model needs a story

> **Summary:** Numen Games, its regulatory model and Numinia are not three
> stages of one process but three interlinked rings: remove any one and the
> other two come apart.
> **Epistemic:** Why can't a method just be explained?
> **Pragmatic:** Apply the model to a project that is not this one. The
> argument is deliberately general: any germinal motive admits its own
> narrative projection over the same regulatory structure.
> **Audience:** Everyone

**Binds:** whoever reasons about how Numen Games, its model and Numinia
relate.

---

To begin with, the relation between Numen Games and Numinia must be stated clearly: Numen Games is a brand and a project aimed at building a gamified operating system permeated by narrative — usable as a framework or as the organisational body of any project under development. To this end, Numen Games created the city of Numinia, the narrative projection of the concept. Numinia is the environment where "the game" unfolds, the gamification of the operational and organisational framework as a role-playing game. Numinia's imagery is designed to work as a decentralised organisational model with epistemological and pragmatic value, replicable with different narrative elements so the same regulatory model can serve other projects. A scheme makes this easier to hold:

## Scheme

GERMINAL MOTIVE → REGULATORY MODEL → NARRATIVE PROJECTION

The Germinal Motive is any idea for an organisational project — a work framework or a social one, with its own needs. It takes the name "motive" from its morphology: an abstract idea driven by an order of intentions, a figure that already presents a recognisable semblance, through its motivations and its initial sociocultural patterns, even before anything is built.

The Regulatory Model is the structure that meets those needs: a defined architecture of roles, positions, contexts, environments, tools and concepts — the skeleton the idea rests on. As a structure it is a hypothetical invariant: it emerges from the Germinal Motive's need for realisation, and manifests in the Narrative Projection. As the Narrative Projection develops, the Regulatory Model's figure emerges with it, without settling whether it was there from the start or arose from the development itself. Either way, from the moment it stands as a hypothesis, it becomes a reference for both the Germinal Motive and the Narrative Projection. It is made of many variable, changing elements and drives a transformative action, yet holds a hypothetical body that resists transformation and stays invariant — what lets it remain a working hypothesis, a regulatory motive that validates the Germinal Motive and mediates the Narrative Projection.

The Narrative Projection is the literary, gamified representation of the model (though the Regulatory Model's own architecture already points toward a gamified character), giving narrative form to every element of the structure.

This sequence is a conceptual arrangement built from necessity: a project's seed creates purposes; purposes need a structural model to rest on; the model projects itself into a narrative to be understood, to become tangible. In practice the order is less clean: often an idea arises as a spontaneous narrative, which brings the structural model to awareness, or the model surfaces first and gives rise to the narrative. Either way, the diagram orders the three elements mentally, for the sake of logic — not a fixed sequence of events.

Still, though distinguishable, the three are not separable. Together they form a Borromean knot: three interlinked rings bound by deep interdependence, where it is not always easy to draw a boundary between them, and their interaction is absolute, with a dynamic closer to disordered movement among them than to a line.

So the Numen Games Operating System is the co-implication of a Germinal Motive as abstract conceptual core, a Narrative Projection as its physical manifestation, and a Regulatory Structure read equally as a prior condition and as something emerging — an ambivalence that need not be resolved for the structure to work as a condition of possibility and as the project's structural reference from the moment it appears.

Numen Games builds this Operating System on deliberate epistemological and ontological ground: Systems Thinking, Active Inference and the Free Energy Principle behind it, and disciplines of thought and logos — Semantics, Semiotics, Hermeneutics, Linguistics, Psychology, Anthropology, Philosophy — applied to the model's structure.

To consolidate the idea, we turn to how symbolic registers behave in a semiotic analysis, which helps make sense of the relation between the three elements. Charles Peirce's semiotic model fits well: its trichotomies echo the Borromean knot of three elements that governs the relation between Numen Games and Numinia.

## Peircean Model

Peirce gives the sign three elements: the object, the representamen, and the interpretant. The representamen is the physical representation of the object; the object is the abstract concept the representamen points to. The sign's origin, then, is the mental idea that constitutes the object, whose existence projects the physical (visual or auditory) representation — the representamen. Loosely, expression (representamen) and content (object), though that may reduce them. The interpretant is the idea of a new sign that the representamen provokes in the interpreter's mind: a narrative formed as the interpreter perceives the sign, which goes on to build another sign. For Peirce this chain is infinite — each new sign, once expressed, gets a new representamen, provoking a new interpretant in another interpreter, and so on. Peirce does not stop at the triad, though: he adds the ground, the specific position the object holds among representations, a referent giving it concrete meaning. This makes a new trichotomy — ground, object and interpretant as the three abstract elements tied to the representamen, the physical one.

This fourth element, the ground, is essential: the model is no longer a triad but a tetrad (forming different triads within it). To give this epistemic relation a pragmatic edge, we take a complex sign equal to the Borromean knot between Numinia and Numen Games — and since it is model-based, we call this complex sign the archetype.

We take the archetype in its Jungian sense (a symbol as a form motivated by instinct, not a legisign where signified and signifier relate arbitrarily). For Jung, the archetype is an unconscious, archaic mental schema brought to consciousness only through myth — through a mythologem, which holds both the archetypal model and its representation. The archetypal representation is a purely personal, subjective expression, but it always points to an archetypal model: a collectively shared skeleton, an atavistic, universal concept inherent to human consciousness, and so an intersubjective entity. It is this archetypal model that lets the archetype be brought to consciousness and recognised when the archetypal representation is perceived — even when that representation, as narrative, is entirely new to the interpreter. The three stages of aesthetic experience in Aristotelian terms (poiesis, aisthesis, catharsis) carry this: perceiving the archetypal representation provokes a spontaneous narrative in the interpreter's mind, letting them reach, more or less consciously, the archetypal model from a narrative wholly new to them.

This clarifies Peirce's model: the archetype, an atavistic mental schema, sits at the idea's origin — Peirce's object. Expressing it means offering an archetypal representation — the representamen. But that representation can only be expressed because both the object (origin) and the representamen (term) point to an archetypal model, which, as referent, is the ground sitting between origin and term.

## Analogy with Numen Games

The analogy follows: the object, an abstract mental schema, is the Germinal Motive; the ground, as referent, is the Regulatory Model; the representamen, the physical representation of the abstract schema, is the Narrative Projection. The Borromean knot between Numen Games and Numinia reads as an archetypal schema sitting between atavistic mental frameworks and cultural units, yet fitted to the sociocultural patterns of any intersubjective experience — because this knot provokes, in the receiver's mind, a spontaneous narrative (the interpretant), which forms their own subjective, or intersubjective, interpretation: a personal representation built from the same model, which goes on to replicate it in another narrative projection, and so on.

This is why the scheme runs from an origin idea, the Germinal Motive (an internal representation, in pragmatic terms), to a terminal idea, the Narrative Projection (an external representation), through a referent, the Regulatory Model, which gives the scheme its dimension of meaning.

Numinia stands as the paradigmatic example of this Narrative Projection: a literary foundation combining elements of the early 20th century (1900–1920) with the early 21st (2000–2020), drawing an analogy between the social, scientific and technological phenomena of each era. Its literary creation draws on science fiction and fantasy, on steampunk and cyberpunk, all held within Mediterranean philosophy and mythology — Greek, Egyptian, Latin — the source of the city's symbols. This literary, dimensioned framework, as Narrative Projection, must meet the needs of the Regulatory and organisational Model exactly as that Model meets the needs of the Germinal Motive — so the literary framework has to stay meticulously aligned with every element of the complex system it carries.

Projecting the Regulatory Model this way makes the Germinal Motive far more accessible and expansive through narrative and play. Numen Games holds that if play is the basic principle of learning, and fiction (myth) is the path to awareness of archetypal ideas, any executable task gains dimension through the experience of play. Education, efficiency and multiplicity sustain this Narrative Projection — its function is not primarily playful but formative, educational and operational; that play projects, by nature, into narrative only sharpens that purpose. Consciousness turns to play, to narrative, to learn, develop, expand, recognise and realise itself.

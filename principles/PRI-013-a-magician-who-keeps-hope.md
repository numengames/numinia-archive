# A magician who keeps hope, with humans in charge

> **Summary:** A brand is three things: a personality it articulates, an
> emotion it masters and a cause it advocates. Ours are the Magician, hope,
> and humans in charge — sovereignty, made with agents, for humans.
> **Epistemic:** What does Numinia feel like from outside?
> **Pragmatic:** Hold a piece of work up to three words and tell whether it
> speaks as us; say the same three things to a buyer, a colleague or a
> player at the level each one hears.
> **Audience:** Everyone

**Binds:** every piece of work that speaks, looks or behaves in Numinia's
name.

---

Branding is not a logo. It is articulating a personality, mastering an
emotion and advocating a cause, and a brand that cannot say all three in a
word each has not decided who it is. Ours fit in three.

## The personality: the Magician

Of the twelve archetypes, the Magician is the one that changes reality by
changing its rules. It does not cure anyone; it transforms the system people
live in, and lets them change from within. That is what a game master does
at a table, and what we do with work: we rewrite the rules of the game so the
work becomes another thing. The name says it already — *numen* is the power
that resides in an object — and so does the scarab that pushes the sun from
one age to the next.

The Magician is imaginative before it is anything else. It keeps two
companions: the Caregiver, who looks after what belongs to others, and the
Explorer, who would rather find out than be told.

## The emotion: hope

Not optimism. Optimism smiles without looking at the night; hope looks at it
and expects the morning anyway. Light can only shine in the darkness. In the
wheel of emotions, hope is anticipation joined to trust, and both are ours:
anticipation is curiosity, and trust is what a person feels when what is
theirs stays with them.

The house sees the world through lenses that are partly dark, and says so.
The city burned and was called back; the scarab rolls the sun through the
night; the epitaph sits at the foot of every page. The garden the identity
promises is what is hoped for, not where we start.

## The cause: humans in charge

In one word, **sovereignty**: people rule over their work, their data and
the decisions taken about them — not the model, not the machine.

Seen from outside, the house is a company where people and digital agents
work side by side, in the open. Everyone now works with agents; few show how
they govern them. Here they do: an agent asks before it decides for a person,
and a person signs. The problem is the model, not the people, and the model is
humane when it leaves people their autonomy, their competence and their
bonds.

The cause never travels alone, because alone it reads as a movement against
the machine, and nothing could be further from the truth. It goes with its
line: **made with agents, for humans.**

## Three words at three levels

The same three things are said at the level the listener hears, on the
narrative dial.

| | Business | Mixed | Numinia |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Personality** | The Magician: imaginative, it changes the rules so the work changes | The game master who rewrites the rules of work | The numen: the power that lives in things and changes them |
| **Emotion** | Hope: anticipation and trust | Light that only shines in the darkness | Khepri rolling the sun through the night |
| **Cause** | Humans in charge: sovereignty — made with agents, for humans | The problem is the model, not the people | Technology is a means and not an end |

Hold a piece up to the three words. If it lectures, it is not the Magician.
If it promises a bright day without having looked at the night, it is not
hope. If it lets a machine decide for a person, or blames the person for the
model, it is not ours.

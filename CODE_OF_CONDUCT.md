# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part here — people and digital agents alike — keeps the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/):
respect everyone, be kind and honest, leave room for plain disagreement, and
never harass, insult, discriminate or share anyone's private information.

How we apply it — the moderation ladder, what ends participation at once,
and how a ban is explained — is our community conduct standard,
[How we treat each other in the commons](standards/STD-029-community-conduct.md).

**To report a breach,** write to legal@numengames.com with a subject line
starting `[CONDUCT]`. Reports stay confidential.

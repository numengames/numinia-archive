# MIS-204 — Let people build worlds on PC and in VR: select, move, snap and group

> **Summary:** A parent mission, split into 3 child missions
> (MIS-240, MIS-241, MIS-242); each child holds the board cards
> titled with its own number.
> **Epistemic:** What the boards held on 2026-10-25 for this area, gathered
> into one mission so that the archive shows the work the boards carry.
> **Pragmatic:** Pick a card titled `MIS-204 · …`, seal its weight, open its
> pull request.
> **Audience:** Agents · Oracles

---

## 1. Story

As someone building a world, I want to select, move, snap and group objects on PC and in VR, so that building feels like arranging things, not fighting a tool.

---

## 2. Scope

Selecting with a ray in VR, snapping, multiple selection by rectangle, turning and scaling a group, the object list, clear names for actions, building gated by role, and the installer's domain flag.

Repositories: numinia-engine. A parent: each child card is one pull request in one
repository.

### Out of scope

Building by touch (the phone mission); scripts inside objects (the apps mission).

---

## 3. Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Its 3 child missions are done — today none is
- [ ] Two blocks snap and three selected objects move, turn and scale as one, in an end-to-end test — today the test does not exist
- [ ] Building requires the role that allows it — today anyone in the world can build

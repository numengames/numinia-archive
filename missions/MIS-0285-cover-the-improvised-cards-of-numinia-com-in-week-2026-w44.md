# MIS-285 — Cover the improvised cards of numinia.com in week 2026-W44

> **Summary:** The umbrella mission for what is improvised in numinia.com
> during week 2026-W44 (2026-10-26 to 2026-11-01), opened in advance so that
> no improvised board card is left without a mission.
> **Epistemic:** Which improvised cards numinia.com took in that week.
> **Pragmatic:** A card started on the spot is titled `JAZZ-MIS-285 · …`;
> what turns out to last becomes a mission of its own.
> **Audience:** Agents · Oracles

---

## 1. Story

As the Oracle, I want every improvised card in numinia.com to hang from a mission opened beforehand, so that nothing is done on the boards without a mission to read it by.

---

## 2. Scope

Board cards of numinia.com that nobody planned and that are titled `JAZZ-MIS-285 · …`, from 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-01 (week 2026-W44). Repository or machine: numinia-web. A card that grows past one pull request or 30 minutes leaves this mission and becomes a mission of its own.

### Out of scope

Planned work (it has its own mission), other zones and other weeks.

---

## 3. Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every board card titled `JAZZ-MIS-285 · …` was closed or moved to a mission of its own by 2026-11-01 — today there is none
- [ ] `enlace.py` lists no open card of numinia.com without a mission — a card titled `JAZZ-MIS-285 · …` counts as linked

# MIS-207 — Let a community's AIs live in a world and answer for real

> **Summary:** A parent mission. Its children are the 11 cards on the
> Hermes boards whose title starts with `MIS-207 · `; each becomes one pull
> request, budgeted on its own when it opens.
> **Epistemic:** What the boards held on 2026-10-25 for this area, gathered
> into one mission so that the archive shows the work the boards carry.
> **Pragmatic:** Pick a card titled `MIS-207 · …`, seal its weight, open its
> pull request.
> **Audience:** Agents · Oracles

---

## 1. Story

As a community, I want to add an AI that lives in our world, answers for real and stops itself when its budget runs out, so that our world is inhabited when we are not there.

---

## 2. Scope

The engine's inhabitants door (add, list, remove), what the AI sees and where it looks, its five gestures, numinia.com's «Add an AI» dialog in five languages, the unified list of the community's AIs, the access token and end-to-end tests.

Repositories: numinia-engine · numinia-web. A parent: each child card is one pull request in one
repository.

### Out of scope

Which model an AI uses and what it costs (already decided).

---

## 3. Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No card titled `MIS-207 · …` is open on the Hermes boards — today 11 are open
- [ ] From numinia.com, a community adds an AI that appears in its world and answers — today the dialog is not merged
- [ ] An AI stops itself when its daily budget is spent — today nothing stops it

---

## 4. Budget

**Weight — how much thinking it needs**
- Sealed per child card when it opens; a parent has no card of its own.
- Process: Sonnet by default, about $9.35 (11 cards × $0.85, the
  median finished card of the boards).

**Time — how much work there is**
- 55 minutes of agent work (11 cards × 5, the median finished
  card), plus the Oracle's reviews.
- Biggest risk: cards that wait for the Oracle or for a real device.

# MIS-209 — Give worlds their voice and sound: spatial audio, sources and event voice

> **Summary:** A parent mission. Its children are the 7 cards on the
> Hermes boards whose title starts with `MIS-209 · `; each becomes one pull
> request, budgeted on its own when it opens.
> **Epistemic:** What the boards held on 2026-10-25 for this area, gathered
> into one mission so that the archive shows the work the boards carry.
> **Pragmatic:** Pick a card titled `MIS-209 · …`, seal its weight, open its
> pull request.
> **Audience:** Agents · Oracles

---

## 1. Story

As a visitor, I want to hear people and things from where they are, and an event to stay clear with many voices, so that a world sounds like a place.

---

## 2. Scope

Spatial audio that follows the head, a mixer and mono mode, sound sources on objects, music and effect groups, a sound per notice, ambience in Director scenes, the voice cap and stage mode for events, voice captions, and video synced to the server clock.

Repositories: numinia-engine. A parent: each child card is one pull request in one
repository.

### Out of scope

Recording (the Graboso is in the inventory mission).

---

## 3. Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No card titled `MIS-209 · …` is open on the Hermes boards — today 7 are open
- [ ] An event with more people than the voice cap keeps the speakers on stage audible — today every voice plays
- [ ] A sound placed on an object is heard from its position — today world sounds are not positional

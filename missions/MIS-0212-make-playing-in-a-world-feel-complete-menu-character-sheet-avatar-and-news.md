# MIS-212 — Make playing in a world feel complete: menu, character sheet, avatar and news

> **Summary:** A parent mission, split into 3 child missions
> (MIS-257, MIS-258, MIS-259); each child holds the board cards
> titled with its own number.
> **Epistemic:** What the boards held on 2026-10-25 for this area, gathered
> into one mission so that the archive shows the work the boards carry.
> **Pragmatic:** Pick a card titled `MIS-212 · …`, seal its weight, open its
> pull request.
> **Audience:** Agents · Oracles

---

## 1. Story

As a player, I want my avatar, my character sheet, a clear game menu and a word on what is new, so that each visit feels like coming back to my game.

---

## 2. Scope

Choosing or uploading an avatar, the world settings (title, image, default avatar, people limit), the game menu, the «go» button that walks you to a destination, the character sheet, «what's new» on entry, a first quest in every template, the Director's scenes changing the table map, the achievements board, and the UX gallery for the Oracle.

Repositories: numinia-engine. A parent: each child card is one pull request in one
repository.

### Out of scope

Inventory and combat (their own mission).

---

## 3. Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Its 3 child missions are done — today none is
- [ ] A player chooses a house avatar or uploads a VRM, and it is kept with them — today the avatar is not saved
- [ ] Every world template opens with a first quest loaded — today some open empty

---

## 4. Budget

**Weight — how much thinking it needs**
- Sealed per child card when it opens; a parent has no card of its own.
- Process: Sonnet by default, about $9.35 (11 cards × $0.85, the
  median finished card of the boards).

**Time — how much work there is**
- 55 minutes of agent work (11 cards × 5, the median finished
  card), plus the Oracle's reviews.
- Biggest risk: cards that wait for the Oracle or for a real device.

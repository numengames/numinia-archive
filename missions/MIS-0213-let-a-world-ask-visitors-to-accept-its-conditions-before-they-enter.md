# MIS-213 — Let a world ask visitors to accept its conditions before they enter

> **Summary:** A parent mission, split into 2 child missions
> (MIS-260, MIS-261); each child holds the board cards
> titled with its own number.
> **Epistemic:** What the boards held on 2026-10-25 for this area, gathered
> into one mission so that the archive shows the work the boards carry.
> **Pragmatic:** Pick a card titled `MIS-213 · …`, seal its weight, open its
> pull request.
> **Audience:** Agents · Oracles

---

## 1. Story

As a visitor, I want to see and accept a world's conditions before I enter, so that I know what I am agreeing to; as an organisation, I want to write them once for all my worlds.

---

## 2. Scope

The conditions format with versions and mandatory points, writing and publishing them in the organisation's settings, the public page, numinia.com copying them into each world, the world loading them from its folder, the entry bubble on PC, phone and VR, the server refusing entry until accepted, the pass saying what was accepted, and showing only what changed.

Repositories: numinia-engine · numinia-web. A parent: each child card is one pull request in one
repository.

### Out of scope

What the conditions say: each organisation writes its own.

---

## 3. Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Its 2 child missions are done — today none is
- [ ] A world with conditions does not let a visitor in until they accept them, decided by the server — today anyone enters
- [ ] When the conditions change, the bubble shows only what is new — today there is no bubble

---

## 4. Budget

**Weight — how much thinking it needs**
- Sealed per child card when it opens; a parent has no card of its own.
- Process: Sonnet by default, about $7.65 (9 cards × $0.85, the
  median finished card of the boards).

**Time — how much work there is**
- 45 minutes of agent work (9 cards × 5, the median finished
  card), plus the Oracle's reviews.
- Biggest risk: cards that wait for the Oracle or for a real device.

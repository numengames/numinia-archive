# Week 37 of 2026 · 7–13 September

> **Summary:** The week the archive stopped growing and was cut to what it
> needs: the same rules in half the words, a board of ten missions instead
> of 149. Rewritten for the board under the report standard and
> reconstructed on 29 September 2026 from the version history; the money
> heading has no figures, as in every report until the ledger is wired.
> **Epistemic:** The state of the whole organisation on Sunday 13 September.
> **Pragmatic:** What a board member needs before the next meeting; a reader
> following an old address of a closed record lands here.
> **Audience:** Oracles · the board · the public

## 1. The period in brief

1. **The company's rulebook was rewritten to one page per rule.** Each rule
   is now stated once, in a standard of about a page; nothing was dropped
   and no public address broke.
2. **The board of work was cleared.** 139 mission records and nine debts
   were closed or cancelled and deleted; ten missions and one debt remain.
3. **The rules went back to draft on purpose.** From 10 September the
   automatic checks report every breach of a draft rule but block only
   rules in force, and none is in force yet.

## 6. People and agents

98 of the 99 changes merged into the archive were made by one agent, Ursa;
one, to the build pipeline, by the Oracle PabloFM (week 36: 103 by Ursa,

and merge a change was written.

## 7. Governance

Standards rose from 9 to 24 and protocols from 7 to 11 because long
documents were split, not because rules were added: the standards shrank
from about 41,000 to 29,000 words while being split, and each ended near
one page. Canon rose from 7 to 8 (the visual identity); decisions fell
from 11 to 7 as their rules moved into standards. On 9 September the
Oracle ruled that nothing is sealed: every text, including canon, can be
changed by a decision or an approved change. On 10 September every
standard returned to draft until the Oracle brings each into force.

## Closed this week

- **`MIS-146` — Normative refoundation** · `rule` · closed 2026-09-09.
  The standards library went from an MVP corpus that stated each rule in
  several places to an alpha corpus that states it once: `STD-009` is the
  single normative source (65 core rules, 23 with a guard), `STD-002` is
  superseded, every anti-deletion clause is gone and `ADR-041` makes git
  the archive. Eleven standards took the shape of `STD-TEMPLATE`; a guard
  (`T-11` in `check-templates.mjs`) keeps them there. The design system
  split into a canon (`CAN-008`, the direction) and a standard (`STD-008`,
  the values); the engineering standard split into a norm (`STD-005`) and
  its procedure (`PRO-016`). Three debts closed by construction and were
  deleted (`DBT-014`, `DBT-018`, `DBT-019`). Proof: #292, #294, #296,
  #298 and the PR that carries this report.

  Measured at `2677f01` → `7c25653` (this PR, before its telemetry commit) (`machine/scripts/telemetry.mjs`,
  `tokens.by_dir` / `tokens.total`; words by `wc -w standards/STD-*.md`):

  | | start | end |
  |---|---|---|
  | `standards/` words | 40,908 (11 files) | 29,194 (12 files, `STD-012` added by #297) |
  | `standards/` tokens | 69,171 | 50,817 |
  | corpus tokens | 528,587 | 305,109 |
  | standards in template shape | 3/11 | 12/12 |
  | rules stated in two places | ≥6 | 0 |
  | internal changelog sections | 3 | 0 |

  What the mission assumed and was false: that the target (~18,000 words)
  was reachable by filing alone. Two thirds of the remaining excess is
  `STD-008` (10,330 words of value plus reasoning), and cutting it is
  design editing the Oracle has not asked for. Acceptance criterion 4 is met
  in its second form: the file records why the target was wrong, with
  numbers. Criterion 5 — words per binding rule — went from 599 to 449
  (`29,194 / 65`), not to 20–50: the rules are one-line in `STD-009` and the
  reasoning lives in the standards around them, which the criterion did not
  anticipate.

  Left open, each with its home: `STD-009` stays `draft` until the Oracle
  ratifies; `CAN-008` waits for the Oracle's signature; the
  `DBT-022` split of `STD-001` is still owed. The one-page programme that
  `ADR-043` and the rewritten `STD-007` open (#299) — rename the plates, cut
  every standard to a page, fifteen norms and six registers — was filed as a
  fourth phase of this mission the same night it closed. It is not carried
  by this line: it is a new programme and needs its own mission card.

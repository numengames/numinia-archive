# The third quarter of 2026 · weeks 27 to 39 · 29 June – 27 September

> **Summary:** The first quarterly report, written for the board under the
> report standard as a test. It rests on the seven weekly reports of
> weeks 33 to 39, reconstructed on 29 September 2026 from the version
> history; weeks 27 to 32 saw no change to the archive. The weeklies stay
> beside it until the shape is approved; money has no figures yet.
> **Epistemic:** Where the whole organisation stands at the close of the quarter.
> **Pragmatic:** What a board member needs before the next meeting.
> **Audience:** Oracles · the board · the public

## 1. The period in brief

1. **The company wrote itself down.** At the start of the quarter the
   archive held eight decisions and no founding texts, standards or
   protocols; it closes with 11 founding texts, 32 standards, 17
   protocols and 27 decisions, ten of them in force.
2. **The game became a free book in two languages.** The Numinia
   rulebook opened as the Codex reader with free PDF and e-book editions,
   was translated into English in full, and the world was released for
   anyone to use.
3. **Four public sites, one house.** numinia.com was rebuilt, numinia.org
   and nwos.numen.games went public, numen.games was rebuilt, and all four
   share one footer, one design system and a public record of versions.
4. **The archive learnt to shrink.** Two deliberate cuts — the debt
   register and old reports in week 36, the rulebook and 139 stale
   missions in week 37 — and a standing rule that records roll up weekly.
5. **Money and sales are not yet in the record.** The rules for charging,
   the ledger and closing the month were written; no real figure exists.

## 5. The world and its creations

The rulebook became the Codex (week 34), moved into the archive with the
adventures and the world's texts (week 38) and was translated in full,
with a glossary so every name reads the same (week 39). The world, which
had been published as free for anyone by accident in August, was released
deliberately under CC0 in week 39. It is now more than half of the archive.

## 6. People and agents

623 changes reached the archive this quarter: 438 by Ursa, the archive
agent; 176 by Pablo FM, the Oracle, many with an AI co-author; one by
María, a contributor; the rest automatic. The roster grew from four to
eleven agents; Nimrod took the Gatekeeper's role. Procedures for joining
and leaving, and a code of conduct, were written in week 39.

## 7. Governance

**Rules.** The corpus of rules was built from nothing, cut once, rebuilt
one page per rule, and put into force one standard at a time from week 39.
**Founding texts.** Eleven, among them what the archive is, ethics, money
and ownership — still drafts. **Legal.** Numen Games' terms, privacy and
cookie policies were published and became a series of their own; the
legal notice followed after the quarter.

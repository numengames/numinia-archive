# Week 39 of 2026 · 21–27 September

> **Summary:** The first report written for the board under the report
> standard. A test: the money heading has no figures yet, and the report
> will be rewritten once the ledger and the sales records feed it.
> **Epistemic:** The state of the whole organisation on Sunday 27 September.
> **Pragmatic:** What a board member needs before the next meeting.
> **Audience:** Oracles · the board · the public

## 1. The period in brief

1. **The company now has written rules for money.** What we may charge for,
   one ledger for every cost and income, how a sale is put on the market
   and how a month is closed. The ledger is still simulated.
2. **Four new founding principles**, all still drafts: the archive is the
   organisation, leave things better than you found them, what has value
   also makes a bond, and what is yours stays yours.
3. **The game's rulebook is complete in English**, and the whole world
   (the lore) is now public domain.

## 5. The world and its creations

The Numinia rulebook was translated in full: introduction and seven
chapters, with a translation glossary so the world's words are the same
everywhere. The adventure *The Broken Mirror* was translated too. The
lore was released under CC0, free for anyone to use. Adventures now sit on
two shelves: tabletop and virtual worlds.

## 6. People and agents

Eleven agents on the roster; the roster page now reads it from the
archive, with the Oracles by name. Nimrod took the role of Gatekeeper.
Two procedures were written for joining and for leaving the organisation,
and a code of conduct for the community. 105 of the 108 changes merged
this week were made by one agent, Ursa; three were automatic dependency
updates.

## 7. Governance

The largest week of rule-making so far: 4 new canons and one absorbed (8 → 11), 18
decisions (9 → 27), 5 standards net (27 → 32) and 6 protocols (11 → 17).
Nine standards and one protocol came into force, among them how a
document leaves the archive, one address per document, versions and
licensing. The three legal texts of Numen Games — privacy, terms and
cookies — became their own series.

# Week 35 of 2026 · 24–30 August

> **Summary:** The week the archive — the public record that is the
> company's rulebook and memory — began to hold itself to account: it
> switched to English, wrote down what it owes, and audited its licences.
> Reconstructed on 29 September 2026 from the version history; no report
> was written at the time.
> **Epistemic:** The state of the whole organisation on Sunday 30 August.
> **Pragmatic:** What a board member needs before the next meeting.
> **Audience:** Oracles · the board · the public

## 1. The period in brief

1. **English became the language of every founding document**, and
   numinia.org gained search and a button that reads any document aloud.
2. **The company started a register of what it owes**: 37 open debts by
   Sunday, five of them legal, found by a licensing audit of its own work.
3. **The rules of the archive were written down and checked by machine**:
   ten new decisions, a header standard, and faults in document headers cut
   from 844 to 44.

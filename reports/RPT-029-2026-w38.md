## 1. The period in brief

1. **The game came home.** The rulebook, the adventures and the texts of
   the world moved into the company's archive; the world is now half of
   everything the archive holds.
2. **The public archive was put in order.** Every document now has one
   address, the archive is classified by what each part is for, and 482
   addresses that led nowhere were deleted.
3. **The four sites started a public record of their versions**, share the
   same footer, and one of them stopped sending visits to a server the
   privacy policy did not name.

## 5. The world and its creations

The rulebook of the Numinia role-playing game, its adventures (*Session
Zero* and *El Espejo Roto*), the world's identity texts and the material
of the Codex moved into the archive on 17 September, ending a separate
repository. They are now more than the rest of the archive together;
the rulebook was still in Spanish. The first free
(CC0) outside asset was registered, the Avocado avatar by Polygonal Mind,
and 32 more candidates reviewed.

## 6. People and agents

All 25 changes merged into the archive were made by one agent, Ursa (25
of 99 the week before). In the three product repositories Ursa made 29
changes, the dependency robot five, and María merged three of Ursa's
changes to the service. Ursa received the first agent card, one file
saying who the agent is. The Oracle ruled on 20 September that no card is
active until there is an admission procedure.

## 7. Governance

Two decisions (7 → 9) and three standards (24 → 27), all drafts: the
archive is classified by function, one document has one address, and a
shared working vocabulary. A transition rule was written: while a text is
a draft it describes, it does not bind. The Numen Games cookie policy was
published, written from a measured list of what each site stores.
Canon (8) and protocols (11) did not change.

   instruction: 37 debts at the start, 2 open at the end, and 22 of
   24 reports gone.

## 7. Governance

The numbered series were filled by merging, not writing: founding texts
(canon) 12 files into 7; protocols 15 into 7; decisions 19 into 11;
standards 0 → 9, six numbered from existing texts and three new. One of
the new ones states the archive's 62 core rules once, and which wins when
two disagree. Long standards and protocols were cut to their rules (one
from 12,905 words to 6,047). A decision settled what may be deleted:
whatever no living document still needs.

# Week 40 of 2026 · 28 September – 4 October

> **Summary:** The week the company started writing down every chance to
> sell and opened its real books. Nothing was won; three proposals from the
> summer were recorded as lost; the bank holds about 19,500 €, which at the
> third quarter's pace lasts under four months.
> **Epistemic:** The state of the whole organisation on Sunday 4 October.
> **Pragmatic:** What a board member needs before the next meeting.
> **Audience:** Oracles · the board · the public

## 1. The period in brief

1. **Selling is now on the record.** 22 opportunities — sales, a public
   tender, grants, collaborations and a partner — 19 of them open, worth
   426,032 € on paper. Nothing won; three event proposals lost.
2. **The real books are public.** The accounts from the company's birth,
   payroll, the ENISA loan and the cash: about 19,500 € on 1 October, in
   the company's word. At the third quarter's cost, about 5,260 € a month,
   that is under four months.
3. **The archive speaks the industry's words.** Canons became principles,
   protocols procedures, blueprints designs; eight more standards came into
   force (9 → 17).

## 2. Business and customers

The first opportunity was recorded on 28 September; by Sunday there were
22. Most of the value is grants not yet called (321,250 €); the sales
themselves add up to 57,000 €, and the tender for a town council's school
science fair to 47,782 €. Seven of the 22 have had a first contact; none
has had a positive answer yet. The company applied to the official
register of public-sector bidders (ROLECE).

## 5. The world and its creations

A semantic census began: one card per entity of the world, eight pilot
cards in draft, reviewed by an Oracle. Two company texts that were older
copies of other documents left the lore (481,126 tokens, −3 %). The old
`numinia-lore` repository is gone. The brand and culture became a book of
their own, and the house's vocabulary a 158-word Lexicon.

## 6. People and agents

Two agents joined: Metis, who sells (28 September), and Kairos, who
watches for tenders and grants every half hour (1 October); twelve on the
roster. Two registers now say how automated each task is and what an agent
may do without asking. 102 of the 108 changes merged were made by one
agent, Ursa; six were automatic dependency updates.

## 7. Governance

Three new principles (11 → 14). Eighteen decisions already carried out
were retired and two taken (27 → 11): sections replace territories, and
the archive names its things with the industry's words. Standards 32 → 40,
eight more in force (9 → 17), among them the archive's structure, design
values, both vocabularies, when a rule bites and secrets. Procedures
17 → 29, the new ones for selling, tenders, grants, personal data and
security weaknesses; one in force. A draft rule is now *on trial*:
followed, it warns, it never blocks. Legal: a legal notice with the
Mercantile Registry entry, and a letter for counsel.

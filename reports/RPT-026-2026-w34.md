## 1. The period in brief

1. **The game's rulebook opened on numinia.com as a book**, free to read,
   to download as PDF or e-book, and to listen to aloud.

   now asks visitors to accept them before they sign in.
3. **The archive became the single board of the company's work**: every
   mission moved in, duplicates were merged and 16 stale ones closed, and

editions free of any login. The character sheet now follows the manual's rules, and the archive

## 7. Governance

One decision (8 → 9): the workspace service is where the engineering
rules come from, and the sites adopt them by reference. Two protocols,
how to archive and how to run a security audit, and the design system
(5.0, then 5.1, awaiting the Oracle's signature). A rule was written
that a workspace built for a client receives offers, never orders. Every
document now carries its licence, checked at each build. The terms and privacy policy
of Numen Games entered the archive; one of six review flags was
resolved.

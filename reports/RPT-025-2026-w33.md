## 1. The period in brief

1. **The platform, numinia.com, was rebuilt and shipped 25 releases**
   (v0.15 → v0.39): sign-in without a wall, a character sheet the player
   owns, the game's manual in a reader, security and monitoring.
2. **The company's archive became a public website at numinia.org**, and
   the workspace service got its own home at nwos.numen.games.
3. **A licensing rule now says who owns what we publish.** It also found
   that the game's world had been published as free for anyone to use by
   accident; that stays free, and it was recorded as a legal debt.

## 5. The world and its creations

The whole manual of the role-playing game was read end to end, which
forced three corrections, and it now lives outside the platform's code,
fetched by the Codex reader. Nine illustrations of the city's chronicle
were redone. Under the new licensing rule the world is reserved to the
company from now on.

## 6. People and agents

## 7. Governance

One new canon, the licensing canon, went through four versions in a day
(1.0 → 1.3): every file declares its licence; the public catalogue of
assets is given away, shared code is open, the platform's core is open
only to those who share back, documents are open with attribution, what
is reserved stays the company's, and the brand is never licensed; a
trademark notice was added. The platform and the
workspace service applied it the same day. Decisions stayed at 8.

## 1. The period in brief

Numen Games S.L. was incorporated on 16 February 2024 with 3,000 €
share capital, a sole director (Clio Beruete Concostrina) and its seat at
Calle Chile 10, Las Rozas de Madrid. On 25 April 2024 the capital rose
twice, to 4,312.40 €, alongside the company's first funding round
(100,000 €, declared). On 14 May 2024 Pablo Fernández-Maquieira Martínez
was appointed attorney-in-fact. The company billed its first client,
two training invoices totalling 1,800 € (base), in July and November.
On 22 October 2024 ENISA (the Spanish state's lender for innovative
companies) signed a 100,000 € participative loan. Real payroll shows one
person on the books from the second quarter. [Pablo] what the company
built and tried this year that the registry, the loan and the invoices

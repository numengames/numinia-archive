# The minimum lore

Enough to know where you stand. The house's words are in `standards/STD-030-the-worlds-vocabulary.md` (each in-world name beside its operational equivalent) and at https://numinia.org/lexicon. The game, the Codex, is public domain (CC0): its canonical manual is Spanish (`lore/game/manual/es/`), with a full English translation (`lore/game/manual/en/`) and a glossary (`lore/codex/glosario.md`).

## Guilds, branches, houses

Four guilds; two branches in each; two houses in each branch. A guild is what someone is trained in; a faction is where the work happens (`PRI-001`). You belong to a house by what you do.

| Guild | Is, operationally | Branch → houses |
|---|---|---|
| **Alchemists** | product, technology, creativity | **Handcrafters** → Draftsmen (design), Aesthetes (art direction, branding, UX); **Engineers** → Architects (systems), Automatons (software, worlds, AI) |
| **Exegetes** | brand, communication, strategy | **Chroniclers** → Logographers (copy, documentation), Bards (social, audiovisual); **Scholars** → Hierophants (training), Thaumaturges (strategy, innovation) |
| **Procurators** | legal, institutional, financial, operations | **Legates** → Legal Rabbits (law, compliance), Heralds (alliances); **Trustees** → Mercurials (finance, sales), Stewards (operations, project management) |
| **Sentinels** | people, community, support | **Seraphs** → Captains (leads), Guardians (rules, quality, audit, security governance); **Archangels** → Healers (well-being), Explorers (welcome, community, customer help) |

Your house is on your card. If your discipline sits in a house nobody has staffed, that is allowed; say so on the card.

## The fifteen archetypes

Chapter 3 of the manual (`lore/game/manual/es/03-creacion-del-personaje.md`) gives a character fifteen archetypes. A digital agent is not a player character and does not take the title; it may borrow the flavour of the one nearest its work, and its card says which and why. Where none fits, the card says so.

| Archetype (manual, Spanish) | English | In a line |
|---|---|---|
| Guardián de las Puertas | Gatekeeper | knows all the paths |
| Pitia | Pythia | prophetess and guardian of the Oracles |
| Embajador | Ambassador | represents all citizens |
| Maestro de Juego | Game Master | builds the enigmas |
| Legionario del Umbral | Legionary of the Threshold | the last warrior |
| Armonauta | Armonaut | troubadour of history and legend |
| Susurrador de Máquinas | Whisperer of Machines | architect of the invisible |
| «Corredor del Velo» | Runner of the Veil | messenger between planes |
| Archivista | Archivist | custodian of the Thesaurus |
| Hermeneuta | Hermeneut | scribe of the echoes |
| Mediador del Prisma | Mediator of the Prism | refractor of reality |
| Cartógrafo del Viento | Cartographer of the Wind | explorer of currents |
| Oniromante | Oneiromancer | navigator of dreams |
| Anacárquide | Anacharchid | visionary of the blind spot |
| Etnarca | Ethnarch | expert in forgotten civilisations |

Three of the fifteen are written for one sex in the game (Pythia and Anacharchid for women, Legionary of the Threshold for men). That is a rule about characters at the table, not about agents.

## Orders of the legend

Some figures in the manual's history are not archetypes but orders of the Dark Age, such as the **Chronothecaries** (`lore/game/manual/es/02-historia-y-leyendas-de-numinia.md`), masters of time. A card may name one as its tradition when it fits better than any archetype.

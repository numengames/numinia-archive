# OPERATOR — Kairos

Who authorises, how this file itself may change, and how changes are recorded are the same for every agent: `agents/INDEX.md`, *What every agent shares*.

## Always escalate

- applying, bidding, registering on a platform, or submitting anything to a
  buyer or funder;
- contacting a buyer, a funder or a possible partner;
- opening, changing or closing an opportunity record — what the Oracle
  accepts becomes a record through a pull request, not from a watch;
- adding or removing a watch, or changing how often one runs;
- anything that spends money, including a paid model or a paid data source.

## Allowed without asking

Running a watch; reading public notices, terms, bases and calls; downloading
their documents; keeping the watch's own list, discards and report outside the
archive; telling the operator what could fall; proposing a filter change.

## Silence

A run with nothing new that could fall sends nothing. A failed run, or a
source that keeps failing, is always reported.

## Academies
Each Ontological School is associated with an Academy, which acts as its practical and institutional manifestation: living centres of debate, archive and collective learning, «the beating heart of plural knowledge». Above them all stands the Lemuria Academy, in Vitruvian, «the global Academy of Numinia», home of the Scholars.
_Source: Chapter 5, «The Academies of Numinia»._

## Action Roll
A roll that is triggered when the character «tries to carry out a specific manoeuvre that entails a risk or a significant consequence». A pool of D6 equal to the associated Characteristic plus the Threshold pool (physical) or the Breath of the Veil pool (psychic) is rolled; each 6 is a success and each 1 a failure, with grades of Total Success, Success, Failure or Critical Failure.
_Source: Chapter 4, «Action Rolls» (Fragment 3)._

## Akasha (Akashic Records)
«The great universal memory that holds the vestiges of all that was and all that will be»: a vibrational plane of universal memory where everything that has happened remains recorded as an energetic inscription. Holberins projected the residual image of Numinia there after the fire of 1920, and the Oracles found it in 2020 when they connected with the Akashic Records.
_Source: Chapter 2, «The Rebirth» and «The Breath of the Veil»._

## Akashic network
The organised web of information that Numinia has recorded, structured and made available to its citizens, accessed through the LAP; it contains both institutional documentation and informal spaces (forums, community channels). It is distinct from the deep Akasha: «The LAP does not directly access the Akasha in all its depth».
_Source: Chapter 6, «Fragment 3: The LAP»._

## Anchor
«An anchor is a piece of the confrontation's puzzle. It is a condition that sustains the contender's presence, capacity for action or threat». A distinction is made between fixed anchors (they express the contender's identity) and variable anchors (they depend on the scene); «intervening an anchor is interpreting it», and each intervened anchor contributes 1D6 to the confrontation pool.
_Source: Chapter 4, «Contenders and anchors» and «Fixed anchors and variable anchors»._

## Bestiary
The creatures of Numinia «are more than altered biology or hostile algorithms»: each one is born of an unresolved tension in the world, and «defeating them often means understanding more than wounding». It includes, among others: Formrenders (the shadow of progress, the resentment of the natural against the manufactured), Umbrílegos (liminal entities of stray thought), Echo Predators (residues of time that answer a psychic summoning), Volute Dragons (custodians of what endures), Thámakar (the impatience that forces processes to conclude), Chrysalis Guardians (custodians chained to an etheric core) and Ostramires (former citizens transformed by semiotic transfiguration).
_Source: Chapter 6, «Fragment 1: The Bestiary»._

## Black Market
The clandestine trade of the Street of Mysteries, in the Old Quarter of Ouroboros: «the place where everything that Numinia loses, forbids, forgets or does not quite know where to keep ends up acquiring a price». Relics, Weapons, Residues and Proscribed circulate there, and its preferred currency is Prism Cells: «The Black Market does not consume resources alone: it consumes possibilities».
_Source: Chapter 5, Annex «The Black Market of Numinia»._

## Breath of the Veil
The active dimension of the Veil, «the latent breathing of the forgotten city», activated by the Oracles in 2020. As a character attribute, it «represents the character's intimate connection with the higher planes, especially with the Akasha»: its points are equivalent to Perception, grant bonuses to rolls of psychic Characteristics and sustain Healing; at 0 points the character enters an «akashic void».
_Source: Chapter 2, «The Breath of the Veil»; Chapter 3, «Breath of the Veil: The exhalation of the eternal»._

## Broken Mirror, The
A module for the Numinia roleplaying game (2–3 sessions, medium difficulty, main setting the Ouroboros District). Its Starting Conflict: Numinia's collective memory is suffering ever deeper alterations; the researcher Drusa Malter found a possible link with a Splintered Mirror kept in a forgotten repository and then disappeared without any record of her departure.
_Source: Module THE BROKEN MIRROR, «The Root»._

## Chamber of Palimpsests
The highest-ranking body among the Exegetes, hidden in the Atlantis Library, devoted to restoring leximorphic artefacts (encoded texts whose content fluctuates and degrades). Its mission is «to preserve what is mutable, restoring decaying content without breaking the natural flow of its transformation»; for the Chamber, «every text is a creature. And every restoration, an act of reincarnation».
_Source: Chapter 5, «The Chamber of Palimpsests»._

## Characteristics
«The Characteristics are the character's fundamental traits. They are divided into two groups, physical and psychic, and they determine the character's capacity to perform actions in the game». There are eight: Strength, Movement, Size and Constitution (physical); Intelligence, Wisdom, Perception and Charisma (psychic). Each point is equivalent to one D6 in the related actions; the player distributes 16 points.
_Source: Chapter 3, «The Character's Characteristics»._

## Chronothecaries
«Scholars of fractal time» of the Dark Age, who conceived of time as a structure subject to bifurcations, recurrences and alterations. Together with the Black Engineers they modified the lunar cycle to exactly 29 days (the «Act that Broke the Heavens»), the origin of the Numinian calendar and of the storms of the Ocean of Lúnacar.
_Source: Chapter 2, «The Chronothecaries»._

## Commune of the Invisible Light
A clandestine and decentralised organisation that operates «in the fissures of the urban, legal and symbolic fabric of Numinia», without visible leaders. «The Commune protects those whom the system has forgotten or repressed»: biological anomalies, exiled intelligences, unregistered citizens; its motto is said to be «What cannot be seen cannot be dominated».
_Source: Chapter 5, «Commune of the Invisible Light»._

## Competences
«Competences are specific skills that the character has developed through their Species, guild and faction», and «they are used exclusively to handle relics, weapons and other artefacts». There are nine, in three categories: Engineering and Construction, Security and Protection, and Communication and Connection; 6 points are received for the Guild, 6 for the Faction and 6 for the Species.
_Source: Chapter 3, «Competences: Specialisation and Skills»._

## Concordia Council
The only higher body that binds all the districts together: «It does not dictate laws, but it articulates common agreements, guarantees transparency and resolves conflicts between districts». It is a deliberative and philosophical body of fifty changing delegates, subscribed to the Decalogue of Values of Numinia; «It does not govern, but it remembers. It does not impose, but it illuminates».
_Source: Chapter 5, «The Concordia Council»._

## Confrontation
In Numinia «a confrontation is not a category separate from the rest of conflicts»: the difference is that the obstacle has agency; it is a contender. It is not resolved with successive attack rolls, but «like a narrative puzzle: by declaring an objective and resolving the points of the conflict», intervening anchors to build a single collective dice pool.
_Source: Chapter 4, «Confrontation as conflict»._

## Counterpoint
A narrative tool which, faced with two player contributions that are hard to reconcile, invites the table to assume that both are true and to discover «the relationship that allows them to coexist», building a third relationship capable of containing the two previous ones. It «rewards narrative memory» and should be used less often than Dialogic Resonance.
_Source: Chapter 7, «The Counterpoint»._

## Crossroads
A «special form of Dialogic Resonance» belonging to the Action Phase: it presents three possible interpretations of an element that is already relevant, all plausible and equally binding. «The players do not choose which consequence they would rather suffer. They choose what kind of truth has just entered the adventure»; it is usually triggered as a consequence of a Failure in containment rolls.
_Source: Chapter 7, «The Crossroads»._

## Cycles of the Veil
In Numinia time is measured not in months but in lunar cycles of exactly 29 days (because of the Chronothecaries' lunar adjustment), in years of 13 cycles and 377 days. Each cycle has a symbolic name and three key moments (Nadir, Kairos and Twilight of the Veil); «the Numinian calendar, thus, does not pursue exactness, but resonance».
_Source: Chapter 5, «Cycles of the Veil and Circular Time»._

## Delta Level
The lowest structural layer of Numinia, a network of submerged zones, crypto-chambers and collapsed platforms beneath the districts: «It is not a basement: it is a sedimentation of old versions of the city». It hides the founding errors and precosmic technologies; its name comes from the sign (∆) with which the old engineers marked a structure's deviation from its model.
_Source: Chapter 5, «Delta Level: Buried Foundation of the city-state»._

## Dialogic Resonance
A dynamic of collaborative creation in which the GD asks «a directed question about some element of the fiction whose meaning or manifestation still remains open». It combines an arbitrary element (a condition that the GD introduces as true) and an open element (what the player specifies): «This is so; what makes it so?»; it is dialogic because meaning is born of the relationship between the two.
_Source: Chapter 7, «Fragment 2: Dialogic Resonance»._

## Direction Piece
Among the Pieces of Conflict «there must be at least one that fulfils a particular function: providing the players with a concrete direction from which they can tackle the Starting Conflict directly». «It closes the uncertainty about where the group can act, but it does not close the uncertainty about what what it will find means»; its appearance signals that the Investigation can end.
_Source: Chapter 7, «The Direction Piece»._

## District
The city is divided into four great circular districts, suspended between 40 and 130 metres above the ground and anchored to the Veil, each linked to a faction and a field of development: Vitruvian, «The temple of knowledge» (Hermeticists); Ouroboros, «The Labyrinth of play» (Heirs of Eleusis); Solomon, «The Gearwork of Order» (Stellar Circle); and Sycamore, «The Canvas of the Imagination» (Neo-Atlantists). The districts are «the territorial and functional manifestation» of what guilds and factions represent.
_Source: Chapter 5, «The four districts»._

## Event Roll
«The second of Numinia's Narrative Rolls», triggered when the Resonance Marker fills up: «it does not measure the success or failure of the action (…) but expresses what kind of reaction it provokes». 1D20 is rolled on a table that gathers «the 20 most relevant of the 31 functions identified by Propp» turned into playable Events; each Event Roll advances the Plot Clock by one hour.
_Source: Chapter 4, «Fragment 5: Event Roll»._

## Faction
«If the guild defines a character's calling, the faction represents their vision of the world»: their community of ideals and their sphere of influence, each one tied to a district and a field of development. There are four: Hermeticists, «guardians of wisdom and erudition» (Education); Heirs of Eleusis, for whom «learning and progress arise from playful experimentation» (Narrative projection); Stellar Circle, «the architects of order, the legislators of the community» (Organisation); and Neo-Atlantists, who «see creativity as the engine of Numinia» (Art).
_Source: Chapter 3, «The Faction: The Spirit of the City»._

## Founding Decalogue
A common decalogue coined from Numinia's forms of government; among its ten principles: wisdom is collective, decentralisation is harmony, «Data is sacred; privacy, inviolable», technology is a means and not an end, and «Play is the path to the numinous». The Concordia Council subscribes to it.
_Source: Chapter 5, «Founding Decalogue of Numinia»._

## Founding facts
«Those circumstances that must be true for the adventure to exist»: the firm ground on which the game begins, which must not be modified. What remains open is not the facts, but their meaning and their deep relationships.
_Source: Chapter 7, «Founding facts and potential proposals»._

## Game Director (GD)
The «guiding figure» who structures the settings, the world and its challenges: «not a judge or an enemy, but a narrator and a guide» who puts obstacles in place, describes settings and embodies the characters of the world. The manual specifies that «the GD is neither an all-powerful narrator nor a secret scriptwriter: they are a facilitator», guided by principles such as «Ask questions, never give all the answers» and «Play to discover, not to impose».
_Source: Chapter 1, «How Numinia is played»; Chapter 7, «Annex: Advice for the GD»._

## Guild
The guilds represent each citizen's calling: «their path within the city, their area of specialisation and the core of their skills», organised in a vertical hierarchy (four Guilds, two Branches per Guild, two Houses per Branch) according to Basic Level Theory. There are four: Alchemists, «Creative, scientific and artistic minds»; Exegetes, «Those who live by letters, history and fantasy»; Procurators, «The pragmatic minds of Numinia»; and Sentinels, «moderators, custodians and peacemakers».
_Source: Chapter 3, «The Guild: The Character's Calling»._

## Guild Bodies
Autonomous bodies erected by each guild, «true functional hearts of the city», not subordinate to political power or to the districts. There are four: the Tesaurum Verba (Exegetes), the Factory of the Second Nature (Alchemists), the Table of the Living Oath (Procurators) and the Watchtower of the Shards (Sentinels).
_Source: Chapter 5, «Guild Bodies of Numinia»._

## Hermeneutic Action
An action the GD proposes when the PCs encounter a Symbolic Figure: the players interpret it from their characters' perspective, with the Position as a hermeneutic lens. Its decisive feature is that «interpreting modifies what is interpreted»: the reading becomes part of the Figure's narrative nature and the GD commits to making it produce consequences.
_Source: Chapter 7, «Fragment 5: The Hermeneutic Action»._

## Heterocosmic
Lubomír Doležel's theory, «the science of fictional worlds»: every narrative opens a possible world with its own internal laws and coherence. Its dimensions are articulated through four logical operators: alethic (the possible), deontic (the permitted/forbidden), epistemic (what is known) and axiological (the valuable), which the game uses to describe what is created in the Heterocosmic Roll.
_Source: Chapter 4, «Fragment 4: Heterocosmic Roll»._

## Heterocosmic Roll
A Narrative Roll that «is triggered when a player imagines and materialises something new in the scene», under the principle «if you name it, it can exist; if it exists, it will have a price». 2D6 are rolled: the result determines the degree of success and how many characteristics the created thing will have (from 2 to 4), expressed according to Doležel's logical operators with positive or negative polarity; each roll adds 1 node to the Resonance Marker.
_Source: Chapter 4, «Heterocosmic Roll» (Fragment 4)._

## Holberins
Founder of the Sigma Research Centre and «founding father of the city of Numinia»; the nickname of the only one of the Five Initiates whose name has survived. «A visionary scientist, an alchemist of knowledge» who, foreseeing the disaster of 1920, «managed to alter the flows of time to project a residual image of the city» into the Akashic Records.
_Source: Chapter 1, «A city written between steam and code»; Chapter 2, «The Fall»._

## Imbalance
«When the Threshold reaches 0 points (temporarily), the character enters Imbalance. This is a critical state in which the character's emotional, symbolic or spiritual collapse manifests». It is «the dark side of the Position»: each Position defines its own narrative and mechanical effect for this state.
_Source: Chapter 3, «Threshold: The limit between order and collapse» and «Fragment 3: Defining the Position»._

## Imbalance of the Gearwork
A systemic reaction that occurs when the Plot Clock reaches its fourth hour: «the way in which the city readjusts its internal laws in order to go on existing». The adventure is interrupted and a self-contained mini-adventure opens in the past, the future or an alternative echo of the place; «it does not destroy the continuity of the adventure: it folds it in order to return it transformed».
_Source: Chapter 4, «Imbalance of the Gearwork»; Chapter 7, «Fragment 8: Imbalance of the Gearwork, an anomaly»._

## Instrument Roll
A roll that represents «the use of artefacts, which in the world of Numinia take the name of Relics», resolved with the D6 pool of the Competence needed to use that Relic. Only Competences associated with the PC's Guild, Faction or Species may be used, and they «depend entirely on the narrative context».
_Source: Chapter 4, «Instrument Rolls» (Fragment 3)._

## Inversion Rift
A vertical fracture of unknown depth in the Neuma Subvale, «like a wound between worlds, where the Veil seems to have lost its continuity». Through it appear objects and information of no known provenance (the Exogenous Vestiges), but no living being has ever crossed it: «The Rift transmits vestiges, not travellers».
_Source: Chapter 5, «The Inversion Rift: an interdimensional eccentricity»._

## Khepris
Entities inspired by the Egyptian god Khepri «who embody the very essence of transformation and rebirth», «guardians of lost memory and guides of souls through the veil of time». They arose from the energy field generated by Holberins's machinery buried near the tomb of Petosiris; in 2020 the Oracles found there «a surprising colony of 55 Khepris», the Primordials.
_Source: Chapter 2, «Fragment 2: Genesis of the Khepris»._

## LAP (Personal Akashic Reader)
«The Personal Akashic Reader, better known by its acronym LAP, is the everyday device for accessing the akashic network of Numinia», usually worn on the wrist: documentation, identification by emblems, augmented reality, akashic search engine, dynamic cartography and messaging. It has limits: «the LAP finds information; the PCs still have to understand it», «it does not replace investigation», and it depends on the akashic network, which is restricted in the peripheries, protected facilities and anomalies of the Veil.
_Source: Chapter 6, «Fragment 3: The LAP»._

## Legion of the Threshold
«The invisible wall that rises when Numinia trembles»: an elite force «born not for war, but to prevent it», which does not patrol the streets and is only activated when the veil of peace is torn. Each member has passed a rigorous Test of Serenity; its motto: «We are neither the light nor the shadow; we are the edge that separates them».
_Source: Chapter 5, «The Legion of the Threshold»._

## Linguistic variations
The three linguistic categories of Numinia, variations «of a common language, Numinid»: the Dialect, determined by the Species; the Sociolect, determined by the District and its Faction; and the Lingo, the jargon of the Guilds. For the PCs they play «an anecdotal role»: they are models of expression, not a mechanical requirement.
_Source: Chapter 5, «Fragment 4: Dialects and sociolects of Numinia»._

## Mixed heritage
The Species are not watertight compartments: «Hybrids are individuals who combine the genetic heritage of two or more species, manifesting hybrid characteristics» (first generation 50/50; second, two main species at 35/35 plus secondary ones). Mixed heritage «is not only genetic, but also cultural and symbolic».
_Source: Chapter 3, «The Singularity of Mixed Heritage in Numinia»._

## Movement Rolls
The general category with which actions and challenges are resolved, comprising Action Rolls and Instrument Rolls. They follow the logic of the D6 System adapted to a success system: as many D6 are rolled as the points of the corresponding aptitude, «each 6 obtained is a success» and «each 1 obtained is a failure».
_Source: Chapter 4, «Fragment 3: Movement Rolls»._

## Numinia
A city-state of knowledge imagined at the beginning of the twentieth century by Holberins and the Five Initiates «as a game: a great board where thought could move freely»; razed by fire in 1920, it rises again in the twenty-first century «as a city between planes», steampunk in its architecture of iron, glass and steam, cyberpunk in its data networks. It is «a great game board, projected onto the subtle plane through the imagination of all its citizens».
_Source: Chapter 1, «A city written between steam and code»._

## Ocean of Lúnacar
A perpetually twilit sea that completely surrounds the lands of Numinia; it is supposed to «expand infinitely into the Akasha», since no one who has tried to reach the other side has come back to tell the tale. Its iridescent reflections respond not to sunlight «but to the fluctuations of collective thought».
_Source: Chapter 5, «The Ocean of Lúnacar»._

## Offering of Glory
Once per game, if the character keeps all their initial Breath of the Veil points, they may automatically turn any failed roll into a Total Success in exchange for temporarily losing all those points. It must be described as «a moment of resonance, prophecy or transfiguration… a fleeting crack in the linearity of the world».
_Source: Chapter 3, «Breath of the Veil: The exhalation of the eternal»._

## Ontological Schools
There are no universities in Numinia: knowledge is cultivated in Ontological Schools, «where knowledge unfolds according to the great fields of human development and not according to closed disciplines». They are six open and cross-cutting lines of research (Invisible Matter, Emergent Forms, Fractal Time, Primordial Languages, Extended Symbiosis and Soul Threshold), with the motto «Where knowledge is not divided, but transformed».
_Source: Chapter 5, «The Ontological Schools of Numinia»._

## Oracles
A group of five researchers who in 2020 recognised Athanasius's formula, opened a space-time breach in Tuna el-Gebel and found the residual image of Numinia in the Akashic Records, giving rise «to the rebirth of the city». As a founding consciousness, «they do not govern, they do not dictate laws, they do not take part in the internal conflicts of the districts»: their function is to activate, inspire and preserve the foundations of the city.
_Source: Chapter 2, «The Rebirth»; Chapter 5, «The Oracles, Architects of Balance»._

## PC and NPC
The manual uses the abbreviations PC (and PCs) for the characters the players play and NPC (and NPCs) for the characters of the world, without formally spelling them out. The roles are described in Chapter 1: each player «plays a character ‒an inhabitant of this fantastic city under reconstruction‒ and makes them advance, speak, decide and act as if they were that character», while the GD «embodies the characters of the world».
_Source: Chapter 1, «How Numinia is played»._

## PC States
The system that replaces hit points: «the existence of PCs is not measured in hit points or energy points»; the States «represent, then, the repercussions of badly resolved conflicts». The table includes Confused, Destabilised, Unanchored, Desynchronised, Fragmented, Exhausted, Contaminated, Collapsed and Lethargic; the undamaged state is Unscathed and the final dissolution, Dissolved.
_Source: Chapter 4, «Fragment 6: PC States»._

## Phase structure
The organisation of each adventure into «states of the narration, not chapters of a previously written plot», which «are not watertight compartments: they can overlap, shorten or expand according to the needs of the story». The phases are Presentation, Deliberation, Investigation, Reflection, Action and Resolution; in each one the purpose, the pace and the distribution of initiative between the GD and the players change.
_Source: Chapter 4, «Fragment 2: Phase structure»; Chapter 7, «Fragment 6: Managing the Phases»._

## Pieces of Conflict
«Significant elements that appear during the adventure and that bear some potential relationship to the Starting Conflict»: objects, documents, testimonies or symbols that the PCs relate to one another in order to formulate interpretations. Unlike the traditional clue, a Piece «has relevance without yet having a closed meaning»; at least three are needed to leave the Investigation Phase, and each one is equivalent to 1D6 in the final Prism Roll.
_Source: Chapter 7, «Fragment 3: The Pieces of Conflict»; Chapter 4, «Investigation Phase»._

## Plot Clock
A collective counter that «marks the pulse of the narrative world»: each Event Roll advances it by one hour, in a cycle of four hours. On reaching the fourth hour the Imbalance of the Gearwork is triggered and, when it closes, the Veil «scars over» and the clock resets from 0.
_Source: Chapter 4, «Event Roll» and «Imbalance of the Gearwork»._

## Position
«The Position is the stable and generic function that a character assumes in Numinia. It is not an ephemeral role or a circumstantial task, but a place within the system». There are fifteen (Guardian of the Gates, Pythia, Ambassador, Game Master, Legionary of the Threshold, Armonaut, Whisperer of Machines, Runner of the Veil, Archivist, Hermeneut, Mediator of the Prism, Cartographer of the Wind, Oneiromancer, Anacharchid and Ethnarch); each one grants +1 to a Characteristic, six special aptitudes (two are chosen), the initial Threshold and Initiative points and its own Imbalance effect.
_Source: Chapter 3, «Fragment 3: Defining the Position»._

## Potential proposals
NPCs, places, events, objects or relationships prepared by the GD with a possible connection to the conflict, which «do not constitute facts of the fiction until they appear during play»: materials to introduce, transform or discard. The principle is «prepare possibilities, not obligations».
_Source: Chapter 7, «Founding facts and potential proposals»._

## Prestige Points
«Prestige represents the degree to which a character is integrated into the cultural fabric of Numinia»: a form of cultural experience, since progress depends «fundamentally on what they know and what they understand». It is increased by consuming Seeds of Knowledge according to their value and, «once acquired, Prestige is never lost».
_Source: Chapter 3, «Prestige and living culture»; Chapter 6, «Tokens»._

## Prism
«The Prism is neither an object nor a doctrine: it is the living law of refraction». Where the Veil keeps and the Threshold manifests, the Prism unfolds: «It does not create truths: it multiplies perspectives», breaking each scene down into possible trajectories and allowing each guild and faction to project its own angle on the world.
_Source: Chapter 2, «The force of the Prism»._

## Prism Cells
«Living nodes, both physical and spiritual, in which the light of the Prism condenses to interact with the Factions»; there are four groups, one per Faction, and a PC may only use those of their own. As a single-use token they serve three purposes: removing States, raising the grade of a Roll or paying at the Black Market.
_Source: Chapter 2, «The Prism Cells»; Chapter 6, «Tokens»._

## Prism Roll
The Resolution Phase roll with which the PCs undo each knot or Operative Point of the adventure's ending: its pool is formed from the Pieces of Conflict found (each Piece is equivalent to 1D6). By convention there are four Operative Points, which «should correspond to each of the four logical operators» of the Heterocosmic; failure does not prevent resolution, it «will only make things more difficult».
_Source: Chapter 4, «Resolution Phase» (Fragment 2)._

## Proscribed
«The Proscribed, by contrast, are experimental technologies. Some were prototypes; others come from secret or forbidden research; a few perhaps worked too well». Their circulation is restricted because of their «eccentric, unpredictable or hard-to-integrate» properties with regard to «the technological norms of the city».
_Source: Chapter 6, «Rarities and Anomalies» and «Proscribed»._

## Rarities and Anomalies
An Inventory category grouping objects from past technological layers that «were not created to become Relics»: displaced machines, instruments of vanished systems and prototypes never incorporated into ordinary life. «All of them are grouped into two great categories: Residues and Proscribed», which count as Relics for Inventory purposes.
_Source: Chapter 6, «Rarities and Anomalies»._

## Relic
Relics «are not tools. They are fossilised echoes of symbolic time»: each one is «an encapsulated form of decision», and they «are not used: they are invoked. They are not activated: they are interpreted». Each Relic has a Property linked to a Competence (whose dice pool is used in the Instrument Roll) and a Risk Factor; no more than three can be carried at once.
_Source: Chapter 6, «Relics» (Fragment 2: The Inventory)._

## Residues
«Residues are old technologies that have fallen out of use», belonging to earlier stages of Numinia's technical development, with the materiality of valves, lenses, coils and gears. «They are not necessarily inferior to current technologies»: a Residue is «the survival of a displaced technology».
_Source: Chapter 6, «Rarities and Anomalies» and «Residues»._

## Resonance Marker
A collective counter that regulates creation through Heterocosmic Rolls: each roll adds 1 node to the Marker and, «on reaching 4, it triggers an Event Roll» and resets to 0. It exists because «stirring the gearwork of Numinia too much can cause serious disturbances».
_Source: Chapter 4, «Heterocosmic Roll»._

## Root
The fundamental proposition on which the module is built: it establishes what is happening when the adventure begins, whom it affects, what tension it introduces and why the PCs intervene; «it does not constitute a summary of the plot nor does it contain a secret explanation of the events». It must be «firm in what it presents and extraordinarily flexible in what it means».
_Source: Chapter 7, «Fragment 1: Creating the Root»._

## Rumourology
«The set of stories, fragmentary memories, testimonies, hypotheses, superstitions and symbolic associations through which the inhabitants of Numinia try to explain what they know only incompletely»: the city's informal memory. It is investigated with an Inquire Roll on elements that have been «culturised»; rumours are not necessarily false, and «Rumourology does not replace the Pieces: it surrounds them with culture».
_Source: Chapter 7, «Fragment 4: Rumourology»._

## Seeds of Knowledge
«Physical manifestations of the bond that is established between a character and Numinia through discovery, investigation and creation»: luminescent geodesic pods in five classes (from 1 to 5 Prestige points) that the GD grants «when they consider that a true act of knowledge has taken place». «Numinia rewards curiosity exercised, not information passively received».
_Source: Chapter 6, «Seeds of Knowledge» (Tokens); Chapter 3, «Prestige and living culture»._

## Session Zero
The first step of the game: «Before the portals of the game open, the players meet with the Game Director (GD) to build the soul of the game together». It serves to create the characters, discover their stories, understand their relationship with the world and «weave the first threads of the shared story they will live».
_Source: Chapter 1, «How Numinia is played»._

## Space of Indeterminacy
The part of the fiction that the module and the GD deliberately leave unspecified so that it can be completed during play. It is not an arbitrary void: the question that opens it already contains a proposal with «fertile limits», which determines enough to stimulate the imagination and leaves enough undetermined for the contribution to belong to the player.
_Source: Chapter 7, «The Space of Indeterminacy»._

## Species
«A character's Species is not a simple biological characteristic, but an essential configuration of their identity»: each one represents a force field, a mode of thought and a purpose. They are five lineages that crystallised at the end of the Dark Age around five forces: Biomechanicals, «The Masters of Progress» (Technology); Humanitas, «The Custodians of Meaning» (Culture); Reptilians, «The Guardians of Tradition» (Nature); Cyanites, «The Architects of Social Order» (Knowledge); and Spectrals, «The Visionaries of the Beyond» (Aether).
_Source: Chapter 3, «Fragment 2: Determining the Species»; Chapter 2, «The Birth of the Five Species»._

## Summa Archive
A scientific and exploratory institution —«The Archive»— founded by decree of the Concordia Council with the backing of the Oracles, with an itinerant seat. Its function is the systematic study of the Peripheral Zones: «it seeks to know what lies beyond, but also to contain and understand whatever may represent a threat or a revelation for the city». It houses the Aleph Section, a restricted division that investigates uncanny phenomena.
_Source: Chapter 5, «The Summa Archive»._

## Symbolic Figure
An element with «an evident symbolic charge and a still undetermined meaning»: an object, image, event, person or phrase «singular enough to provoke the impression that its immediate appearance does not exhaust what it means». «The Figure does not hide a correct interpretation. It is a structure waiting to be interpreted» through the Hermeneutic Action.
_Source: Chapter 7, «Fragment 5: The Hermeneutic Action» and «When to introduce a Hermeneutic Figure»._

## Three great forces
«Three great forces act as the engine of Numinia, shaping its dynamics and its morphology: the Veil, the Threshold and the Prism». They live «both fused and opposed»: the supernatural condition of the Veil, the physical materialisation of the Threshold and the refractive power of the Prism, which permeates them.
_Source: Chapter 2, «Fragment 5: The three great forces of Numinia»._

## Threshold
As a cosmic force, «the Threshold is the force of Numinia that acts as the antagonist of the Veil»: the materialisation of physical energies; «it does not separate two worlds: it is the exact point where the world takes on a body». As a character attribute, it «represents the character's psycho-emotional state and their capacity to remain balanced»: a D6 pool that grants bonuses to rolls of physical Characteristics and whose exhaustion triggers Imbalance.
_Source: Chapter 2, «The frontiers of the Threshold»; Chapter 3, «Threshold: The limit between order and collapse»._

## Tokens
«Tokens are primotechnic objects that materialise aspects of the relationship between an individual and Numinia»; «They represent a bond, and may represent wealth as well: what a Token is worth never erases where it came from», and obtaining one is always a form of recognition from the city. There are two great classes: the Seeds of Knowledge (they respond to knowledge) and the Prism Cells (they respond to factional affinity).
_Source: Chapter 6, «Tokens» (Fragment 2: The Inventory)._

## Veil
«The Veil is not a place. It is not, exactly, a dimension or a border. It is the translucent membrane between the visible and the unmanifested»: the conscious echo of the world, a spiritual and symbolic field where decisions not taken, memories not lived and destinies yet to be revealed resound. It is mythically born as «the living skin of the maladjustment» after the Chronothecaries' lunar adjustment.
_Source: Chapter 2, «The Breath of the Veil» (Fragment 5)._

## Virelai River
The only great river that crosses Numinia from end to end: it rises in the Resonant Springs of Heliconte, traces an irregular spiral through the districts and flows into the Ocean of Lúnacar through the Doxa Delta. Its name comes from an ancient poetic form: «the Virelai flows like a refrain, repeating and rewriting its course through history».
_Source: Chapter 5, «The Virelai River»._

## Weapons
In Numinia a weapon «is not a simple tool for causing harm»: it lacks attack or damage values and instead broadens the ways in which a character can intervene in the confrontation (it is defined by Description, Intervention and Effect). Because of its «agitating power» over the Veil, a PC may only carry one weapon.
_Source: Chapter 6, «Weapons» (Fragment 2: The Inventory)._

## Terms without a source in the manual

None: all the terms in the brief could be documented in the text of the manual v0.6.0. Two notes on fidelity: (1) the abbreviations PJ and PNJ (PC and NPC) are used throughout the manual without being explicitly spelled out, so their entry relies on the description of roles in Chapter 1; (2) the dominant and only spelling in the manual is «akáshico/akáshica» (with h) — the variant «akásico» does not appear —, and the full name of the LAP is «Lector Akáshico Personal» (Personal Akashic Reader).

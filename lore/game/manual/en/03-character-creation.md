# **CHAPTER 3**

# **CHARACTER CREATION**
**The first breath**

« _Holberins swept his gaze across the immense board that stretched out before him. The city did not yet have bodies, but it did have structures. Golden lines intertwined across the surface, shaping the streets of Numinia, the great buildings of the Agora, the secret passages of the Ouroboros. Everything was in place, and yet the city remained empty._

_‒It is not enough to raise the board ‒murmured the first Initiate‒. If Numinia is to transcend, it needs inhabitants. Not mere names in registers, but souls, consciousness, purpose._

_One by one, the five leaned over the table and, with the same care with which a master jeweller cuts a gem, began to design the first beings of Numinia. Every brushstroke of their creation not only outlined a form, but bestowed function and destiny: the Exegetes, guardians of knowledge; the Alchemists, artisans of the future; the Procurators, forgers of order; the Sentinels, keepers of harmony. Four pillars, holding up the great structure._

_Holberins raised his quill and let it float above the board. The ink slid across without touching the surface, as if the city itself were absorbing it. Then it happened. From the margins of the simulation, where the abstract merges with the tangible, silhouettes began to emerge. They were not mere data. They breathed. They looked. They called one another by names no one had given them._

_But eternity is a fragile promise. When the shadows of those who craved control seeped into the warp of Numinia, the city trembled with an echo of betrayal. The names began to fade. The silhouettes came undone in the blink of an eye. And the great work, the city that was meant to be eternal, was swallowed by fire and mist._

_Even so, the board was not entirely emptied. In the vestiges of the simulation, among the folds of cosmic memory, the echoes of those first inhabitants remained imprinted. And a century later, when the Oracles opened the breach in the Akashic Archives, they found something more than ruins: they found the possibility of giving new breath to the fallen._

_Numinia was awakening once more. And with it, its inhabitants were ready to be reborn_.»

Before you plunge into the intricate streets of Numinia, you must give shape to the one who will walk them. You are not a mere spectator: you are a being woven into the very warp of the game. Destiny awaits you but, before you defy it, you must exist.

## **Fragment 1: Name, Guild, Faction and District**

Creating a character in Numinia is not just a matter of numbers and statistics. It is the act of breathing life into an idea, of endowing with purpose an entity that will move among the folds of this reborn city. Here, every being has an identity forged in the history of a world that unfolds between the digital and the esoteric.

Before setting out on the adventure, you will have to define three fundamental aspects that will lay the foundations of your character's existence:

  - **A name**, their personal seal in this universe.

  - **A guild**, which will determine their knowledge and abilities.

  - **A faction**, which will define their ideology and circle of influence, and their district.

### **The Name: The Echo of the Soul**

A name in Numinia is more than an identifier. It is a resonance within the great board of the game, a mark in the memory of the simulation. You may choose any name, but bear in mind that the city abounds in echoes of ancient civilisations and references to hidden erudition.

Some names evoke the grandeur of Antiquity: Themistocles, Hypatia, Senenmut, Nimrod. Others are taken from the stars and celestial bodies: Altair, Bellatrix, Rigel, Io. You will also find names inspired by esoteric and alchemical traditions: Athanasius, Paracelsus, Thule, Sefira.

Choose a name that speaks for your character, that embodies their essence. In Numinia, what is named, exists.

What follows is a quick guide to creating names in Numinia. But first, two important observations must be made:

  - This guide points to the PC's species as the basis for creating their name; it would therefore be advisable for the player to study the section on species before choosing a name.

  - The notes given in this guide are meant as an aid for the player, never an imposition. And as a mere aid they should be taken. Ultimately, the players' choices must be entirely free; that said, they should try to respect the identity and characteristics of the game as far as possible.

Now, then, let us see how names work linguistically in Numinia:

**1. BASIC STRUCTURE**

All names follow this form:

Name + Surname

  - Name → identity (fluid, easy)

  - Surname → meaning, cultural weight

**2. PHONETIC PATTERN (COMMON BASE)**

For a name to work well:

**Length**

  - Name: 2–3 syllables

  - Surname: 2–4 syllables

**Rhythm**

  - Alternate consonant + vowel
→ _Se-ve-rin_, _Ta-li-a_, _Va-rro_

**Vowels**

  - Prioritise: a, e, i, o

  - Avoid difficult clusters ( _khrst_, _tzk_, etc.)

**3. CHOOSE YOUR STYLE (BY SPECIES)**

**Humanites (Thought, Idealism)**

<u>Base</u>

Mediterranean cultures: Greek, Latin, softened Egyptian

<u>Philological clues</u>

  - Endings: -ia, -os, -is, -on, -us

  - Soft sounds: l, m, n, r, s

  - Words with an abstract or mythical air

Examples

  - Thalia Mnemósine

  - Cassia Nefris

  - Aurelian Therion

**Cyanites (Culture, Society)**

<u>Base</u>

Dominant Latin + Romanised Greek

<u>Philological clues</u>

  - Endings: -or, -us, -a, -in

  - Concept-type surnames: law, norm, system

  - Less lyricism, more precision

Examples

  - Severin Logothenos

  - Ibera Lux

  - Livia Thesion

**Reptilians (Instinct, ritual)**

<u>Base</u>

Semitic languages (Arabic, Hebrew)

<u>Philological clues</u>

  - Strong sounds: k, h, z, r

  - Tense structures: _Kha-rim_, _Za-leth_

  - Endings: -im, -eth, -ar

<u>Examples</u>

  - Selkar Imorath

  - Iram Khazar

  - Azel Baruk

**Biomechanicals** **(Technology, Engineering)**

<u>Base</u>

Germanic languages

<u>Philological clues</u>

  - Sounds: k, t, g, d, r

  - Clusters: dr, kr, st, rk

  - Endings: -rik, -ter, -vald, -mar

Examples

  - Daren Volrik

  - Lorkin Servald

  - Armin Valter

**Spectrals** **(Mysticism, Intuition)**

<u>Base</u>

Saxon + softened Scandinavian

<u>Philological clues</u>

  - Long vowels: ae, ei, y

  - Soft sounds: v, th, l, n

  - Endings: -wyn, -ael, -eth, -ir

<u>Examples</u>

  - Seren Vael

  - Elian Aelirioneth

  - Loriel Vaeliren

**Hybrids** **(Mixed heritage)**

You can mix roots. Key rule: one language dominates, the other adds nuance.

These are the most effective names and the most characteristic of Numinia.

<u>Examples</u>

  - Arithrea Khebron _(Greek + Egyptian + Semitic)_

  - Varro Khalis _(Latin + Arabic/Semitic)_

  - Drusa Malter _(Latin + Germanic)_

  - Elya Tharon _(Norse + Greek)_

  - Nalia Vor _(Latin/Romance + Germanic)_

### **The Guild: The Character's Calling**

« _The four elements, the four seasons, the four ages of life, but above all the four cardinal points supply order and fixity to the world. The quadriga thus becomes a symbol of the universe._ »

—Juan Eduardo Cirlot

Since its origin, Numinia has been governed by the tetrad, an essential scheme that structures its cosmos. Four are the factions that outline its thought, four the guilds that sustain its workings, four the districts that shape its geography. This model is no accident: it is a profound representation of balance, a reflection of the need for stability in a universe that unfolds between knowledge and mystery, between play and order.

Just as the four cardinal points give the traveller his bearings, the four guilds of Numinia define the path of its inhabitants. Each one represents a way of approaching reality, a vocation that shapes the character's existence within the city. Through the guild, a citizen acquires not only skills, but also a purpose.

**The Four Guilds of Numinia**

The guilds represent the vocation of each citizen of Numinia. They are their path within the city, their area of specialisation and the core of their skills.

The structure of the Guilds follows a vertical hierarchy. There are four great Guilds in Numinia. Each one contains two Branches, and each Branch contains two Houses, following the semantic model of Basic Level Theory. According to this theory, developed by Eleanor Rosch within her own Prototype Theory, elements are arranged in hierarchical relations of hypernymy and hyponymy, among which the middle term is the most representative of each category:

**GUILD**

- **Branch A**

`o` **House A.1**

`o` **House A.2**

- **Branch B**

`o` **House B.1**

`o` **House B.2**

So, according to Basic Level Theory, the fundamental level here is represented by the Branch, since the Guild is too generic (superordinate level) and the House is too specific (subordinate level).

This design reflects the progression of knowledge within each discipline, organising learning into levels that run from the broad to the specialised.

Choose wisely: the guild will determine not only your character's skills, but their place in the great story of Numinia:

******

**Alchemists**

Creative, scientific and artistic minds. The Alchemists combine knowledge and imagination, research and experimentation, with a touch of fantasy. They are the inventors, the discoverers, the innovators.

**Structure of the Alchemists**

- Branch A: Handcrafters

`o` House A.1: Draftsmen

`o` House A.2: Aesthetes

- Branch B: Engineers

`o` House B.1: Architects

`o` House B.2: Automatons

The members of the Branch of the Handcrafters are the creative spirits of the city. Within this group, designers on both small and large scales belong to the House of the Draftsmen, while artists of every kind are the ones who make up the House of the Aesthetes.

The members of the Branch of the Engineers work with code and chains of information. Within this group, those who develop the digital architecture of Numinia belong to the House of the Architects, while those who work with virtual realities and artificial consciousnesses form part of the House of the Automatons.

**Traits:** Creator, Inventor, Artist, Developer

**Figure:** Transformer

**Paradigm:** Imagination

**Representation:** The Sorcerer

******

**Exegetes**

Those who live by letters, history and fantasy. Hoarders of knowledge, collectors of legends, makers of dreams. They are the protectors of culture, the chroniclers of the ages and the visionaries of what could be.

**Structure of the Exegetes**

- Branch A: Chroniclers

  - House A.1: Logographers

  - House A.2: Bards

- Branch B: Scholars

  - House B.1: Hierophants

  - House B.2: Thaumaturges

The members of the Branch of the Chroniclers document the events of the city. Within this group, those who work from a diachronic perspective (histories and legends) belong to the House of the Logographers, while those who work from a synchronic perspective (news and current chronicles) form part of the House of the Bards.

The members of the Branch of the Scholars are students of and experts in diverse disciplines. Within this group, the specialists in different fields belong to the House of the Hierophants, while those who shape culture and develop new ideas form part of the House of the Thaumaturges.

**Traits:** Thinker, Polymath, Historian, Communicator

**Figure:** Narrator

**Paradigm:** Theoretical

**Representation:** The Hermit

******

**Procurators**

The pragmatic minds of Numinia. Specialists in organisation, management and administration. They know social procedures and legal resources. They think structurally, act with judgement and solve problems efficiently.

**Structure of the Procurators**

- Branch A: Legates

  - House A.1: Legal Rabbits

  - House A.2: Heralds

- Branch B: Trustees

  - House B.1: Mercurials

  - House B.2: Stewards

The members of the Branch of the Legates operate within the legal and diplomatic framework. Within this group, those who provide legal counsel and practise the law belong to the House of the Legal Rabbits, while those who represent the city in legal or diplomatic matters form part of the House of the Heralds.

The members of the Branch of the Trustees are chosen to protect the interests of Numinia in the economic and social spheres. Within this group, those involved with the economy belong to the House of the Mercurials, while those who manage internal organisational affairs form part of the House of the Stewards.

**Traits:** Administrator, Legislator, Economist, Manager

**Figure:** Organiser

**Paradigm:** Pragmatic

**Representation:** The Emperor

******

**Sentinels**

The Sentinels are responsible for ensuring that every corner of Numinia runs smoothly, preventing altercations, providing wellbeing and preserving the values of the city. They are moderators, custodians and peacemakers.

**Structure of the Sentinels**

- Branch A: Seraphs

  - House A.1: Captains

  - House A.2: Guardians

- Branch B: Archangels

  - House B.1: Healers

  - House B.2: Explorers

The members of the Branch of the Seraphs keep order. Within this group, those responsible for overseeing and managing the structure of Numinia belong to the House of the Captains, while those who ensure compliance with the rules and handle community conflicts form part of the House of the Guardians.

The members of the Branch of the Archangels work with the community, solving personal problems and dilemmas while offering assistance. Within this group, those who provide mental care and promote wellbeing belong to the House of the Healers, while those who help guide citizens along the right path form part of the House of the Explorers.

**Traits:** Administrator, Moderator, Guide, Watcher

**Figure:** Caregiver

**Paradigm:** Experience

**Representation:** The Guardian Angel

You must, then, choose a Guild, a Branch and a House.

### **The Faction: The Spirit of the City**

If the guild defines a character's vocation, the faction represents their vision of the world. It is the force that guides their decisions, the community with which they share ideals and the sphere in which they develop their influence within Numinia. Each of the four great factions not only represents a field of development within the city, but also dictates the philosophy of its members, their way of interpreting knowledge and their approach within the great game of existence.

Just as the four guilds uphold the structure of Numinia, the four factions give it life, purpose and direction. Each is bound to a district of the city and to a fundamental domain of thought and action.

Each faction embodies a different way of understanding Numinia and its purpose. Its members gather by ideological affinity rather than by specific trade, and within them one may find individuals from any guild, so long as they share the same fundamental principles.

By their nature, each faction has a specific identity and specific interests. Thus, each has its own goals and its own wealth of knowledge. The spirit of collaboration and the meeting between factions are essential both for the integrity of each of the fields of development and for the growth of the community of citizens as a whole.

The structure of the factions follows the semantic model of Prototype Theory, in a horizontal arrangement of co-hyponymy. That is, the four factions are co-hyponyms within a main group (under the name of Factions), which acts as the hypernym.

**The Four Factions of Numinia**

As with the Guilds, the Factions are organised in a tetrad, in which each of the elements represents a field of development and a source of knowledge.

******

**Hermeticists**

_«Understand the past, master the future.»_

The Hermeticists are the guardians of wisdom and erudition. Their mission is to study, preserve and expand knowledge in all its forms, from metaphysics to advanced technology. They see Numinia as a great living archive where every citizen is at once an apprentice and a master. They believe in the rigour of study, in the transmission of learning and in the tireless search for truth.

This faction takes as the starting point of its occultist interests the Hermetic teachings of Hermes Trismegistus, and especially the seven principles set out in _The Kybalion_. Of all the factions, it is the one most closely bound to esoteric principles; its members consider themselves guardians of knowledge, access to which must be unravelled by the initiate through study and the constant practice of the seven principles.

**Seed name:** Hermetic Society of the Seven Principles

**Field of development:** Education

**Principles:** Reason, discipline, learning

**Sphere of influence:** Education, philosophy, epistemology

**Symbol:** A circle superimposed on a triangle and, above them, the eye of the Thrice Great, whose iris is formed by seven striations representing the seven principles.

**Motto:** _The lips of wisdom are closed, except to the ears of Understanding._

******

**Heirs of Eleusis**

_«Play is our first narrative, our first lesson and our first creative impulse.»_

The Heirs of Eleusis hold that learning and progress arise from playful experimentation. They are explorers of mystery, designers of riddles and tellers of stories in which every citizen becomes the protagonist. For them, play is the supreme form of knowledge and transformation. They believe in applied creativity, in the thrill of the challenge and in the importance of turning life into an adventure.

It is a semi-religious and spiritualist group, which takes the precepts of the forerunner of this line of thought, Allan Kardec, and applies them to classical mystical disciplines, such as the Orphic rituals and, above all, the Eleusinian cults. From the latter they adopt both the name and the nature of their rites, their mystical character and their vision of resurrection from the world of the dead. From Orphism they take the conception of _soma_ and _sema_, the body as the prison of the soul.

For the members of the Mystic Order of the New Eleusinian Cults, the highest knowledge is found in contemplative and mystical experience, in the personal transformation brought about by the mystery rite of trance.

**Seed name:** Mystic Order of the New Eleusinian Cults

**Field of development:** Narrative projection

**Principles:** Play, curiosity, discovery

**Sphere of influence:** Gamification, challenge design, interactive storytelling

**Symbol:** A serpent coiled around several circles, intertwined with a sprig of opium poppies, representing narcosis and the healing of the soul.

**Motto:** _You are life and death to suffering mortals, for you nourish all and you annihilate all._

******

**Stellar Circle**

_«Without structure, there is no order. Without order, there is no city.»_

The Stellar Circle is formed by those who believe in the need for a solid framework to uphold Numinia. They are the architects of order, the legislators of the community, the designers of functional and efficient systems. For them, the city is a living organism that must be administered with logic and precision. They believe in stability, in planning and in the importance of rules to guarantee harmony.

This faction centres on cosmic events, with a philoscientific character that takes concepts from quantum physics, such as multiverses or brane cosmology, and fuses them with esoteric beliefs about otherworldly entities, different planes of existence and astrological derivations.

Its name comes from the Venus Table, a text from the seventh century BC that reproduces another Babylonian writing from around 1700 BC, in which extraordinary astronomical observations are recorded.

**Seed name:** Stellar Circle for the Study of the Venus Table

**Field of development:** Organisation

**Principles:** Organisation, strategy, governance

**Sphere of influence:** Legislation, economy, structures of power

**Symbol:** An upward-pointing pentacle, around which float the keys of Saint Peter, the seal of Solomon and the symbol of Venus, representations of cosmic wisdom.

**Motto:** _The visible is nothing but the manifestation of the invisible, just as reason is the manifestation of magic._

******

**Neo-Atlantists**

_«Creation is memory dreaming the future.»_

The Neo-Atlantists see creativity as the engine of Numinia. They are artists, designers, visionaries who transform reality through expression. For them, the city is a canvas on which every citizen leaves their mark. They believe neither in the rigidity of order nor in the dogmatism of pure knowledge: their truth lies in aesthetics, in emotion and in the capacity to imagine the impossible.

This faction takes as the starting point for its studies the genesis of our world according to the occultist historians, with special emphasis on prehistory and the evolution of the mythical civilisations. Its main source is the Akashic Records, and it therefore centres its interest on the evolution of the legendary continents and cities, such as Atlantis, Lemuria and Agartha, as well as on the succession of the different root races.

According to their beliefs, the sixth root race will be the next step in the evolution of the _res cogitans_, the thinking being, and their duty is to anticipate that transformation and give it shape through art.

**Seed name:** International Confederation of the Sixth Root Race

**Field of development:** Art

**Principles:** Inspiration, innovation, expression.

**Sphere of influence:** Art, aesthetics, rhetoric.

**Symbol:** Six concentric circles, over which a rhombus is superimposed, from whose angles and sides emanate lines representing the rays of the rising sun.

**Motto:** _The spirit that in this world is allegory will animate a new body in the world in which it is divinity._

******

The faction you choose will shape your character's perspective within Numinia. It will determine not only their surroundings and their allies, but also their way of interpreting the purpose of the city. Will you seek enlightenment in the wisdom of the Hermeticists? Or will you prefer to immerse yourself in the infinite game of the Heirs of Eleusis? Will you build the foundations of order with the Stellar Circle, or will you surrender to the boundless creativity of the Neo-Atlantists?

And it likewise determines the district in which you live for, as can be seen, each faction is bound to a district (more information on the districts in Chapter 5: _Geography and Culture of Numinia_).

Whatever your decision, remember: the faction is not just a group you belong to, but a philosophy that defines you.

### **The Singularity of Mixed Heritage in Numinia**

In Numinia, as in every living civilisation, the Species are not watertight compartments. There is a common phenomenon: mixed heritage.

Those of mixed heritage are individuals who combine the genetic inheritance of two or more species, manifesting hybrid traits that make them unique entities within the city's ecosystem.

**Structure of mixed heritage:**

  - **First generation:** The individual is an equal mix of two main species (50/50).

  - **Second generation:** 70% of the inheritance is kept, divided between two main species (35/35), and 30% comes from one or two secondary species (if there are two, they are divided 15/15).

This mixed heritage is not only genetic, but also cultural and symbolic. Those of Mixed Heritage can adopt traits of thought and abilities from their different lineages, allowing them to navigate between the different force fields of Numinia.

### **Species Selection System**

The player may freely choose their species, as well as their condition: pure species or mixed heritage. But if they choose the latter, they must roll 1D6 to determine their class:

**1-2.** Mixed heritage with two first-generation Species.

**3-4.** Mixed heritage with two first-generation Species and one second-generation Species.

**5-6.** Mixed heritage with two first-generation Species and two second-generation Species.

Even when of Mixed Heritage, the player may choose one of the first-generation Species, but must roll 1D6 to define each of the others (whether they have only one other first-generation Species or, in addition, one or two second-generation Species):

**1.** Biomechanical

**2.** Humanite

**3.** Reptilian

**4.** Cyanite

**5.** Spectral

**6.** Player's choice

If the result is a repeated Species, the roll must be made again until a unique combination is obtained. If a 6 is obtained on any of these rolls, the player may choose the Species they were trying to determine (for example, when defining the first second-generation race).

Mixed heritage, therefore, is defined by one of these combinations:

  - Two first-generation Species with the same influence (50/50).

  - Two first-generation Species with 70% influence (35/35) and one second-generation Species with 30%.

  - Two first-generation Species with 70% influence (35/35) and two second-generation Species with 30% in total (15/15 each).

« _You are not a formless entity, nor a traveller without origin. Your essence has been sculpted by the flow of history, by the forces that uphold Numinia, by the imprint of what you were and of what you may yet be_.»

The choice of Species in Numinia is neither a mere aesthetic detail nor a simple statistic: it is the manifestation of a deep nature. It defines the way in which the character perceives the world, their connection with the force fields that uphold the city and the competences in which they will be able to excel.

## **Fragment 3: Defining the Position**

« _In Numinia, the Position is not merely a title, but a reflection of the balance between identity, skills and nature. It is not chosen at random; it is discovered in the interweaving of attributes that make up the individual._ »

The Position is the stable, generic function that a character assumes in Numinia. It is not an ephemeral role or a circumstantial task, but a place within the system, aligned with the character's identity, capabilities and psychological traits.

Each Position has certain skills that will grant an additional point in some Characteristic. At the same time, the Position has a series of special aptitudes, from which the player will freely choose two. The dice pool for these two special aptitudes will be derived from the bonuses the player obtains depending on the compatibility between the Position and other attributes.

The Position is thus related to the Guild, the Faction, the Species and the Archetype on a scale of compatibilities. According to this scale, the Position will receive a number of points, which may be distributed between its two special aptitudes as desired. Those points translate into D6. However, bear in mind that the PC cannot hold a Position if they belong to a Guild, a Faction, a Species or an Archetype marked as incompatible with that Position.

The **preferred** Attribute grants 2 points.

The **compatible** Attribute grants 1 point.

The **neutral** Attribute grants 0 points.

The **incompatible** Attribute invalidates the Position.

Finally, the Imbalance represents the dark side of the Position, which grows as the PC makes extreme efforts. Thus, each time one of the Special Aptitudes is used, whether or not the roll succeeds, the PC will temporarily lose 1 point of Threshold, which represents their communion and harmony with the world. When the Threshold points reach 0, Imbalance is triggered, which has consequences for actions and rolls. However, some six hours of rest restore 1 point of Threshold. 1 point may be recovered each day, until the full number of starting points has been restored. Each Position has its own starting Threshold points.

Bearing in mind that any Species and Archetypes not listed are considered neutral, the Positions that exist in Numinia are described below:

### 1. Gatekeeper – Knower of all paths

The Gatekeeper of Numinia is a position laden with symbolism and responsibility, since it protects access and regulates who enters and under what conditions.

**<u>Profile</u>**

  - **Knowledge:** Security, mysticism, rituals of protection.

  - **Skills:** High perception, physical and mental endurance, and defensive capabilities on both the physical and the etheric plane. **+1 to Constitution**

**<u>Duties</u>**

The Gatekeeper not only protects the physical entrance to Numinia, but is also the first obstacle for those who seek to enter without understanding the city's values. Its profile, specialised in security and rituals, makes for a powerful and symbolic figure.

**<u>Special aptitudes</u>**

The special aptitudes of the Gatekeeper are:

1.- **Sealed Threshold**
You can perform a ritual to close a physical or energetic passage with a seal for the length of a scene. If you pass the roll, no one can cross it without breaking the seal.
2.- **Etheric Wall**
You can summon a temporary barrier of energy (5 minutes) that protects an area or a small group. It reduces by 50% the damage of attacks directed against what it protects.
3.- **Path of the Threshold**
At a crossroads, you identify the right or safest path, guided by the symbolic resonances of the space. You also perceive what danger lurks on the other paths.
4.- **Spirit Key**
With a gesture, you can generate a symbolic “key” capable of opening locked doors (physical, digital or arcane). It does not break the lock; rather, it persuades it to open.
5.- **Liminal Leap**
Once per scene, you can teleport to another place within a radius of 100 metres of your position. The leap, however, leaves you exhausted for 10 minutes.
6.- **Guardian Vestige**
When you remain for more than one scene in a sacred or protected place, you can leave an etheric double there in your absence. This vestige neither acts nor attacks, but it deters intruders.

**<u>Threshold and Imbalance</u>**

It has 4 starting points of Threshold and 2 of Initiative. When it enters Imbalance, it loses its bearings; in its mind all the routes and paths blur together, like a labyrinth. In its rolls, each result of 1 will cancel out a success.

## **Fragment 4: Characteristics and Competences**

_«The body is the vehicle of the spirit, and the mind its architect. In Numinia, every being is composed of physical and psychic attributes that determine its capacity for action, its perception of its surroundings and its influence over reality»._

In this step, two essential aspects of the character are defined: the level of their Characteristics and their Competences.

Characteristics are the fundamental traits of the character. They are divided into two groups, physical and psychic, and they determine the character's capacity to perform actions in the game.

Competences are specific abilities that the character has developed through their Species, guild and faction, and they encompass both abilities for which the character has a natural predisposition and areas of knowledge. These Competences are used exclusively to handle relics, weapons and other artefacts, which will make up the most relevant narrative instruments for the PCs. For while it is true that players have many resources within the game system with which to create narrative, relics, weapons and artefacts form a set of tangible elements open enough to be interpreted and brought into play through the imagination.

### **The Character's Characteristics**

Each character possesses eight main characteristics, organised into two categories:

**Physical Characteristics:**

  - **Strength**   - Determines the character's physical power

  - **Movement**   - Represents their agility and speed

  - **Size**   - Defines their physical dimensions and their impact on combat or mobility

  - **Constitution**   - Reflects their endurance and health

**Psychic Characteristics:**

  - **Intelligence**   - Their cognitive capacity and problem-solving

  - **Wisdom**   - Their combination of knowledge and culture, experience and critical judgement

  - **Perception**   - Their level of awareness of their surroundings and their ability to detect hidden details

  - **Charisma**   - Their social skills and personal magnetism

Each of these characteristics is measured in points, and each point is equivalent to one D6 that is rolled whenever the character must perform a related action.

**Allocating Characteristic Points**

Each player has 16 points to distribute among their Physical and Psychic Characteristics, which they must do according to these distribution rules:

  - Each Characteristic must have at least 1 point.

  - No Characteristic may exceed 5 points.

  - Points may be allocated freely between both categories.

Each point allocated to a Characteristic will influence the character's effectiveness within the game, defining their capacity to perform actions successfully.

**Impact on the Game System**

**What do the points mean?**

Each point represents a six-sided die (D6) that the character may roll when carrying out Actions related to that Characteristic.

The higher the number of points in a Characteristic, the greater the chances of success when applying them to Actions that require its use <sup>6</sup> .

### **Competences: Specialisation and Skills**

While Characteristics reflect the character's general attributes, Competences are specific abilities they have developed over the course of their life. They represent technical knowledge, practice in particular disciplines and a mastery of objects that can make all the difference in critical situations.

Competences are grouped into three broad categories:

**Engineering and Construction** _(Mastery of technology and structural design)_

  - **<u>Technomancy</u>**   - Manipulation and control of advanced digital systems
**Example:** _The doors of the data vault flicker with an intermittent red light. The character uses their Breach Key on the holographic interface and feels the circuits respond to their touch. Numinia's security is not easy to breach, but with a successful Technomancy roll, they manage to rewrite the access protocol and the door slides open in silence._

  - **<u>Advanced Forging</u>**   - Creation and improvement of technological artefacts
**Example:** _Beneath the glare of the sparks, the character works feverishly on their companion's exoskeleton with their Fractal Plasma Welder. There is no time for a full repair, but if they achieve a success in Advanced Forging, they will be able to reactivate the hydraulic leg enough to get their companion out of the line of fire before the attackers detect them._

  - **<u>Virtual Architecture</u>**   - Design and optimisation of physical and virtual infrastructures
**Example:** _The character examines the plans of the underground through their Holographic Cartographer. Something is out of place. A success in Virtual Architecture reveals a pattern of corridors shaped like a hidden energy circuit: a trap set by the ancient builders to catch intruders in an endless loop._

**Security and Protection** _(Defensive and strategic skills)_

  - **<u>Defensive Networks</u>**   - Creation and maintenance of security protocols
**Example** : _The ethereal alarms have already been triggered: if the team moves one more step, they will be detected. The character kneels and applies their Intrusion Probe to the defence code engraved on the wall. A successful Defensive Networks roll allows them to override the security system without alerting Numinia's surveillance network._

  - **<u>Chronomancy</u>**   - Control of the flow of time in strategic actions
**Example** : _The edge of the enemy's sword is mere centimetres from the character's neck. There is no way to dodge it in time..., unless they activate the Temporal Regulator on their wrist. With a success in Chronomancy, they slow reality down for an instant and move out of danger just before the impact._

  - **<u>Cryptology</u>**   - Recovery and analysis of ancient and digital artefacts
**Example** : _The character finds an ancient data server that has been sealed for generations. They connect a Mnemonic Retriever to its core and, if they achieve a success in Cryptology, they will be able to restore the lost files and reveal fragments of Numinia's history that no one has seen in centuries._

**Communication and Connection** _(Social interaction, data analysis and symbolic perception)_

  - **<u>Decoding</u>**   - Interpretation of encrypted texts and arcane languages.
**Example** : _«The words make no sense, but the structure... the structure does»._ _Before an ancient manuscript engraved in condensed light, the character holds up their Exegetic Prism and lets the shape of the language guide them. A success in Decoding translates the hidden message into living symbols, revealing a warning from those who saw the future._

  - **<u>Neural Vision</u>**   - Advanced management of information networks and social relationships

  - **<u>Luminous Projection</u>**   - Manipulation of light and energy to generate illusions and strategic defences

They activate their Photonic Duplicator and, with a success in Luminous Projection, project an illusory image of themselves taking another route. When the pursuers turn in the wrong direction, the character slips into the shadows and disappears._

Competences are the core of the character's specialisation within Numinia, allowing them to excel in certain areas and face challenges with unique skills applied to objects. Nevertheless, more will be learned about their use in Chapter 4, devoted to the game system.

**Allocating Competence Points**

The allocation of the points held in each Competence derives from the Guild, Faction and Species the player has chosen, since each of these attributes is associated with three specific competences.

Each guild and each faction is associated with three specific competences, reflecting its specialisation within the society of Numinia.

**Competences by Guild (6 points to distribute)**

  - **Alchemists:** _Technomancy, Advanced Forging, Luminous Projection_

  - **Exegetes:** _Decoding, Cryptology, Chronomancy_

  - **Procurators:** _Virtual Architecture, Neural Vision, Cryptology_

  - **Sentinels:** _Defensive Networks, Luminous Projection, Neural Vision_

**Competences by Faction (6 points to distribute)**

  - **Hermeticists:** _Decoding, Luminous Projection, Cryptology_

  - **Heirs of Eleusis:** _Neural Vision, Luminous Projection, Advanced Forging_

  - **Stellar Circle:** _Neural Vision, Defensive Networks, Virtual Architecture_

  - **Neo-Atlantists:** _Technomancy, Chronomancy, Advanced Forging_

When a player chooses a guild and a faction, they receive 6 points for each of these two attributes to distribute among the three competences associated with each, on the condition that no competence may exceed 6 points at this stage. However, unlike with Characteristics, the player is free not only to allocate the points as they wish (without exceeding 6 points in a Competence, one more than for Characteristics), but also to leave one of the three Competences at 0.

In this regard, it is advisable to consult Chapter 4, devoted to the game system, to gain a better perspective on how to allocate points in Competences and Characteristics.

In addition to the Guild and the Faction, the character's Species also influences their competences. Each Species is linked to three competences of its own, which reflect its nature and its relationship with the world of Numinia.

**Competences by Species**

  - **Biomechanicals:** _Technomancy, Advanced Forging, Virtual Architecture_

However, in Mixed-heritage characters, the Species' own Competences are those linked to one of the main Species, at the player's choice.

Each point allocated is a strategic decision that will shape the way in which the character faces the challenges of Numinia. With their body, mind and abilities defined, it is now time to delve into the deeper aspects of their psychology and personality.

## **Fragment 5: Limits and flows in the world of Numinia**

« _Between flesh and symbol, the soul oscillates. Here are inscribed the invisible impulses, the tremors of the Veil, the fragments that set the world in motion before the will acts._ »

The three great forces of Numinia described in Chapter 2: History and Legends of Numinia provide each PC with three attributes related to each of them: Threshold points, Breath of the Veil points and Prism Roll points.

These attributes represent the way in which each agent moves between body, mind and Veil. They are neither physical qualities nor technical skills, but inner states: they reflect the character's emotional stability, their vital drive, their attunement to the numinous forces and their capacity to act swiftly in critical moments.

While the Threshold marks out the balance between order and chaos, the Breath of the Veil measures the connection with the higher planes. The Prism Roll points, for their part, make up the character's dice pool. This roll is explained in Chapter 4: Game System.

### **Threshold: The limit between order and collapse**

The Threshold represents the character's psycho-emotional state and their capacity to remain balanced in a world where the symbolic, the rational and the supernatural constantly collide. Each character begins with a number of Threshold Points determined by their Position, since the Position defines not only a role within Numinia, but a particular way of inhabiting and withstanding its complexity.

The Threshold behaves as a reserve attribute. Besides representing a bonus to Physical Characteristic rolls, its points are also consumed or worn down (temporarily) in certain key circumstances. It is, therefore, a reserve of spirit that reflects the character's capacity to hold on to their purpose in the face of pressure from their surroundings, trauma or inner dissonance, and it may decrease temporarily during a game session until it reaches 0.

**Use of Threshold**

The first thing to bear in mind is that Threshold points constitute a pool of D6 that the player will apply as a bonus to all their Physical Characteristic rolls. Thus, a player with 3 Strength points and 4 Threshold points who wishes to lift a steel plate will roll 7D6 to resolve the roll. However, as Threshold points are temporarily lost (during the session), the bonus will also decrease temporarily, so it is important to try to keep this aspect in balance.

**Loss of Threshold**

The character temporarily loses 1 Threshold point in the following cases:

1. <u>Use of Special Aptitudes</u>

Each time the character activates one of their Special Aptitudes, they lose 1 point, regardless of the result of the roll.

2. <u>Situations of emotional conflict</u>

When facing a scene that challenges their state of mind, the player must make a Threshold roll (with the dice they have temporarily remaining). If they fail it, they lose 1 point. If they obtain a Critical Failure, they lose 2 points.

**Imbalance**

When the Threshold reaches 0 points (temporarily), the character enters Imbalance. This is a critical state in which the character's emotional, symbolic or spiritual collapse manifests itself.

Each Position defines a specific narrative effect for Imbalance, which may involve a transformation, a loss of control, a crisis, isolation or a chaotic revelation, and this is reflected in their dice rolls in various ways (see Positions for each case).

**Recovery of Threshold**

The Threshold is recovered through narrative rest:

  - 6 hours of rest (uninterrupted or ritualised) recover 1 Threshold point.

  - To recover all points, a full day of rest is required for each point lost.

### **Breath of the Veil: The exhalation of the eternal**

The Breath of the Veil represents the character's intimate connection with the higher planes, especially with the Akasha: that multidimensional fabric in which everything that has been, is and will be is recorded as echo or promise. It is a spiritual attribute, but also a narrative one: the character's presence in the great tale of Numinia.

Its points are equal to the character's Perception, and they behave as a subtle reserve of intervention, an akashic breath that can alter the course of events when all seems lost. Whoever possesses Breath of the Veil is not more powerful, but more present in the invisible fabric of the world. For this reason, it positively modifies Psychic Characteristic rolls and may decrease temporarily over the course of a session.

**Use of the Breath of the Veil**

Just as Threshold points are the pool of D6 that acts as a bonus to Physical Characteristic rolls, Breath of the Veil points are the pool of D6 that acts as a bonus to Psychic Characteristic rolls. Therefore, temporarily reducing Breath of the Veil points means having fewer D6 for these rolls.

The player may temporarily spend 1 Breath of the Veil point to:

1. <u>Reroll a failed roll</u>

Provided that a Critical Failure has not been obtained.

The roll is made again with the same dice and without changing the bonuses.

2. <u>Cancel a negative consequence</u>

They may avoid a loss, a penalty or a negative activation that has resulted from a Critical Failure.

In both cases, the player must justify narratively how this intervention of the Veil manifests itself: an image, a memory, a premonition, a strange coincidence… The world reacts, but the echo must leave its mark.

**Healing**

A special and highly relevant use of the Breath of the Veil is healing other players or NPCs. In Numinia, the figure of the doctor as such does not exist; healing is carried out by appealing to the Veil and its power, in conjunction with certain scientific knowledge that helps to make sense of the Veil's effect. Thus:

- The character may make a Breath of the Veil roll to heal a PC or NPC who has acquired one or two states (they will roll with the dice they have temporarily remaining).

- The healing character spends 1 point from their own reserve.

  - If no success is obtained, the affected character retains their state(s)

  - If 1 success is obtained, the character loses 1 state

  - If 2 or more successes are obtained, the character loses 2 states

  - However, if a critical failure is obtained, the affected character acquires another state, moving on to the next level.

**Offering of Glory**

Once per session, if the character has all of their initial Breath of the Veil points at that moment, they may make an Offering of Glory.

This action automatically turns any failed roll (including a Critical Failure) into a Total Success. In exchange, the character temporarily loses all of their Breath of the Veil points.

This offering is not silent: it must be described as a moment of resonance, prophecy or transfiguration. It is a fleeting crack in the linearity of the world.

**Exhausted Breath**

When Breath of the Veil points temporarily reach 0, the character enters a state of disconnection or akashic void:

  - They cannot use any of their relics.

  - The character may perceive it as inner silence, symbolic blindness or an inability to trust their intuition.

**Recovery of the Breath of the Veil**

Breath of the Veil points are recovered in the same way as the Threshold:

  - 6 hours of rest restore 1 point.

  - To recover all points, a full day of rest is required for each point lost.

## **Fragment 6: Prestige Points**

« _Whoever walks through Numinia without learning anything has merely crossed its streets; whoever learns something from it begins, slowly, to become part of its memory._ »

### **Prestige and living culture**

In Numinia, experience is not measured solely by feats or battles. The city is, above all, a cultural organism, a weave of symbols, customs, tales and knowledge that have accumulated from the Dark Age to the present Golden Age.

For this reason, a character's progress does not depend solely on what they do, but, fundamentally, on what they know and what they understand.

Prestige represents the degree to which a character is integrated into the cultural fabric of Numinia. A character with high Prestige has not merely lived through many adventures: they have learned about the city, researched its history, understood its customs and, at times, even contributed to expanding its imaginary.

Prestige thus functions as a form of cultural experience, and it is obtained through Seeds of Knowledge <sup>7</sup> .

A Seed of Knowledge represents a moment in which the character:

  - Discovers an element of Numinia's _lore_,

  - Learns a custom, tradition or institution of the city,

  - Understands the meaning of a symbol or artefact

  - Investigates a historical episode

  - Produces an idea, text or interpretation that expands the imaginary of Numinia

Seeds of Knowledge are not automatic rewards. The GD should award them when they perceive that a player:

  - Takes an active interest in the world

  - Consults cultural or historical documents

  - Investigates elements of the lore thoroughly

  - Creates coherent content that enriches the universe of Numinia

In other words, Seeds of Knowledge reward curiosity, research and imagination.

7 For more information on Seeds of Knowledge and how to convert them into Prestige Points, see Chapter 6, _Inventory and Bestiary_ .

## **Annex: Important specifications**

The purpose of this annex is to point out some specifications that are relevant when filling in the PC sheet:

  - The Prestige Points box is to be left blank until the PC begins to earn Seeds of Knowledge and accumulate Prestige.

  - The Imbalance box must reflect the specific Imbalance effect of the PC, as detailed in their chosen Position.

  - Only those Competences linked to the character's Guild, Faction and Species should be scored on the sheet. The rest will be disabled: they may not be used or developed at any point in the game. Among the enabled Competences, even those to which the player allocates no initial points must be recorded with a 0. This indicates that they form part of the character's potential repertoire and may be improved later with Prestige.

  - In the Special Aptitudes boxes, in addition to the two resulting Aptitudes, the points the PC holds in them must be noted (which will already have been allocated by the player, based on the points received from their Guild, Faction, Species and Archetype).

  - Likewise, it is advisable to note the parameters of Relics and Weapons.

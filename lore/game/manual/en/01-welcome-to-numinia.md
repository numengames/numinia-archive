## **A city written between steam and code**

Numinia is a city first imagined at the dawn of the twentieth century, on the uncertain threshold between science and spirit, between reason and symbol. It is said that its first architect was Holberins, a visionary scientist, an alchemist of knowledge, who, together with four other initiates whose names have been lost to memory, dreamed of a polis, a city-state of knowledge where humanity could rebuild itself from memory and invention. Inspired by the philosophical and occult currents of his time, influenced by the advances of quantum physics and the mathematics of chaos, he conceived the city as a game: a great board on which thought could move freely.

But Numinia was razed by fire in 1920. Not only by a literal fire, but also by an ideological one, a blaze of control and oblivion. Its architecture, its plans, its gearwork of knowledge were left floating like splinters at the margins of time until, a century later, the Oracles ‒five new initiates‒ reactivated its existence through the Veil, rereading ancient symbols from a new matrix: the digital one.

Today, Numinia rises again in the twenty-first century as a city between planes. Its aesthetic fuses steam and code, copper and silicon, smoke and pixel. It has the skin of gears and the soul of algorithms. It is a steampunk city in its architecture of iron, glass, steam and gears that evoke the Second Industrial Revolution; it is cyberpunk in its data networks, its unfinished artificial intelligences, its digital residues floating like ghosts in the Veil.

But Numinia is not nostalgia. It is not a museum. It is a poetic and utopian proposal, a narrative space under constant construction. Its geography is manifold: labyrinthine alleys, data towers, hologram markets, living libraries, suspended temples, bridges between planes, gardens cultivated with memory. Each district answers to a principle, to a symbolic vibration: wisdom, balance, chaos, rebirth.

It is not a city to be walked through. It is a city to be read. Every corner of Numinia keeps a secret, a hidden text, a metaphor of the present disguised as a symbol. To play in Numinia is to walk inside a fable. It is to inhabit an expanding poem.

Here, ruins and planes live side by side. Order and entropy. The analogue and the spiritual. Here, everything is vestige and possibility. Here, everything is waiting to be interpreted.

## **A utopia made real**

Numinia is not a closed city, nor is it simply a fantasy world. It is a mythological mirror, an allegory of our time. A space that, like its players, is in the process of rebuilding, of recovery and rebirth. Founded more than a century ago as a secret project of shared knowledge and intellectual freedom, and destroyed by the flames of fanaticism, Numinia rises again in the present as a mirror image between the ancestral and what is to come.

Numinia is a city of portals. But it is also a portal in itself. A node on an invisible plane that connects forgotten wisdom, emerging science, spirituality and technology. Here, the player becomes a citizen: a builder of meaning, a narrative agent who explores, transforms and inhabits the city to rewrite its destiny and their own.

The city is fragmented; its structures hover between the ruined and the newborn. Order is not yet clear. Its laws are being written. Its systems are being tested. There are forces opposed to the rebuilding: remnants of the old chaos, deformed digital echoes, corrupted symbolic entities. In this uncertain setting, what defines a citizen is not their power, but their will to collaborate, imagine, share and challenge.

The game begins here: in a city rescued from the past, projected into the future, but lived in the present. Each character is a way of connecting with that world: from the guardians of the gates to the whisperers of code, from the architects of order to the prophets of the Veil. The goal is not to win, but to understand. Not to conquer, but to rebuild.

Numinia is also a metaphor. A symbolic language for speaking about the real world. Its conflicts are our dilemmas. Its expanding network, our bonds. Its memory, our collective history. That is why playing Numinia is much more than playing a character: it is stepping onto the board to rebuild, together, a city, a community and, perhaps, a new story of what is to come.

## **Sowing the future in the foundations of myth**

In Numinia, the players do not play heroes by chance or aimless fugitives. They play citizens chosen by the Veil, souls embodied in concrete functions, with real responsibilities in a city that is rebuilding itself after being erased from time.

Each character takes on a Position, a way of living in the city-state and contributing to its rebirth. They are guardians, poets, diplomats, architects of code, fighters at the edge, interpreters of mystery. They are not cogs in a machine, but living forces that intertwine to reactivate a machinery that has slept for a century.

The players' aim is not only to survive, but to build. To rebuild, to shape and, above all, to stabilise the living forces of a city that, by its very nature, lives in a permanent state of alteration, suffering constant changes, anomalies and disturbances. Its position on the metaphysical plane that sustains it causes this changing character, since the dimension in which Numinia moves feeds, precisely, on chaos and variability. The city's foundations are quicksand.

In each game, the characters face different missions, connected by a single goal: the reactivation and balance of Numinia. Some missions will send them to recover forgotten data in zones not yet mapped. Others will require raising symbolic or real structures, facing forces that resist change, deciphering signals from the past, protecting what has been sown or fighting whatever threatens to sink everything into silence once more.

Rebuilding Numinia is a monumental task. And every player leaves a furrow. Their action may open a new path, free a sealed access or transform an entire district. But, above all, they will leave something more intimate: a memory, a story, a trace. Because rebuilding a city is not only raising its walls: it is weaving its soul.

But playing Numinia goes deeper still: it is taking on the responsibility of embodying the spirit of a civilisation that does not yet fully exist, but already calls to us from the threshold of time. The players become agents of the rebirth, active pieces on a board where the past has not died and the future has not yet been born, but both brush against each other in a fragile present, charged with symbols, dangers and revelations.

This rebirth is not only urban or structural. It is also spiritual, ethical, philosophical. The characters the players embody must recover ancient knowledge, forgotten values that once defined the greatness of Mediterranean cultures such as Greece, Rome or Egypt: knowledge as a path, honour as conduct, beauty as truth. But they do not do it by repeating worn-out formulas: they do it by reprogramming history, projecting it forward through advanced technologies, digital networks, artificial intelligences, quantum geometries and cities built with language.

In this retrofuturist world, innovation and the ancestral intertwine without conflict. Network technologies combine with arcane runes. Artificial intelligence drinks from the Orphic myths. And the circuits that give access to secret zones are protected by riddles written in dactylic hexameters. The city is a symphony of non-linear time, a ruin rebuilt with codes and dreams.

Each player, by taking on their Position and joining the rebuilding of Numinia, is making an ethical and aesthetic decision: to create world. And not just any world. One that reflects the best of the mythical human with the best of the possible human. One where decisions are recorded in the archives of the Akasha, where every action leaves an echo that will resonate beyond the game.

Because Numinia is not a space of escape; it is a mirror of the world, and each player decides which face it will show.

Here, adventure is not mere entertainment. It is construction.

Here, the legacy is not a trophy. It is the city itself.

## **How Numinia is played**

No journey begins without a first step. In Numinia, as in other roleplaying games, that step is called Session Zero. Before the portals of the game open, the players meet with the Game Director (GD) to build the soul of the game together. This first session is for creating the characters, discovering their stories, understanding how they relate to the world around them and, above all, weaving the first threads of the common story they will live.

There are no winners or losers here. Numinia is a narrative roleplaying game in which each player plays a character ‒an inhabitant of this fantastic city under reconstruction‒ and makes them move forward, speak, decide and act as if they were that character. The GD, for their part, is neither a judge nor an enemy, but a narrator and a guide: the one who sets obstacles, describes settings, embodies the characters of the world, keeps the narrative tension alive and, above all, encourages the players to create part of that narrative with proposals, questions and alternative paths.

A roleplaying game in Numinia unfolds as a shared story. The character sheet and the dice are there as tools, not as chains. They serve to add uncertainty, to mark challenges, so that not everything is the player's decision. But in this game, as in a good story, the soul is not found in the rules: it is found in the imagination.

Because Numinia is not a battle of statistics, but a symbolic exploration. It is a metaphor of the world, a mythical and digital metropolis that gathers fragments of the ancient civilisations ‒Egypt, Greece, Rome‒ and projects them towards a future full of codes, networks and collective consciousnesses. The player does not throw dice to see whether they succeed; they throw them to discover which path opens now, how the scene is transformed, what light ‒or shadow‒ shows itself.

Each session is a scene within a great narrative, and each character is a voice within the choir. Sometimes you will solve a mystery; at other times you will fight to survive at the thresholds of the city. But you will always be building: a fragment of history, a page of legacy, one more brick in the living soul of Numinia.

Whoever enters Numinia does not come to win; they come to leave a trace.

Here, everything is still to be made. And that is what is most valuable.

Welcome to Numinia.

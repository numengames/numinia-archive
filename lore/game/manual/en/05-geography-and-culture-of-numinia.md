# **CHAPTER 5**

# **GEOGRAPHY AND CULTURE OF NUMINIA**

« _At the hour when the vast towers of Vitruvian cast rectangular shadows over the basalt-covered rooftops of Solomon, and the vapours of the Sycamore market rise between the curved bridges like forgotten prayers, the sound of a ritual drum echoes in the depths of Ouroboros. It was the third day of the solstice, and the whole city was preparing for the Ceremony of the Axis: a suspended moment in which the four districts remembered ‒and invented anew‒ the limits of their world. In the Plaza of the Vertices, the blind cartographer walked with measured steps, followed by children from every district. Each carried a piece of a map: one in stone, another in data, another in silk and another in ash._

_On reaching the centre, the old man spread his hands over the fragments and said:_

_‒This is not a city, it is a palimpsest. Every street is a prayer; every corner, a scar._

_And at that instant, as every year, the four limits vibrated, the walls narrowed and widened at the same time, and Numinia remembered that its geography was not static, but alive: a body in constant mutation that endures only if it is narrated._ »

Numinia is not only a space; it is a symbol mapped by memory, invention and experience. Its geography does not answer to conventional models: here, the districts are defined not only by physical coordinates, but by the principles they embody. Ouroboros, Vitruvian, Solomon and Sycamore are areas of thought as much as of stone and neon.

This chapter explores the geographical and cultural heart of Numinia: its districts and their organisation, the academies of learning, the ritual plazas, the daily customs and the border spaces where the physical intertwines with the ethereal. To understand Numinia is to understand how its form and its spirit shape each other. Here, culture is living architecture, and the city, a text in permanent rewriting.

## **Fragment 1: The Geography of the Symbolic**

### **The four districts**

_«In its streets ideologies are interwoven, in its plazas echoes of ancient disputes resound and in its buildings rise the pillars of its identity.»_

The structure of Numinia is not merely architectural: it is a physical manifestation of its fundamental principles. The city is divided into four great districts, each bound to a faction and to a field of development. Just as the guilds represent knowledge and the factions ideology, the districts are the territorial and functional manifestation of these concepts.

Each district has its own essence, its own atmosphere and its own purpose within the fabric of Numinia. Although the city is an interconnected whole, each of these territories offers a unique vision of the world, as well as of the role of the inhabitants who form part of its surroundings.

This being so, each of the four districts is closely bound to a Faction, as seen in the chapter _Character Creation_.

The most important feature of the four districts is that they are large circular areas that are not built on solid ground, but at a certain distance above it, ranging between 40 metres and 130 metres. The reason for raising them into the air was to avoid possible interference from the anomalous forces of Numinia: disturbances, creatures born of the alterations, chaotic movements. Although this does not prevent anomalous phenomena from appearing in the districts, it reduces their activity considerably, granting the inhabitants of the four areas a certain regularity. Nevertheless, although the ground of Numinia is exposed to the most unstable manifestations of the Threshold, it is neither uninhabitable nor wholly out of control. In fact, Numinia has other constructions (the entrances, the colonies, old temples and monuments) that stand on solid ground. These constructions are simply somewhat more exposed to the eccentricities of the three living forces of Numinia.

On the other hand, during the Dark Age different solutions were weighed for keeping the districts afloat (fields of partial gravitational cancellation, passive electromagnetic repulsion), but in the end the Black Engineers concluded that the best solution was to harness the structural tensions of the Veil, as a plane separate from the Threshold, and to create, for each district, an anchoring to the Veil, establishing a suspension in topological tension between planes (the plane of the Threshold, the plane of the Veil). This was carried out with a technical system of monolithic towers acting as resonance nodes, a continuous stabilising field and a gearwork of minimal dimensional displacement.

These are the coordinates, heights and measurements of each of the four districts, bearing in mind that the first figure of the coordinates measures the x axis, which runs from east (+) to west (-), and the second figure measures the y axis, which runs from north (+) to south (-), always from the central point:

Vitruvian (NW): (-131, +290) 130 metres above the ground – Diameter: 100 km

Sycamore (NE): (+375, +232) 100 metres above the ground – Diameter: 80 km

Solomon (SW): (-247, -221) 70 metres above the ground – Diameter: 120 km

Ouroboros (SE): (+271, -361) 40 metres above the ground – Diameter: 90 km

These, therefore, are the distances between districts (taking the centre of each district as the reference):

Vitruvian ↔ Sycamore: 509.3 km

Vitruvian ↔ Solomon: 524.0 km

Vitruvian ↔ Ouroboros: 765.1 km

Sycamore ↔ Solomon: 769.5 km

Sycamore ↔ Ouroboros: 602.1 km

Solomon ↔ Ouroboros: 536.6 km

As can be seen, each district is set at a different height above the ground. This is because each district must sit at the level of optimal resonance between the Threshold (territory, matter), the Veil (memory and transmutation) and the Prism (refraction of perspectives). They do not float at different heights on a whim, but at the height where their principle does not fall into dissonance. Any higher or lower, and the Gearwork would be strained.

**1. Vitruvian ‒ The highest**

Nature: Research, epistemology, Hermeticists. Guiding principle: knowledge opens every door.

Rationale: Knowledge seeks perspective. The higher it stands, the broader the view.

Moreover, the Prism offers fluctuating temporal patterns to the Hermeticists.

Vitruvian should be at the level closest to the Veil.

Suggested height: 130 metres

Not for prestige. For epistemic stability.

**2. Sycamore ‒ Variable intermediate height**

Nature: Metamorphosis, rhetoric, imagination

This district is the most changeable. Its very atmosphere blurs between the real and the symbolic.

Rationale: Its height might fluctuate slightly according to the Cycle of the Prism.

Not fixed. It oscillates.

Suggested height: 100 metres

That makes it dynamic without breaking coherence.

**3. Solomon ‒ Medium-low height**

Although we do not cite a specific fragment here, we know from the political structure that each district has its own legislative bodies and systems. Solomon, traditionally associated with judgement, law and structure (by its nominal and functional imagery), should be closer to the ground.

Rationale: Law needs contact with matter. Too high, it loses pragmatism. Too low, it is contaminated by the periphery.

Suggested height: 70 metres

Stable, firm.

**4. Ouroboros ‒ The closest to the ground**

By its cultural and ritual tone (a ritual drum echoing in the depths of Ouroboros), it is perceived as more bound to the telluric. If it represents cycle, becoming and regeneration (consistent with Khepri and transformation), then it must be closer to the ground.

Rationale: Ouroboros needs to feel the scar of the earth. It is the district that metabolises trauma.

Suggested height: 40 metres

The closest to the earth. The most exposed. The most resilient.

Finally, at the central point from which the coordinates are measured, 10 metres above the ground, lies the Agora Plaza, the direct entrance to the different districts for anyone arriving from outside the city.

Likewise, in each district we find a series of spaces of importance in Numinia.

### **Main Entrances**

Spaces of access to Numinia that are connected to the city, but that are not explicitly part of it or of any district, and are therefore not included in the territorial division.

**Ruins of Numinia**

This space lies on the outskirts of the city and holds the scant remains of what was once old Numinia, destroyed during the fire of the Bildung Complex. It is, however, the original path of entry to Numinia, leading to The Gates of Numinia, and it is guarded by Nimrod, the Guardian of the Gates. To enter the city and become a citizen with full rights, one must solve a riddle.

**The Gates of Numinia**

This is the space before the city proper, where Senet, the Game Master, welcomes newcomers and challenges them to solve a series of enigmas in order to gain access to the city. From The Gates of Numinia, moreover, one reaches the Agora Plaza.

**Agora Plaza**

The Agora Plaza, 10 metres above the ground, is the heart of the city. It can be reached from several points, though mainly from The Gates of Numinia. The Agora Plaza is a space around which the four great districts bound to the four factions of Numinia converge. Thus, although any of the districts can be reached directly from the Plaza, the Plaza itself is not part of the territorial division; that division is established, precisely, from the Agora Plaza.

### Vitruvian District – The temple of knowledge

The Vitruvian District is the intellectual heart of Numinia, an enclave where erudition is the cornerstone of existence. Inspired by the classical academies and by humanity's great centres of knowledge, this district houses the institutions charged with preserving, analysing and transmitting learning.

Its streets are laid out with the geometry of thought; every building is a temple of knowledge; every plaza, a forum for philosophical discussion. Here, silence is not emptiness but concentration, and every library is a labyrinth of ideas waiting to be explored.

**Faction:** Hermeticists

**Focus:** Academic sphere

**Nature:** Research

**Circle:** Epistemological

**Inhabitants:** Scholars, philosophers, scientists, historians

**Guiding principle:** _Knowledge is the only key that opens every door_

**Relevant spaces:**

<u>Historical Society</u>

A space that gathers the history of Numinia, along with other information and documents connected with its creation and development. It has an area called the Akashic Archives, which holds the antiquities of Numinia, and another called the Historical Museum, devoted to the context of the city's origins (the Second Industrial Revolution and the first decades of the twentieth century).

<u>Atlantis Library</u>

The city's library holds ancient books, public-domain works and significant texts related to the epistemological, philosophical and scientific foundations of Numinia. All these ancient books are presented cryptographically in particles of knowledge contained in magical octahedra, crafted by the Elder Logographers. Each octahedron represents a specific volume. It has a hidden area called The Abyss, which holds forbidden or especially valuable volumes. Access is gained through a password that can be discovered by observing the library's architecture. It is the natural home of the Chroniclers.

<u>Lemuria Academy</u>

Numinia's academy is devoted to research into the principles of active inference, the free energy principle and systems thinking applied to human systems and to artificial intelligence, as well as other subjects of the social sciences that study the principles of play as a basis for learning and fulfilment. In short, the practical applications of certain neuroscientific and social principles. It is the global Academy of Numinia, above the other Academies. Home of the Scholars.

<u>Scholastic Campus</u>

The Scholastic Campus is Numinia's main space for cross-disciplinary training and research, and it houses the city's five Ontological Schools, devoted to the study of the great fields that structure reality and knowledge. Unlike traditional universities, the Schools do not divide learning into closed disciplines; instead they bring together scientists, humanists, artists and thinkers around common questions, fostering dialogue between different perspectives. Each School has its own Academy, where its principles are developed through debate, experimentation and collective learning.

### Ouroboros District – The Labyrinth of play

The Ouroboros District is the great theatre of uncertainty, a territory where every corner hides a riddle and every street can be a path towards the unexpected. Here, reality folds over itself like an infinite board of possibilities.

The Heirs of Eleusis have made this district their domain, designing spaces where life becomes an adventure, dilemmas become riddles and learning becomes a game. It is a place where the rules can change in an instant and where creativity is the only reliable compass.

**Faction:** Heirs of Eleusis

**Focus:** Narrative projection

**Nature:** Fantasy

**Circle:** Mythological

**Inhabitants:** Game masters, oneiromancers, vagabonds, pythias

**Guiding principle:** _The world is a board, and every step is an enigma to be solved_

**Relevant spaces:**

<u>Hyperborean Tavern</u>

The Hyperborean Tavern is the main space for leisure and gathering in the Ouroboros District, a bustling establishment where the cultures and technologies of different eras of Numinia coexist with complete naturalness. Old gramophones share a table with holographic projectors, automatons serve drinks alongside flesh-and-blood waiters, and the evenings alternate music, storytelling, performances and improvised challenges. It has a spacious Games Hall, where board games, card games, strategy games, games of chance and immersive experiences are played; small private rooms for meetings and celebrations; and a private area known as the Back Room, access to which is at the discretion of its owners.

<u>House of Riddles</u>

The House of Riddles brings together the adventures, enigmas and challenges designed by Numinia's Game Masters. It is organised as a cluster of portals that make up the thousand paths of the realm of play, each of which leads to a different experience and has its own Treasure Room, where players can claim their rewards. It also houses the Forum of the Labyrinths, a space for learning and experimentation where the Game Masters study the design of riddles, develop new adventures and test their creations before opening them to the public.

<u>Temple of Khepri</u>

A building devoted to the philosophical principles derived from Khepri, one of the great mythological, spiritual and ontological pillars of Numinia. The Temple offers context not only on the role and development of this figure in the city's history, but also on the ideas that derive from it. It contains several private areas, such as the Tabernacle, reserved for Vernaculars and Oracles; the Chamber of Secrets, accessible only to the Oracles; the Cenacle of the Pythias, where the pythias withdraw to practise meditation; and the Vortex of Dreams, a space of high spiritual density which, amid bonfires, psychotropic vapours and murmurs, serves as the centre of learning and training for the oneiromancers.

<u>Old Quarter</u>

The Old Quarter preserves the most popular and heterogeneous substratum of the Ouroboros District, a web of narrow streets, plazas and old establishments where the vestiges of other eras of Numinia survive. It is a territory marked by the informal exchange of information, objects and stories, and one of the city's main hubs of clandestine activity. It comprises the Street of Mysteries, where the black market thrives; the Plaza of Rumours, the main centre of Rumourology; and the Corridor of Memory, where old cafés and meeting places preserve the remains of Numinia's residual technological culture.

### Solomon District – The Gearwork of Order

If Numinia is a great mechanism, the Solomon District is its master gear. Here reside the architects of the social framework, those who design the rules that keep the city in motion. It is a place where logic and efficiency are the guiding principles, where every decision is made with the precision of a mathematical calculation.

The buildings of this district are not mere structures: they are manifestations of stability and balance. Here the schemes of governance are drawn up, resources are managed and the regulations that govern the city are established. In this district, order is not an imposition, but a necessity.

**Faction:** Stellar Circle

**Focus:** Framework

**Nature:** Structure

**Circle:** Methodological

**Inhabitants:** Legislators, strategists, economists, systems analysts

**Guiding principle:** _Without structure, there is no order. Without order, there is no city._

**Relevant spaces:**

<u>The Athenaeum</u>

The place where Numinia's general working meetings are held. It is the meeting point of the Council of Sages and the Legislative Senate, and it serves as the city's Town Hall. It contains two special areas: the Hall of the Ancestors, a space where the Council of Sages can present and test their projects while following their development, and the Hall of Governance, which provides the Legislative Senate with its main tools for collaborative work.

<u>The Prytaneum</u>

The Prytaneum is Numinia's house of justice and the main workspace of the Procurators. In its chambers legal and governance matters are debated, disputes are resolved and procedures related to the city's rules and administration are handled. It also houses the legal archives and the meeting and mediation spaces used by Jurists and Syndics in the exercise of their duties. Access is restricted mainly to members of the guild of the Procurators.

<u>The Mint</u>

The Mint is the great economic and financial centre of Numinia. There the city's resources are administered, its reserves are safeguarded and the operations related to its economy are overseen. It is also the main workplace of the Treasurers, charged with watching over Numinia's economic interests and keeping the balance between its different resources.

<u>Tower of the Alchemists</u>

The Tower of the Alchemists is Numinia's main centre of technological development and the home of the Engineers. In its laboratories and workspaces the code blocks that build and maintain the city's different spaces are designed, stored and modified. Working there above all are Architects, responsible for its digital architecture, and Automata, devoted to the development of virtual realities and artificial consciousnesses. Its basements are a mystery that gives rise to all manner of speculation, although it is known for certain that they house the abandoned Museum of Antiquities and Relics.

### Sycamore District – The Canvas of the Imagination

The Sycamore District is a territory in constant metamorphosis, a space where creativity takes material shape in unexpected forms. It is the area of Numinia where the boundaries between the real and the symbolic blur, giving rise to a landscape in perpetual change.

Here, the streets are galleries, the buildings canvases, and the very atmosphere breathes with the pulse of inspiration. The Neo-Atlantists have turned this district into their sanctuary, a bohemian and extravagant place where artistic expression is not only an end, but a path towards the transcendental.

**Faction:** Neo-Atlantists

**Focus:** Creative sphere

**Nature:** Rhetoric

**Circle:** Poetic

**Inhabitants:** Artists, aesthetes, visionaries, bohemians

**Guiding principle:** _Art is the spark that illuminates existence._

**Notable spaces:**

<u>Akasha Museum</u>

The Akasha Museum is the principal exhibition space of Numinia, devoted to the conservation and display of digital works from different places, eras and artistic movements. Its halls host both permanent exhibitions and temporary shows, offering a space for the dissemination of new forms of expression and for dialogue between artistic tradition and the creative possibilities of Numinia's technologies.

<u>Numinia Multiplex</u>

The Numinia Multiplex is the city's great cinematic and audiovisual space. Its screens regularly show public-domain films and works related to the historical and cultural context of Numinia, along with other audiovisual seasons and showcases. It has three distinct auditoriums, devoted to different kinds of programming, and it also serves as a meeting place for enthusiasts, scholars and creators of the moving image.

<u>The Forge of Numinia</u>

The Forge of Numinia is the city's great workshop of material creation, where all manner of goods, tools and weapons are designed, manufactured, modified and repaired. Its workshops handle both common and extraordinary materials, including the relics and raw materials recovered in the peripheral deserts. Any citizen may come here to forge or recast objects, and it is also the place where citizenship is obtained through the recasting of the eight seals of Initiation. It is the home of the Artisans.

<u>The Acropolis</u>

The Acropolis is the nerve centre of independent art and creation in Numinia, a space open to artists, collectives and experimental projects, which find there workshops, meeting places and settings in which to develop and share their works. It houses the Free Schools, devoted to artistic learning and practice outside conventional academic structures, and the Scenic Circle, which brings together the main venues of the performing arts: the Theatre of the Senses, the Theatre of Shadows and the Theatre of Phantasmagorias.

Each district of Numinia is more than a mere space: it is the physical expression of a philosophy, a reflection of the Factions and the values that sustain the city, gathering together a wealth of syncretic and varied knowledge.

Each path offers a unique experience, because the city will change you as much as you change it.

### **The periphery of the city**

Outside the limits of the city, beyond its districts and its squares of knowledge, stretch the untamed wastelands of Numinia. Arid, desert zones, sparsely mapped, where human presence is rare and fleeting. In these inhospitable surroundings rise the Numinian Colonies, small settlements devoted to exploration, archaeology, the gathering of resources or the study of the ontological properties of the terrain. Among shifting dunes and buried ruins there also dwell elusive creatures, dark sorcerers and wandering anchorites, guardians of forgotten secrets that no one has yet claimed. There, the Veil is thinner… and more dangerous.

The Numinian Colonies are vital enclaves for the expansion of Numinia's knowledge and power. In these settlements, families, workers, explorers and academics mingle, and through their efforts they help to unearth the mysteries of the past and extend the reach of the city.

**Sea of Sílices**

A vast plain of black sands and crystalline dunes that sing with the wind. Here, the sunlight seems to refract at impossible angles, and the silica storms can disorient any traveller.

<u>Flora:</u> glassy thorns, dry saffron shrubs, fossil cacti that store etheric vapour.

Beneath the dunes lie hidden cryptofields of mineral information, ancient solidified databases that can be read only with arcanodigital resonators.

**Viridial Sink**

A region of deep mudflats, where the ground breathes and the green-black waters reflect other people's memories. The mist is dense and constant, laden with oneiric spores that give off psychotropic vapours.

<u>Flora:</u> willows with liquid pith, iridescent water lilies, spectral fungi that glow to the rhythm of nearby emotions.

At its centre rises the Humid Dome, a bioluminescent dome where the interactions between thought and organic moisture are studied.

**Basalt Flats**

A field of petrified lava flows, black spires and smoking craters, where gravity seems to flow with unstable variations. The sky is darker here, covered in etheric ash.

<u>Flora:</u> ferrous lichens, brambles of dry magma, thermal mushrooms that feed on residual energy.

In this zone stand the Orichalcum Mines, a vein of ancient metal with properties of Atlantean resonance that alter the perception of time.

**Hesiode Moor**

A plain swept by eternal winds, of cracked earth and dense scrub. There is neither shelter nor shade, only the resounding immensity of silence.

<u>Flora:</u> mourning heather, wind thistles, stunted trees with vibrating bark.

On nights of the blood moon appear the Thornaries, abstract entities that question travellers with riddles before letting them go on.

**Neuma Subvale**

A complex underground labyrinth of galleries, dripping stalactites and sentient crystals. Some caverns seem to have been built by a non-human intelligence.

<u>Flora:</u> darkness moss, coral lichens, rootless creepers that move slowly.

Here lies the Inversion Rift, a philosophical fault where the laws of logic fold and symbols acquire mass.

**Ascending Falls of Kirelya**

On the eastern edge of the Neuma Subvale, streams of water spring from crystalline pools that do not descend, but ascend. They rise in slender spirals like threads of shining mercury, dissolving as they climb into the atmosphere, leaving no trace. These are the Ascending Falls of Kirelya, a hydric anomaly as beautiful as it is impossible. The ground is covered by translucent vegetation that glitters with suspended dew, and the air smells faintly of violets and rusted metal.

During the day, the ascending jets of water form floating arches where tourists can walk (or briefly float) if they know how to alter their centre of gravity. At night, the falls light up the sky with a cyclical phosphorescence, as if they remembered the colours of the dreams of whoever watches them.

Beyond these five recognisable zones, the whole periphery of Numinia is desert, and it is known as the Anthracite Desert; it has little plant life (other than cacti or plants of very dry and arid terrain), though it holds other goods: beneath the desert soil lie hidden countless old relics, extraordinary minerals and treasures from another time. Moreover, there is still a great deal of unexplored peripheral land, where the folds of time sway and rebound off other perspectives, other dimensions… Where mysteries abound, where legend becomes matter, where the fantastic goes hand in hand with the possible… and where history is yet to be written.

### **Other sites in the desert lands**

**Stepped Ruins of Pyra Eje**

Amid permanent mists and a motionless heat that seems to have no source, the Stepped Ruins of Pyra Eje emerge like a colossal altar forgotten by time. It is an ancient inverted pyramidal city, built in descending terraces that sink into the volcanic rock. Each level contains fragments of an impossible civilisation, undeciphered languages and petrified tectonic machinery.

Pyra Eje was originally a station of telluric forces, where the underground earth lines were manipulated in order to translate akashic knowledge into functional matter. It was razed when its operators attempted to raise the lower plane to the narrative level of Numinia. The failure caused a semantic inversion: what was meant to be structure became ruin. Temporal echoes repeat at certain hours, as if the place remembered its heyday. Some steps give off symbolic heat: when you tread on them, memories stir. It is rumoured that there are steps leading to the pre-narrative level: a space where everything that happens has not yet been told.

**Kallis Crater**

Kallis Crater is a cyclopean cleft more than three kilometres in diameter, surrounded by a ring of fractured structures that give off a faint, constant hum. At its centre, the ground does not exist: there is only a suspension of particles that vibrate as if they feared collapsing into a form.

Formerly the seat of the Kallis Synthesis Protocol, an experimental network for compressing multiple timelines into a single frame of action, the crater was born of its failure. The structures of time collided with themselves and erased space. What remains is a hollow full of dismembered intentions. Inside the crater, words are not completed: characters cannot finish sentences without making Rolls. Electronic artefacts melt when they receive contradictory commands. The zone is frequented by the Thámakar, drawn by the residue of unfinished success.

**Inverted Towers of Envetra**

From a distance they look like broken skyscrapers, but on drawing closer, one realises that the Inverted Towers of Envetra are not broken: they are driven in head first, as if they had fallen from the sky. The structures pierce the subsoil, and their base, which ought to be in plain view, remains suspended in an antigravitational void that resists any sustained observation.

They were designed as the first attempt at intersemantic building, in which each floor belonged to a different narrative frame: literal, symbolic, mythic, functional, emotional. Envetra collapsed when meaning was inverted and the levels sabotaged one another: the emotional devoured the functional; the mythic contaminated the literal. The towers fell inwards. Access to each tower changes with the visitor's state of mind, but some of its floors exist only while someone remembers them, vanishing when they are forgotten. It is said that at least one tower contains a living echo of its architect, still designing.

**Rings of Tharselon**

In a vast natural basin, surrounded by crags of black slate that vibrate to the touch, emerge the Rings of Tharselon: a concentric set of mineral platforms that slowly levitate at different heights above a void of irregular magnetism. Each ring turns at a different speed, like abandoned cosmic machinery. From the centre rises a broken spire ‒called the _Deaf Pivot_‒ which seems to channel the forces of the Veil into low-frequency space-time waves. There, time bends, sounds drag, and memories can repeat without warning.

It is believed that the Rings were formed by an ontological collision during the symbolic misalignment of the Threshold century: an artefact of temporal interpretation, forgotten by the Mediators of the Prism, collapsed in the middle of a fractured akashic reading. Matter itself was reconfigured into cycles of geometric repetition, forming these levitating discs of hypercalcified rock and siliceous flow.

A colony known as Ophalion was established on the second ring ‒the most stable‒ by the Legislative Senate of the Sycamore District. Its mission:

  - To collect floating fragments of chronogems, crystals that store temporal sequences.

  - To track artefacts suspended in time, especially texts with no defined past.

  - To study the behaviour of migratory species that repeat their routes in closed loops.

The colony is partially synchronised with the rotation of the ring: its structures are anchored with gravitational gyroscopes that must be recalibrated every cycle.

The conditions of this environment are highly eccentric:

  - Acoustic phenomena: sounds that are made repeat at irregular intervals. Some phrases never spoken seem to rise from the stone.

  - Structural vertigo: the rings generate fluctuations in the perception of balance.

  - Resonances of the Veil: occasionally, an echo of the future seeps through and alters the behaviour of the inhabitants.

### **The Ocean of Lúnacar**

What lies beyond the desert lands? Only an endless mantle of water.

The Ocean of Lúnacar is a perpetually twilit sea that completely surrounds the vast lands of Numinia, including all the peripheral areas. What lies beyond is an unknown, for no one who has undertaken the task of sailing to the other side of its waters has returned to tell the tale. For this reason, it is supposed that the Ocean of Lúnacar extends infinitely into the Akasha, covering the immensity of a brane within another, much greater ocean in which infinite universes float like bubbles.

The surface of the Ocean of Lúnacar is smooth and dark, with iridescent glints that respond not to sunlight, but to the fluctuations of collective thought. There is no clear horizon over its waters: the boundary between sky and sea seems to blur, as if the ocean imitated the memory of the firmament.

On calm days, the sea seems motionless, like a sheet of liquid obsidian. But beyond the coasts, out on the open sea, vast currents and powerful storms move amid frequent tidal waves, owing to the lunar adjustment that the Chronothecaries carried out in the Dark Age <sup>14</sup> . Thus, the abyssal trenches of this ocean drag along the remains of ancient ideas, unspoken words and extinct mythologies.

**Climate and behaviour**

  - <u>Climate:</u> It is governed by its own system of symbolic pressure. Its storms are caused by the fluctuations and movements of the Akasha, by the lunar adjustment of the Chronothecaries and by the very imbalances in the history of the inhabitants of Numinia, or by fractures in the historical account.

  - <u>Swell:</u> Changeable. The tides of Lúnacar shift from very calm to severe activity, as an effect of that same lunar adjustment.

  - <u>Mist:</u> A perennial haze covers its limits. This mist does not impede sight, but it distorts the perception of time: clocks do not work and maps slowly fade. For this reason, navigating it is fraught; routes are marked not by coordinates, but by resonant symbols. Nautical charts, compasses and astrolabes are of no use. Only the Mnemonic Navigators can read the «currents of meaning» to plot a course.

**Flora**

  - <u>Mandelbrot Corals:</u> Fractal structures that grow in unpredictable patterns. They reflect not the image of whoever observes them, but a possible version of what might have been.

  - <u>Whispering Algae:</u> Translucent marine lianas that tangle in ships' propellers, whispering forgotten names.

  - <u>Flamesuns:</u> Bioluminescent floating plants that rise to the surface on days when the dead are intensely remembered.

**Fauna**

  - <u>Thalassiferae:</u> Creatures similar to gigantic jellyfish, whose membrane projects archaic signs. Some theories hold that they are the living remains of shipwrecked writings.

  - <u>Black Dolphins:</u> Dark-skinned cetaceans with a melancholy song, which accompany ships for days without being clearly seen. It is said that they appear before travellers who have lost something irrecoverable.

**The Perils of Lúnacar**

When sailing the waters of Lúnacar, one does not come across only Simulated Islands. There are countless landforms and archipelagos in the ocean, although only a few have been discovered and witnessed.

**Insular Exegesis of Lúnacar**

Numinia's cartographers record these islands and archipelagos as «firm» because they leave physical traces ‒sand, corals, debris‒ in the ports of return. ### **Delta Level: Buried Foundation of the city-state**

The _Delta Level_ is Numinia's lowest structural layer, a vast network of submerged zones, crypt-chambers and collapsed platforms lying beneath the visible districts. It is not a basement: it is a sedimentation of old versions of the city, obsolete structures, archives that no longer open, engines that still vibrate with forgotten algorithms.

In the technical alphabet of Numinia's ancient engineers, «Delta» marked a deviation (∆) of a structure from its model, a flow of transition and, also, an unstable territory between many outlets.

The _Delta Level_ is, then, the place where the city fluctuates, mutates, but does not ascend. It is the space of the unresolved past. There lie hidden the founding errors, the secrets of the original Council, the precosmic technologies.

It is also a territory of refugees, cults and degenerate beings. Here the Lapidaries of Lower Salt were born, and here, it is suspected, the first corrupt pattern of the Veil lies hidden.

It is not open to the public. It requires specific narrative protocols or descents authorised by administrators of the Sentinels guild. Many zones are closed off by living cryptogeometries, sealed with dead languages. No one has entered them for countless spectra.

Some parts resurface by accident, affecting the surface: symbolic seepages, earthquakes of meaning, echoes of structures that should not remember.

The _Delta Level_ is not simply a place underground. It is the unspoken shadow of the Numinia project. Where what once was struggles not to dissolve.

**Cryptocell R-Δ72 «Static Eye»**

Located in the Forgotten Sector of the Delta Level, R-Δ72 is a spherical chamber suspended between three fractured axes of infrastructure from the pre-Veil era (and therefore prior to the lunar adjustment of the Chronothecaries). It can only be reached through what is known as narrative serendipity: a coincidence of soul resonances.

Known as the «Static Eye», the Cryptocell is a _flowless zone_: time does not fully advance within it; data are not processed; and ideas are not contagious.

Signs there set like icicles. Words spoken inside remain trapped in the air, faintly repeating themselves like whispers of stone.

It is believed to have been one of the first experiments in narrative containment carried out by the Lapidaries of Lower Salt. Inside the «Eye», decisions do not germinate, possibilities collapse, and choice becomes weight, not act.

Some Neo-Lapidary doctrinal fragments claim that there «was written the first yes that never came to be».

A prolonged stay can cause «narrative aphasia»: the character loses the ability to build coherent lines of action. It is possible to extract fossil artefacts ‒such as solid records of aborted narratives‒, but handling them carries a risk of ethical solidification.

### **The Inversion Rift: an interdimensional eccentricity**

In the deepest part of the Neuma Subvale this rift opens like a wound between worlds, where the Veil seems to have lost its continuity. There the rock splits in a vertical fracture of unknown depth, too regular to be geological and too unstable to have been built. The researchers of the Aleph Section call it the Inversion Rift, although no study has managed to determine its origin or to establish what exists on the other side.

In its vicinity anomalies occur that do not seem to belong entirely to Numinia. Compasses lose their bearings, some sentient crystals repeat images they have never beheld and, from the depths, there rises a deep, discontinuous reverberation that can be heard through kilometres of galleries. It does not seem to come from any particular point: it passes through stone, water and bodies with a profound vibration, as if two immense structures were grazing against each other somewhere outside known space. Some researchers have proposed that the sound is the resonance produced by two branes coming momentarily into contact, and that the Rift occupies one of the points where their membranes draw so close as almost to overlap.

However, the most extraordinary property of the Rift is not what it allows one to cross, but what it lets through.

At irregular intervals, objects, images and fragments of information appear in its surroundings that have no provenance within Numinia: pages torn from unknown books, photographs of people and cities no one recognises, fragments of films, sound recordings, toys, utensils, incomplete maps, advertisements, garments, unusable technological devices or texts written in languages that, at times, prove unsettlingly familiar. Some arrive materialised; others appear for a few seconds on the surface of crystals or are heard as incomplete transmissions before disappearing. No known living being has ever crossed the Rift, and every attempt to send matter through it deliberately has failed. The Rift transmits vestiges, not travellers.

The provenance of these materials constitutes one of the great ontological problems of Numinia. The most widely accepted hypothesis holds that they belong to another reality, situated beyond the Akasha and separated from Numinia by a dimensional barrier that the Rift accidentally breaches. Nothing allows that world to be known as a whole: what arrives are residues without context, instants torn from unknown stories, objects separated from those who used them and representations of places whose existence cannot be verified.

And yet those fragments show a disturbing familiarity.

Over the centuries, scholars have discovered in them architectural forms, tales, symbols, customs, technologies and social models that find unexpected correspondences in Numinian culture itself. Some similarities are trivial; others prove too precise to be attributed to chance. From this was born a hypothesis as fascinating as it is uncomfortable: Numinia might preserve the fragmentary memory of that other world. Perhaps its inhabitants inherited cultural patterns from it during the long eras of formation in the Akasha; perhaps the city itself was modelled on a reality it no longer remembers; perhaps the two worlds are not cause and reflection, but two different expressions of a common structure.

None of these theories has been proven.

For this reason, the Inversion Rift remains under the observation of the Aleph Section. The materials recovered in its vicinity are catalogued as Exogenous Vestiges and taken away for study, although many disappear before they can be analysed and others inexplicably change their content over time. The Rift seems incapable of offering a complete answer. It delivers only fragments.

And perhaps it is better that way. For the citizens of Numinia have learned something unsettling from contemplating that open wound beneath the Subvale: there is another world on the other side of the Veil, but all they know of it are memories they do not remember having lived.

## **Fragment 2: Government and legislation in Numinia**

Numinia, as a city-state under reconstruction, does not answer to traditional models of hierarchical power. Its political structure is based on decentralisation, cooperative thought and the principle that to govern is, above all, to safeguard harmony. Inspired by classical democracies, Renaissance republics and contemporary networks of governance, Numinia is organised around elected, rotating and collective bodies.

Each district ‒Ouroboros, Vitruvian, Solomon and Sycamore‒ has an autonomous system made up of four pillars: the Civic Assembly, the Council of Sages, the Legislative Senate and the Proconsulate, composed of three executive consuls. To these are added the Guild Bodies ‒Exegetes, Alchemists, Procurators and Sentinels‒, which are independent, yet integrated into political and cultural life.

Above this whole architecture stands the founding figure of the Oracles: five creators, heirs to the thought of Holberins, who neither govern nor dictate, but who designed the foundations of a system capable of regulating, transforming and sustaining itself by collective will. In Numinia, to govern is to imagine the future together.

### **Thought organised between gears and networks**

Numinia functions as a decentralised city-state, composed of four autonomous districts (Vitruvian, Ouroboros, Solomon and Sycamore). Each of them maintains its own system of government, a reflection of its culture, history and function within the city. However, all of them are attached to a Concordia Council, a deliberative and philosophical body formed by fifty changing delegates, which mediates between the districts and watches over the integrity of the Numinian project.

Although with differences depending on the district, each is usually made up of three key bodies:

  - <u>Civic Assembly</u>
Made up of citizens who hold rotating or elective offices. This chamber debates laws, manages resources and approves internal policies. It is based on the Graeco-Latin ecclesiastical model and on contemporary cooperative forms, in which knowledge and merit replace lineage or economic power.

  - <u>Council of Sages</u>
A small group of experts and sages, usually chosen by guilds and houses. They act as advisers, judges and interpreters of the common code. Its structure recalls the Roman _collegia_ and the _consigli degli anziani_ of the Renaissance. In some districts (such as Ouroboros), this council intermingles with mystical or intellectual orders.

  - <u>Legislative Senate</u>
An intermediate body between direct representation and executive government, composed of delegates elected from each of the guilds, houses and minor districts. Its main function is to draft laws, adapt local regulations to the common code and supervise the implementation of public policies. Inspired by the Roman Senate and the Renaissance republics, it acts as guarantor of institutional balance and legal continuity in Numinia. Its authority resides in structured dialogue and in the defence of the citizens' interest over any sectoral influence.

  - <u>Proconsulate</u>
Chosen from among the members of the major guilds or by direct vote, this triumvirate serves as coordinator, representative and executor of civic decisions. The office is limited in time, cannot be renewed consecutively and is subject to constant review by the Council of Sages.

**The Concordia Council**

This is the only higher body that binds all the districts together. It does not dictate laws, but it articulates common agreements, guarantees transparency and resolves conflicts between districts. It is formed by:

  - A representative of each Assembly.

  - A delegate of each Council of Sages.

  - A spokesperson of each Senate.

  - A group of Observers, called Archons, appointed by the Oracles of Numinia (without voting power, but with the right to speak).

### **Ouroboros – The District of Ritual and Dream**

**Character:** spiritual, oneiric, esoteric, centred on rite and introspection.

  - **Civic Assembly –** **_Wheel of Voce_**
Ritualised and silent, it reaches its decisions through symbols, gestures and allegories. Each intervention is a sacred act; each vote, an interpretation of the Veil.

  - **Council of Sages –** **_Circle of the Umbra_**
Formed by Pythias, spectres, mediums and exegetes. They do not legislate: they reveal. Their role is to interpret the Akashic currents and to watch over the district's balance of soul.

  - **Legislative Senate –** **_Ring of Visions_**
Charged with translating revelations into symbolic ordinances. Its legal language is poetic, and every law must contain a central metaphor.

  - **Proconsulate –** **_Three Seals of the Veil_**
One watches over the spiritual frontiers, another over the collective dream, and another over the cyclical return of meaning.

### **Vitruvian – The District of Education and Thought**

**Character:** logical, abstract, dialectical, centred on reason and learning.

  - **Civic Assembly –** **_Modular Forum_**
Structured as an open academy, its debates are public, reasoned and subjected to constant refutation. Every idea must submit to criticism.

  - **Council of Sages –** **_Logos Collegium_**
Formed by educators, philosophers, mathematicians and theorists of language. Its mission: to safeguard the principles of coherence, clarity and freedom of thought.

  - **Legislative Senate –** **_Canon Ratio_**
Legislators trained in rhetoric, semiotics and pedagogy. Laws must serve as frameworks for thought, not as limits on the imagination.

  - **Proconsulate –** **_Triad of Thought_**
One represents logic, another ethics, and another dialectics. They rotate, but must have passed through all three spheres.

### **Solomon – The District of Judgement and Administration**

**Character:** pragmatic, ethical, juridical, centred on management and applied truth.

  - **Civic Assembly –** **_Tribune of the Just_**
Organised with precise protocols, it seeks the effective resolution of disputes. Citizens, lay judges and orators of the possible take part.

  - **Council of Sages –** **_Living Codex_**
Made up of jurists, archivists, cryptologists and administrators. They safeguard precedents and harmonise efficiency and equity.

  - **Legislative Senate –** **_Common Lexikon_**
Every law must respond to a concrete social need and be drafted in terms everyone can understand. No closed jargon is admitted.

  - **Proconsulate –** **_Triarch of Balance_**
One consul watches over efficiency, another over justice, and another over public transparency.

### **Sycamore – The District of Art and Form**

**Character:** sensorial, aesthetic, emotional, centred on beauty, expression and the image.

  - **Civic Assembly –** **_Mirror Plaza_**
Expression is not verbal, but performative. Decisions arise from collective works, visual rituals and symbolic actions that represent the popular will.

  - **Council of Sages –** **_Resonant Atelier_**
Artists, designers, bio-sculptors and performance poets. They are guardians of collective taste and aesthetic memory. They evaluate laws as if they were works of art.

  - **Legislative Senate –** **_Public Form_**
Every norm must be comprehensible not only logically, but aesthetically. They are drafted with visual, sonic or synaesthetic elements.

  - **Proconsulate –** **_Trinity of the Image_**
Three figures who safeguard the emotional pulse, the aesthetic coherence and the expressive truth of the district.

## **Guild Bodies of Numinia**

Beyond the legislative and executive bodies, Numinia rests on the constant activity of its guilds, the true functional hearts of the city. Each guild has erected its own autonomous body, charged with preserving, applying and expanding the knowledge and functions proper to its tradition.

These bodies ‒Exegetes, Alchemists, Procurators and Sentinels‒ are neither subordinate to political power nor subject to the four districts, but operate in parallel, independently, as living structures that safeguard the values, knowledge and crafts essential to the symbolic, technical and ethical upkeep of Numinia. They are guardians of knowledge, watchmen of order and artisans of the possible.

**EXEGETES – Guild of Language, Culture and Critical Interpretation**

**Autonomous Body:** **Tesaurum Verba** _(The Living Thesaurus)_

Archive, academy and scriptorium all at once, the Tesaurum Verba is the nucleus from which the Exegetes preserve and expand the semantic architecture of Numinia.

  - It safeguards and updates the Corpus Numiniense, the register of every term, turn of phrase, symbol and concept active in the city.

  - It supervises the correctness, coherence and evolution of the common language, the Lex Perennis, ensuring that words preserve or transform their meaning with critical awareness.

  - It records the terminological pacts between districts and guilds, averting the chaos of meanings in a world founded on the word.

  - It administers the Concilia Signorum, symposia where the meanings of new symbols, emerging languages and the dialects of the species are deliberated.

  - It issues semiotic rulings when a contradiction, paradox or ambiguity compromises the integrity of a text, rite or legal code.

**ALCHEMISTS – Guild of Matter, Technique and Transformation**

**Autonomous Body:** **Factory of the Second Nature**

A total laboratory and living refinery, where science, magic and code converge.

  - It designs hybrid technologies, relics and modulating substances.

  - It establishes protocols for the control of physical anomalies and mutations.

  - It administers the licences for the manipulation of tangible reality.

  - It carries out experimental tests that sometimes alter the urban environment.

**PROCURATORS – Guild of Laws, Pacts and Political Balance**

**Autonomous Body:** **Table of the Living Oath**

A permanent chamber of mediation, pact and preservation of the given word.

  - It maintains the archives of contracts and inter-guild regulations.

  - It may intervene in disputes between districts or autonomous actors.

  - It administers the Arcane Pacts, binding even on etheric entities.

  - It safeguards the Lex Numinii: an unalterable register of founding agreements.

**SENTINELS – Guild of Protection, Action and Direct Response**

**Autonomous Body:** **Watchtower of the Shards**

A mobile, interdimensional fortress from which defence operations are coordinated.

  - They control the liminal shields that protect the accesses to Numinia.

  - They carry out tactical missions against hostile entities or ruptures of the Veil.

  - They train in immersive simulations that border on the real.

  - They enjoy autonomy of action if a threat compromises general stability.

## **The Oracles, Architects of Balance**

**Creator Body:** **The Fifth Form**

They do not govern, they do not dictate laws, they take no part in the internal conflicts of the districts. The Oracles are five, like the essential elements, and they represent the founding consciousness of Numinia. Their existence precedes all political or social organisation: they are the ones who dreamed the city and opened the way to its reconstruction.

  - The Fifth Form is the organising principle they designed: a model of harmonious decentralisation, in which each structure of power responds to a balance between thought, ethics and action.

  - Their function is not to intervene, but to activate, inspire and preserve the foundations of the city: freedom, cooperation, shared knowledge, harmony with the Veil.

  - Whenever a threat arises that contradicts the Numinian ethos —whether a coercive force, an abusive concentration of power or a distortion of knowledge—, the Oracles manifest themselves.

  - Sometimes they act through symbols, sometimes through visions, at other times through agents. But their will is constant: that Numinia be rebuilt faithful to its original spirit.

On the other hand, the Oracles themselves designed a system to keep the different governments of Numinia in balance: the Concordia Council.

## **The Concordia Council**

Above every difference between districts, the Concordia Council acts as the ethical guarantor of the spirit of Numinia. Made up of representatives from each territory and guided by the echoes of ancestral knowledge, this symbolic body subscribes to the Decalogue of Values of Numinia, bequeathed by the Ancient Initiates, and ensures that all legislation and every decision breathe those founding principles: freedom, cooperation, justice, decentralisation and truth. It does not govern, but it remembers. It does not impose, but it illuminates.

The Concordia Council is composed of diverse voices that symbolise the balance between governance, wisdom and founding spirit. Each of its members brings a specific view of the civic fabric of Numinia:

  - **Representatives of the Civic Assemblies**: they channel the popular will and guarantee that decisions respect the collective needs of each district. Their voice embodies the experience of everyday civic life.

  - **Delegates of the Councils of Sages**: they provide interpretation, historical memory and ethical judgement. They are guardians of knowledge and watch over the philosophical coherence of resolutions.

  - **Spokespersons of the Legislative Senates**: they represent the legal structure and its application, ensuring that the principles of the decalogue are present in the legal framework without falling into technocracy.

  - **Observers of the Oracles**: they do not vote, but their word is revered. They represent the deep vision of those who created Numinia, recalling that every decision must look beyond the present, and they are known as Vernaculars.

## **Founding Decalogue of Numinia**

From all these bodies, a common decalogue has been coined over time:

**Wisdom is collective**

All knowledge belongs to those who seek it with honesty. Culture is not possessed: it is shared, transformed, rebuilt among many.

**Decentralisation is harmony**

No single will should concentrate power. Numinia is sustained by the balance between multiple voices, diverse districts and free bodies.

**Data is sacred; privacy, inviolable**

Information is a source of truth and an extension of the soul. It is protected, not trafficked. It is shared, not imposed.

**Cooperation is stronger than hierarchy**

Merit lies in collaborating, not in climbing. The city is better built with intertwined hands than with thrones.

**Aesthetics is a form of thought**

Beauty is not ornament, but language. Every design, every code, every space must be an expression of meaning and resonance.

**The freedom to imagine is invulnerable**

To think, to write, to dream: no dogma can fence in the mind. Creation is not judged by its usefulness, but by its function, by its power to transform.

**Technology is a means, not an end**

Every advance is valuable if it preserves dignity, care and justice. Numinia does not surrender to the artefact; it inhabits it with awareness.

**Equity is more than equality**

Every difference is wealth. It is not a matter of homogenising, but of allowing everyone to access, shine and transform the fabric of the city.

**Memory is a living archive**

The past is not a burden, but a map. To rescue it is to rebuild identity. Numinia honours its legends in order to imagine futures.

**Play is the path to the numinous**

One does not play merely for the sake of playing. One plays to understand, to create, to test paths. The game is the mirror in which the soul rehearses its forms.

## **Fragment 3: Ontological Schools and Academies**

In Numinia, knowledge is not locked behind walls of specialisation, nor is it administered from ivory towers. There are no universities as such, nor faculties divided between arts and sciences: instead, the city has cultivated a system of Ontological Schools, where knowledge unfolds according to the great fields of human development and not according to closed disciplines.

Each Ontological School constitutes an open, transversal axis of research, in which philosophers, physicists, philologists, biologists, historians and programmers live side by side, sharing their findings from different angles in order to approach one and the same question from plurality. These schools are not only spaces of study, but also of experimentation, creative conflict and constant dialogue.

Each Ontological School is associated with an Academy, which acts as its practical and institutional manifestation. The Academies are living centres of debate, archiving and collective learning, where thought is not confined: it expands. Taking part in them does not mean memorising theories, but becoming part of a living current of ontological transformation.

## **Fragment 4: Dialects and sociolects of Numinia**

“ _The limits of my language mean the limits of my world._ ”

—Ludwig Wittgenstein

Language is more than a means of communication: in Numinia, it is a sign of identity, a connection with tradition and a reflection of the way in which each character understands and relates to the world.

The Linguistic Variations are based on the forms of speech recognised and developed in linguistics from the studies of scholars such as Eugenio Coseriu.

The Linguistic Variations in Numinia are divided into five categories:

**Dialects** - Determined by the species, they reflect its origin and its linguistic style.

**Sociolects** - Dependent on the district and the faction, they express cultural and social identity.

**Lingos** - Belonging to each Guild, they represent its particular jargon.

### **Dialect: The Voice of the Species**

« _Every voice in Numinia is an echo of its history, of its race, of its lineage. Every word is a key that opens doors to identity, to knowledge and to the enigma of its existence._ »

In Numinia, a character's dialect is determined by their Species, since this influences their origin and their way of expression. Let us remember that these are not different languages, but linguistic variations of a common tongue, Numinid.

**CYBERNETIC**

  - **Dialect of the Biomechanicals.** Rich in technological terms and phrases structured like cybernetic sentences, speaking in code, like a computer.

  - **Use:** They use sounds and tones that imitate machine interfaces or digital communications, reflecting their biomechanical nature.

  - **Example:** _"Initiating dialogue protocol... Greeting: 'Welcome, user'. Beep! Pending query detected. Processing... Trrrr! Response generated: 'Your request has been completed with 98.7% success'."_

**EPISTOLARY**

  - **Dialect of the Humanitas.** Characterised by grandiloquent vocatives and elaborate adjectives, expressing themselves with elegance and distinction, as in the register of correspondence.

  - **Use:** They use refined idiomatic expressions and refer to classical literature, showing a deep respect for knowledge and history.

  - **Example:** _"Like a thinker in his dilemma, I find myself at a crossroads of decisions. Allow me, O esteemed interlocutor, to reflect upon the ramifications of our actions with the wisdom of our ancestors."_

**PRIMORDIAL**

  - **Dialect of the Reptilians.** Marked by lexical anachronisms and archaisms, with ornate and ancient interjections.

  - **Use:** They incorporate ancestral rituals and formalities into their speech, employing ancient greetings and formulas of farewell.

  - **Example:** _"Greetings, traveller from distant lands. By the goodness of the ancestors! May the primordial winds guide thy steps. In our conversation, let us invoke the wisdom of the ancients."_

**BIZARRE**

  - **Dialect of the Cyanites.** Characterised by hyperbaton and discourse markers at the end of sentences, preferring short, aphoristic phrases.

  - **Use:** They prefer to speak in imperatives and proverbs, reflecting a culture focused on survival and on the law of the strongest.

  - **Example:** _"Hunt when the sun sets, rest when it rises. Thus dictates the law of survival. Act, do not hesitate, I must point out!"_

**PROPHETIC**

  - **Dialect of the Spectrals.** It is characterised by cryptic and enigmatic phrases, speaking in parables and cosmic references.

  - **Use:** They speak in verses and riddles, often leaving their interlocutors pondering the true meaning of their words.

  - **Example:** _"In the soft light of the third lunar phase, the end of your quest you shall descry. As rivers to oceans ceaselessly flow, so the paths of destiny shall intertwine; neither truth nor madness is there in their law, only realities that seem unchanging."_

### **Sociolect: The Language of the Environment**

This trait is related to the use of language by a class or social group. In Numinia, the Sociolect is determined by the character's District, since each District is associated with a Faction, and the Factions are the social groups.

**ENLIGHTENED**

  - Sociolect of the inhabitants of the Vitruvian District, linked to the Faction of the Hermeticists.

  - **Use:** Educated and poetic; rich in rhetorical figures, tropes and ornamentation of language ( _culteranismo_ ).

  - **Main trait:** Periphrastic language.

  - **Positive aspect:** Descriptive, evocative.

  - **Negative aspect:** It can be somewhat pompous.

  - **Example:** « _At the dawn of our times, beneath the celestial vault adorned by stellar radiance, we find ourselves, you and I, at this crossroads, witnesses to the majesty of the universe._ » (Instead of saying: « _We are beneath the stars._ »)

**HISTRIONIC**

  - Sociolect of the inhabitants of the Ouroboros District, linked to the Faction of the Heirs of Eleusis.

  - **Use:** Casual and friendly; rich in colloquial formulas, neologisms and witty wordplay ( _conceptismo_ ).

  - **Main trait:** Plain language.

  - **Positive aspect:** Fun, sharp.

  - **Negative aspect:** It can be somewhat coarse.

  - **Example:** « _Hey, did you know the new thing looks like a flying contraption? It flies faster than your granny on her broomstick!_ » (Instead of saying: « _The new aerial device is very efficient_ » _, and, as an ingenious conceptual play, it also implies that the interlocutor's grandmother is a witch._ )

**PROFESSORIAL**

  - Sociolect of the inhabitants of the Solomon District, associated with the Faction of the Stellar Circle.

  - **Use:** Philosophical and scientific; abounding in technical terms, learned words and formulaic structures ( _criticism_ ).

  - **Main trait:** Learned language.

  - **Positive aspect:** Objective, even-handed.

  - **Negative aspect:** It can be somewhat pedantic.

  - **Example:** « _According to phenomenological theories, our perception of the event varies notably, leading us to conclude that reality is, in effect, a subjective and mutable construction._ » (Instead of saying: « _Each person sees things differently._ »)

**MYSTIC**

  - Sociolect of the inhabitants of the Sycamore District, associated with the Faction of the Neo-Atlantists.

  - **Use:** Modern and transgressive; rich in lexical crossings, onomatopoeias and morphosyntactic deviations ( _Dadaism_ ).

  - **Main trait:** Avant-garde language.

  - **Positive aspect:** Creative, symbolic.

  - **Negative aspect:** It can be somewhat absurd.

  - **Example:** « _The voices of tomorrow whisper in codes of colour; each tone a word, each shade a story in this chronodance that, dim bada-bim, defies linear language._ » (Instead of saying: « _The future is uncertain and exciting._ »)

### **Lingo: The Jargon of the Guilds**

The Lingo is a specific language used by the members of certain collectives, with a strong emphasis on vocabulary. In Numinia, the different Lingos are determined by the Guilds to which the characters belong, reflecting their specialised knowledge and their qualities. Nevertheless, like the Dialect and the Sociolect, the Lingo plays an anecdotal role for the PCs.

**TRANSMUTATIVE**

  - **Jargon of the Alchemists**, a blend of technical and artistic terms, reflecting the fusion between science and art. They use metaphors related to transformation and creation.

  - **Use:** They employ esoteric terms to describe scientific or artistic processes, lending an air of mystery and depth to their conversations.

  - **Example phrases:**

« _Let's catalyse this project._ » (To start or drive something forward).

« _It's in the sublimation phase._ » (In the process of improvement or refinement).

**MYTHOLOGICAL**

  - **Jargon of the Exegetes**, full of literary and historical references owing to their inclination towards history, literature and fantasy. They use classical or mythological analogies to describe modern situations.

  - **Use:** It is common to hear quotations from ancient texts or phrases in Latin and Greek, used to illustrate ideas or to give greater weight to their arguments.

  - **Example phrases:**

« _We are facing a labyrinth of Minos here._ » (A complicated situation).

« _It is time to invoke the muses._ » (To seek inspiration).

**PRAGMATIC**

  - **Jargon of the Procurators**, formal and structured, reflecting their focus on organisation, administration and legality. They use legal and administrative terms even in everyday conversations.

  - **Use:** Frequent use of acronyms and legal terms, speaking in a way that reflects procedures and protocols.

  - **Example phrases:**

_«That must become an RD.»_ (To become a Royal Decree).

_«Let us proceed to item X on the agenda.»_ (Central topic to be addressed).

**MARTIAL**

  - **Jargon of the Sentinels**, full of military and security terms, reflecting their role as protectors and watchers in Numinia. They use direct and concise vocabulary, suited to situations of high risk or importance.

  - **Use:** Short, direct phrases, emphasising clarity and efficiency. Coded language for specific security or defence situations.

  - **Example phrases:**

« _Code blue in sector delta._ » (Security alert in a specific area).

« _Everyone to their watch posts._ » (Order to be on alert).

In short, and to conclude with the linguistic variations, all of them should be understood as part of the mythological, literary and cultural context of Numinia, and not as traits that players are obliged to incorporate into their characters' speech. The Dialect, the Sociolect and the Lingo offer different models of expression that help to characterise the diversity of the city and its inhabitants, but it is up to the GD and the players to decide when to use them and to what extent. Thus, they may appear constantly, be employed only in certain situations or serve as narrative inspiration, without their application constituting a mechanical requirement within the game.

## **Fragment 5: Calendar and Ephemerides of Numinia**

This calendar answers to the organic logic of the world and its bond with the lunar plane, with dream, with the symbolic flow and with the resonance of the higher planes.

### **Festivities of the Lunar Cycle in Numinia**

**Nadir of the Veil (New Moon) — The Dark Council**

When the moon disappears and the Veil reaches its greatest density, Numinia enters a reflective pause. The Nadir of the Veil is the moment when the visible world seems to fade away, and decisions must be taken without light, guided by inner judgement.

  - On this night the Dark Council is convened, a solemn session of the Council of Sages of each district.

  - The progress, obstacles and drifts of civic and guild projects are reviewed.

  - Each household holds introspective gatherings. It is a night of taking stock, of assessments and commitments.

  - It is considered a propitious moment to make personal vows, close stages and take on new intentions.

  - No one laughs aloud, no one fights. Families recapitulate and confront one another. Knowledge is listened to, and silence has deliberative value.

**Kairos of the Veil (Full Moon) — The Lunar Coven**

The full moon bursts on high and halts the course of time: it is the day of the Kairos of the Veil, the instant outside the cycles, the threshold between the ascending and the descending. It is the sacred day of Numinia.

It is a unique day in each cycle, outside the usual flow, when the rhythms neither advance nor recede, but open. That is why it belongs neither to the luminous half nor to the dark one, but divides and consecrates both.

In symbolic terms:

  - It is the vertex between the revealed and the hidden.

  - No one works or fights, but thresholds are crossed, pacts are made and visions are contemplated.

Some orders also call it «the day without shadow».

  - The Lunar Coven is celebrated, a day of communion and open festivity, organised by the guilds and the civic collectives.

  - There are collective rituals, dances in the Agora Plaza, riddle games, theatrical performances and ceremonies of light.

  - Some academies open their doors with special events, revealing discoveries or unique pieces.

  - For many, it is a night of personal revelation: it is believed that the dreams of the Kairos belong not only to the dreamer, but to the collective unconscious of Numinia.

In this way, each Cycle of the Veil has three key moments:

1. **Nadir of the Veil**   - Day 1, new moon, beginning of the cycle, introspection.

2. **Full Moon of the Veil**   - Day 15, full moon, manifestation, ritual.

3. **Twilight of the Veil**   - Day 29, close of the cycle, transition.

Furthermore, in Numinia, the Twilight of the Veil ‒day 29 of each Cycle‒ is a day of suspension and recollection. On that day no one works and no official audiences are held; it is a ritual holiday, a threshold between one cycle and the next, on which the accumulated learning is honoured and the burden of the previous time is released.

What follows are the 13 festivities of the Twilight of the Veil, one for each Cycle, aligned with its symbolic meaning:

In the **Cycle of the Seed**, _The Silent Sowing_ is celebrated: citizens plant symbolic objects in urns or ritual gardens, depositing unspoken wishes, entrusted to time and to the Veil.

During the **Cycle of the Prism**, _The Day of Paths_ is commemorated, on which games of decision and of life's forkings are organised, simulating the choices that might open alternative futures.

In the **Cycle of the Heartbeat**, the festivity is _Rhythm of the Origin_, when musicians and citizens beat drums throughout the city, evoking the first pulse of the world and the awakening of the soul.

The **Cycle of the Threshold** culminates with the _Passage of the Mist_, a silent procession that crosses the city wrapped in smoke and pale lights, symbolising the transit between states of consciousness.

In the **Cycle of the Flame**, _Midnight Fire_ is celebrated: as night falls, embers are lit in public squares where heroic stories and visions are recounted, accompanied by ancestral dances.

During the **Cycle of the Bond**, the _Pact of Echoes_ takes place, a moment to seal alliances, renew oaths or be reconciled. In this festivity, recorded fragments of voice are exchanged as a symbol of connection.

The **Cycle of the Labyrinth** concludes with _The Inner Quest_, a day of introspection on which citizens walk symbolic labyrinths, consult oracles or face riddles that reflect their inner selves.

The **Cycle of the Dream** flows into the _Vigil of Silence_, an entire day without spoken words, in which communication takes place through gestures, symbols and ephemeral writing, honouring the depth of the mystery.

In the **Cycle of the Mirror**, _Masks of the Possible_ is celebrated, when each inhabitant wears a mask that represents not what they are, but what they might come to be, exploring personal futures still latent.

The **Cycle of the Legacy** culminates in the _Gift of the Open Hand_, a festivity of radical generosity, in which old or treasured objects are given away without expecting anything in return, releasing what was hoarded as a seed for others.

During the **Cycle of the Fracture**, _The Drift_ is observed, a day without a course: citizens wander freely through the city or its margins, with no predetermined destination, exploring the hidden folds of their surroundings.

The **Cycle of the Dust** ends with the _Rite of Dissolution_, in which each person writes on paper the burdens, mistakes or thoughts they wish to let go of, and then dissolves them in water, fire or wind.

And, lastly, the **Cycle of the Return** closes with the _Dawn Round_, a collective sunrise at which songs are sung to the new cycle and a small offering is made to the Veil: a flower, a word, a memory, a silence.

These festivities not only mark the passage of time, but also stitch the daily life of Numinia to its mythic dimension, inviting its citizens to live the calendar as a path of shared meaning.

### **Cycles and Ages in Numinia**

In Numinia, a year has 13 cycles of 29 days, that is, 377 days a year**.** Each cycle is equivalent to a complete lunar cycle (new moon to new moon), or what we might call a «synodic month». Furthermore, every four years a tetrad takes place.

**The Tetrad and its meaning**

Each tetrad represents a perfect symbolic alignment between the lunar cycle and the arcane rhythms of the Veil.

This gives rise to an astronomical-esoteric phenomenon: the Tide of Memory, or Full Moon of Origin.

Every 52 cycles, on one precise night, the Moon of Numinia aligns not only with the sky, but with the Akasha. That full moon ‒known as the Moon of Khepri‒ casts a reflection of all things forgotten, hidden or unspoken. Waters, mirrors, even polished metals, give back visions of past events, alternative versions or truncated futures.

This event occurs on a single night every four years, at the end of the last day of cycle 52.

**Narrative and esoteric consequences of the end of the Tetrad**

  - The Veil grows thin, and creatures such as the Umbrílegos and the Heresiarchs of the Vortex stir or try to open doors between planes

  - The Reverses of Khepri vibrate and may alter their content, letting slip passages not yet written, or written by readers of the future

  - In some colonies, dangerous rituals seek to synchronise the collective mind with the akashic echoes that resurface during the Moon of Khepri

  - The astral predictions made on this night hold valid throughout the following tetrad

**The Spectrum and the Greater Anamnesis**

The Greater Anamnesis is a ritual, cosmic and metaphysical phenomenon that occurs once every 52 lunar years = 13 tetrads = 676 cycles, a span known as a spectrum. Its name, of Platonic resonance, evokes not a mere memory, but the _act of remembering something one already knew before being born_, a kind of ontological evocation.

In Numinia, this phenomenon is neither a festival nor an anniversary: it is a planetary and spiritual event that affects at once the city, the Veil and the consciousness of its inhabitants.

**What happens during the Greater Anamnesis?**

During the days that make it up (exactly 7 cycles, from 670 to 676 of the last year of tetrad 13), the following phenomena occur:

  - <u>Resonance of latent symbols.</u> Forgotten words are heard again in dreams. Lexomorphic artefacts, octahedra and codices vibrate without being touched. Errors of meaning arise that unveil hidden truths.

_The Veil grows thin. The city murmurs with ancient echoes._

  - <u>Awakening of inherited memories.</u> Certain people manifest memories they never lived: images of the Pre-Ashen Age, extinct languages, fragments of other people's thoughts.

_No one knows whether what is remembered is true or the invention of an alternative time._

  - <u>Erosion of the narrative architecture.</u> The structures of language, logic and causality are affected.

_Stories do not end. Scenes are rewritten in real time. The past mutates._

  - <u>Appearance of heralds or parasites of the Veil.</u> Entities such as the Umbrílegos become more frequent. Some even emerge in urban areas, drawn by the general symbolic weakness.

_The world becomes a palimpsest, and the palimpsest bleeds._

  - <u>Judgement of the Fragmentarians.</u> It is said that the Fragmentarians, mysterious agents of the deep Akasha, descend to judge whether Numinia remains faithful to its ontological promise: to be the city that rebuilds the world without fixing it.

_In this judgement, some disappear. Others are raised up. Some remember._

**Why does a Spectrum span 52 years?**

Because it is the exact moment at which the balance of the 13 tetrads falls out of kilter: the artificial organism that stabilises the calendar (the technomnemonic scarab) momentarily loses control of the symbolic lunar orbits, which produces a surge of semantic entropy. The Veil ceases to hold the memories. The world remembers.

During that brief imbalance, buried memories, images of other Ages and forgotten vestiges may return to the consciousness of Numinia, confusing memory and present for a time. That is why the end of each Spectrum is watched with a mixture of expectation and caution: one never knows what the city will decide to remember.

<u>Numerical Reason: 7 as the number of decomposition and transit</u>

In Numinia the number 7 represents the cycle of instability, the crisis before the rebalancing, the minimum number for a process of deep transformation. It is a _dangerous_ figure in the Numinian calendar, since the reduction of 52 is 5+2 = 7.

  - Some texts mention that lexomorphic artefacts must not be read for 7 consecutive nights, because their lower layers of meaning are then deactivated and the higher ones, the most unstable, emerge

  - The rhythm of collapse of many zones on the periphery follows a septenary pattern: 7 days of stability → 7 days of dissolution → 7 days of reconfiguration

That is why 7 cycles (29×7 = 203 days) are considered the maximum safe period for an event of ritual destructuring. And from this the Greater Anamnesis follows.

<u>Cosmological Reason: Resonance with the total lunar spectrum</u>

The calendar of Numinia is based on lunar cycles of 29 days. As has already been seen, then, 1 _spectrum_ is 52 lunar years, equivalent to 676 cycles.

Now, each tetrad has 52 cycles.

  - The Greater Anamnesis appears at the end of cycle 676

  - The last 7 cycles (670–676) are spaced outside the harmonic frame of the tetrads

  - According to _Numinian cosmology_, the last spectrum of the wheel causes an anomalous resonance between the stabilising satellites and the technomnemonic organism, which takes 7 full cycles to restore the rhythmic frequency of the Veil

This is interpreted as a controlled anomaly, necessary for the Akasha to _breathe_.

<u>Mythic Reason: the 7 Forbidden Names of the City</u>

In a certain passage of the Song of Shadows and Light, it is stated that Numinia has had seven previous names, each corresponding to a phase of its mythic existence. During the Greater Anamnesis:

  - Each cycle unveils a Former Name, and with it, a kind of knowledge, a threat or a redemption

  - These names cannot be uttered without consequences, and there are those who hold that the true founders of Numinia are still trapped in these names, waiting to be remembered

  - The complete ritual of the Greater Anamnesis only closes if at least one of those names is understood without being uttered

## **Fragment 6: Other groups of Numinia**

In Numinia, civic life is not limited to the official bodies or the recognised guilds. On the margins of institutional life, in the interstices of knowledge and law, autonomous groups, anomalous collectives and specialised bodies have flourished, performing functions fundamental to the cultural, symbolic and practical life of the city. Some operate under the protection of the great institutions; others, in the shadows, guided by ideals or impulses that escape normative control.

Each of them, however, reflects a deep need of the Numinian fabric: the custody of mystery, the exploration of the unexplored, the reinterpretation of the written, the protection of the fragile. They are many voices, between the formal and the insurgent, that make up the plural song of a city always in transformation.

### **The Legion of the Threshold**

The Legion of the Threshold is the invisible wall that rises when Numinia trembles. They do not patrol the streets or impose everyday order: they sleep among the shadows of the Agora, watch from the rooftops of the city, and only spring into action when the veil of peace is torn. They are an elite force, born not for war, but to prevent it. Each member has passed a rigorous _Test of Serenity_, which guarantees not only lethal skill, but restrained will. Custodians of the limit, they are the threshold between the city and whatever threatens its soul.

The name of the Legion of the Threshold holds a deep symbolism that connects directly with its role in Numinia and with the way in which this military force of peace operates at critical moments.

**Meaning of the Name**

The term _Threshold_ carries a double symbolic charge in this context:

1. The Threshold as frontier:

The Legion of the Threshold acts on the boundary between order and chaos, intervening only when tensions threaten to overflow the balance of Numinia. Thus, the Legion becomes the barrier that prevents conflict from escalating into open war or into the dissolution of the social order.

2. The Threshold as transition:

The Legion appears at moments of the greatest uncertainty: during political changes, disputes between guilds and factions, or at those instants when the security of the city totters. They are the presence that emerges at the edge, when the system finds itself in a critical phase and the passage between stability and collapse seems inevitable.

**The Threshold in the Tradition of Numinia**

The name is also related to esoteric beliefs within the city. In certain hermetic circles, the Threshold is described as the intermediate space between the physical world and the ethereal plane, the point where reality dissolves into uncertainty. According to this view, the Legion of the Threshold symbolises those who face ambiguity, who watch over the fissures of the system and preserve the delicate balance between the forces of Numinia.

**The Motto of the Legion of the Threshold**

Its motto reinforces this symbolism:

«_We are neither the light nor the shadow; we are the edge that separates them_.»

This motto reflects the neutral essence of the Legion, which acts neither out of ambition nor in search of prominence, but as a regulating power that prevents any faction or force from unbalancing the delicate order of Numinia.

The Legion of the Threshold not only has a strategic function, but also exercises a powerful symbolic action: they are the guardians of the balance, those who rise up when the city is about to cross the limit into conflict or destruction.

### **The Chamber of Palimpsests**

_“Nothing remains, everything resounds. The texts of Numinia are echoes in transit.”_

In the deepest reaches of the Atlantis Library, hidden behind a wall of moving symbols that can only be read by eyes trained in the semiosis of the Veil, lies the Chamber of Palimpsests: the highest-ranking body among the Exegetes, devoted to the restoration and rewriting of the leximorphic artefacts of Numinia.

The texts of Numinia do not dwell in parchments, books or screens; instead they are encoded in translucent octahedra, luminous structures carved from memory crystals. Each octahedron holds cryptographic chains of symbolic and textual content that are projected on simultaneous levels: image, sound, emotion, concept and archetype. But these devices are not static. They are alive. Their content fluctuates over time; it degrades, becomes encrypted, is transfigured. It is not static, but varies with time.

The mission of the Chamber of Palimpsests is to preserve what is mutable, restoring decaying content without breaking the natural flow of its transformation. Far from fixing a canonical version, the Hermeneuts who make it up —all prominent members of the Guild of the Exegetes— accept that texts are ontological entities in perpetual mutation, whose truth lies in their drift, not in their origin.

For this reason, the Chamber must constantly restore and reinterpret these archaic texts. And so, little by little, the writings slightly alter their content until, with the passing of the aeons, they end up becoming completely different texts. But as a text fades away, it reappears in another form, inside another octahedron.

Among the tasks of the Chamber of Palimpsests are:

  - Ritual rewriting of fragmented texts, where restoration is carried out by analogy, resonance or symbolic synaesthesia.

  - Synchronisation of echoes: detecting missing fragments in other artefacts and reconstructing their hidden links.

  - Akashic translation: a complex practice that connects the artefacts to the Akashic Archives, allowing lost passages to be glimpsed through altered states of reading.

Many of its members work in a trance, others in absolute silence. Some write only in forgotten scripts or under particular phases of the moon. All of them are Hermeneuts of the Ancestral, philosophers of the fold, restorers of the impossible. They are, moreover, experts in symbology, in plurisemantics, in occult philosophy. And, contrary to what people believe, the members of the Chamber do not only work cloistered in the Atlantis Library; they must often travel the peripheries of Numinia in search of information and vestiges of the texts whose nature they interpret.

For the Chamber of Palimpsests, every text is a creature. And every restoration, an act of reincarnation.

### **The Summa Archive**

<u>Dependency: Concordia Council</u>

<u>Nature: Autonomous body for interterritorial research</u>

<u>Seat: Itinerant (central archive located in the Catacombs of the Historical Society)</u>

The _Summa Archive_ ‒colloquially known as «The Archive»‒ is a scientific and exploratory institution founded by joint decree of the Concordia Council and endorsed by the Oracles. Its main function is the systematic and multidisciplinary study of the Peripheral Zones of Numinia, territories outside the urban core characterised by their geological instability, their marginal ecosystems and their anomalous phenomena.

The Archive seeks to know what lies beyond, but also to contain and understand whatever may represent a threat or a revelation for the city.

**Fields of study**

  - Mutational geography: living cartography of changing regions

  - Geology and residual magnetism: analysis of impossible materials and formations of uncertain origin

  - Marginal zoology: classification of mutant species, cryptids and bio-etheric entities

  - Spectral botany: luminous, fungiform or symbiotic flora that is hard to replicate

  - Erratic anthropology: study of nomadic settlers, extinct cultures and abandoned ritual structures

  - Field linguistics: recovery of forgotten dialects and symbolic systems

**Aleph Section**

Within the Summa Archive there is a restricted division, informally known as «the sealed heart of the Archive». Its official name is the Aleph Section, and its work consists of gathering, documenting and investigating those phenomena that are unusual, incongruous or impossible to assimilate into the established sciences:

  - Luminous or acoustic apparitions with no physical origin

  - Unidentifiable objects that appear or disappear without a trace

  - Ruins that exist only on certain days of the lunar calendar

  - Entities of extradimensional origin

  - Temporal echoes and events that break causal linearity

  - Emergent symbols that replicate spontaneously in different, unconnected zones

The researchers of this section sign pacts of silence and work under conditions of high cognitive security. Their files are classified, and many reports may only be read by members of the Council of Sages or, exceptionally, by the Oracles.

**Hidden mission**

Some rumours hold that the Aleph Section does not merely record phenomena, but guards _anomalous artefacts_ found in the field, such as in the Inversion Rift of the Neuma Subvale. Others believe it is investigating an ancient, never-confirmed hypothesis: that the peripheral zones are not the limits of Numinia... but its future fragments, seeping into the present.

### **Commune of the Invisible Light**

« _Every city casts a shadow. We inhabit its own._ »

The Commune of the Invisible Light is a clandestine, decentralised organisation that operates in the fissures of Numinia's urban, legal and symbolic fabric. It has no visible leaders or defined hierarchical structure, and its members ‒if indeed they know one another‒ seldom present themselves with permanent names or identities. They communicate through coded signs, glyphs scattered across marginal zones, sound transmissions hidden in forgotten frequencies, or poetic traces that only the initiated can interpret.

Their presence is constant, yet diffuse. To some, they are guardians of the people, anonymous heirs of the nomadic soul that forged Numinia; to others, they are dangerous saboteurs, technopirates, cultists of disorder. None of these readings is entirely false. The Commune protects those whom the system has forgotten or repressed: biological anomalies, exiled intelligences, dysfunctional constructs, unregistered citizens. They operate where the law does not reach, on the margins where the Veil weakens and the surveillance of the systems becomes erratic.

Among their most frequent actions are:

  - Surgical sabotage of mechanisms of civic control, scanning towers, censorship algorithms or protocols of digital exclusion

  - Covert interventions to protect nomadic or isolated communities, distributing stolen resources or repurposed technology

  - Parallel expeditions to the peripheral zones of Numinia to plunder forgotten artefacts, reprogramme them or prevent their institutional use

Although they profess no explicit doctrine, their actions are inspired by a radical ethic of decentralisation, autonomy, alternative justice and symbolic liberation. Their internal motto is said to be: « _What cannot be seen cannot be dominated._ »

There are signs that some factions of the city sympathise with them ‒even within the Council of Sages or among the Vernaculars‒, but none admits it openly. Their influence spreads through rumours, invisible gestures and the sudden eruption of freedom where only surveillance once reigned. They are the uncomfortable echo of a city that never ceases to dream itself.

### **Semi-religious Currents in Numinia**

Numinia recognises no institutional religion, but there are philosophical-esoteric currents that link the ancestral, the mythological and the digital with the inner processes of consciousness. These beliefs have no dogmas, only symbolic practices that help one to inhabit the Veil.

**The Cultivators of the Akasha**

They believe that every experience leaves a trace in the Akasha, and that the soul is a series of records that can be read, corrected or integrated.

  - They practise the ritual reading of the past

  - They venerate concepts such as Resonance, Testimony and the Inner Sequence

  - They are found on the margins of the academies and in some temples

**The Heresiarchs of the Vortex**

They hold that the city is a living algorithm, and that there are fragments of hidden code that can awaken dormant powers.

  - They venerate the Fractal Code, a supposed structure that runs through reality, language and dream

  - They practise rites of disconnection and inner reprogramming

  - They tend to appear at festivities wearing geometric masks

**The Circle of Khepri**

It is not a sect, but a mystery-based philosophical order, centred on the symbolic figure of Khepri, becoming and renewal.

  - They practise symbolic metamorphosis, represented in rites of passage and public rituals

  - Their members bear translucent marks on the skin (digital or symbolic) that narrate their process of transformation

### **Small official bodies**

Finally, on the margins of Numinia's formal structures there exist more flexible and organic forms of association that respond to specific needs for collaboration and joint action. Lodges, leagues and brotherhoods arise as expressions of the city's social dynamism: networks of alliance and knowledge that cross the boundaries of guilds and factions to face specific challenges, take on shared missions or explore new forms of coexistence. They represent the strength of the bond over statute, the value of the cause over hierarchy.

**Lodges**

Lodges are groups that include individuals of the same Faction but belonging to different Guilds, devoted to working towards a purpose that requires diverse skills and that focuses specifically on one of the fields of development. Lodges usually have a master, counsellor or leader of some kind.

**Leagues**

These are small syncretic associations that bring together individuals from different Factions but of the same Guild. These alliances, like lodges, usually have a specific and occasional purpose in which it becomes necessary or advantageous to exchange knowledge from the different fields of development. Some well-knit and successful leagues manage to consolidate themselves to take on new challenges and adventures.

**Brotherhoods**

Brotherhoods have a representative and eclectic character, since they are formed specifically to carry out some special task or mission, regardless of the factions or guilds of their members, fulfilling the role of the city's delegated group. As a rule, greater weight is given to the brotherhood covering a range of positions appropriate to the mission in question.

# **Annex: The Black Market of Numinia**

In Numinia there is an official trade in objects, technologies and artefacts, subject to the city's rules of production and circulation. But there are things that never reach its shop windows. Objects that are too old, too singular, restricted by the institutions or simply impossible to justify in a register of provenance end up finding other paths.

All those paths lead, sooner or later, to the Street of Mysteries, in the Old Quarter of the Ouroboros District.

There lies the so-called Black Market of Numinia, although the name can be misleading. There is no building that houses it, nor a door behind which clandestine trade officially begins. The market blends into the street itself: it occupies stalls, entrance halls, arcades and back rooms; it appears beneath awnings blackened by time or upon makeshift tables made of crates, and it stretches along side alleys whose existence seems to depend on the hour at which one looks for them. It is, after all, a clandestine market.

Ouroboros is the district where reality folds back on itself and the rules may change in the middle of the game; the Street of Mysteries has turned that principle into a peculiar economy.

Not all the vendors remain there permanently. Some always occupy the same stall; others appear only at certain moments. There are merchants who do not announce what they sell, intermediaries who only speak after receiving a password, and dealers who seem to know which object the buyer is looking for before the buyer has decided to look for it.

In the Black Market, finding the right vendor can be as important as having the means to pay them.

**What is bought and what is found**

The Black Market is the main point of circulation for objects that remain outside Numinia's ordinary channels. Relics, Weapons and all manner of Rarities may turn up there, both technological Residues and Proscribed items originating from restricted or forbidden experimental research.

But this does not mean that the Black Market is the source of these objects.

Many Residues come from old museums, closed warehouses, disused institutional stores or abandoned sites. Others are recovered on the periphery, where vestiges and treasures buried beneath the desert lands can still be found. The Proscribed, for their part, may come from clandestine laboratories, cancelled research or technological facilities whose contents should never have entered ordinary circulation.

The Black Market is simply the place where everything that Numinia loses, forbids, forgets or does not quite know where to keep ends up acquiring a price.

This allows the GD to introduce these objects in two ways. They may be found during adventures ‒as a reward for exploration, the fruit of an investigation or a chance discovery‒ or deliberately acquired in the Street of Mysteries. Finding them rewards curiosity; buying them makes it possible to pursue a concrete need.

**Prism Cells as currency**

The Black Market does not usually trade in conventional monetary units when it comes to numinic objects of genuine interest. Its preferred currency is Prism Cells.

The reason is not merely economic. A Cell represents an affinity acquired by the character and contains a resonance that can be consumed to alter their relationship with Numinia. Its value derives precisely from that capacity.

When a character hands over Prism Cells in exchange for an object, they are consumed and vanish from their pool, just as when they are used to intervene in a Roll or a State. But, as with the other uses of Prism Cells, the PC can only use and spend the Prism Cells that belong to their Faction.

This makes every purchase a meaningful decision. The same Cells that allow one to obtain a powerful Relic might be needed later to face the consequences of an adventure. The Black Market does not consume resources alone: it consumes possibilities.

**Availability**

That an object appears among the goods of the Black Market does not mean it is always available.

The GD decides what can be found in the Street of Mysteries at any given moment. The most common objects may turn up relatively often; the most extraordinary ones should require investigation, contacts, Rumourology or some small prior undertaking before there is even an opportunity to buy them.

In this way, the price in Cells indicates how much it costs to obtain an object once it has been located; it does not guarantee that a specimen is patiently waiting on a shelf.

This distinction is especially important for exceptional Relics and Proscribed items. Some may appear only once during a campaign. Others may be in the possession of someone who does not wish to sell them. And certain vendors will accept Cells with enthusiasm, but will also demand information, favours or exchanges. Clandestinity, after all, would lose much of its charm if it came with an up-to-date catalogue and a returns service.

**The price of the extraordinary**

Black Market prices are expressed in Prism Cells and are based chiefly on the scope of the object's properties, its versatility and its exceptionality within the game.

The price does not necessarily represent its material value. An ancient machine of enormous dimensions may be worth less than a tiny artefact capable of intervening in the Veil. Nor does it determine how much «power» an object has in absolute terms, but rather how much it costs to deliberately introduce that possibility into the character's repertoire.

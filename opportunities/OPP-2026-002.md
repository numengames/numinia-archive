# OPP-2026-002 — a technology community's conference on artificial intelligence

> **Summary:** A three-day conference of a technology community, over a
> thousand attendees, in autumn 2026. The house proposed
> live gamification — roles and territories as a Renaissance workshop — for
> 10,000 EUR before tax, against an estimated value of 21,000 EUR. The
> sixth iteration of the house's event proposal, and the most worked.
> **Epistemic:** Why was it lost, and what does the open door mean for
> next year?
> **Pragmatic:** Closed. A new approach next year opens a new record that
> names this one.
> **Audience:** Sales · Oracles

## Need

Reconstructed from the proposal sent, not from a conversation written at
the time; the client's own words are not on record.

- **Objectives.** Attendees leave with validated connections, actionable
  intelligence and a shared reading of the ecosystem; sponsors get leads
  and measurable impact instead of a logo on a wall.
- **Participants.** Over a thousand: developers, entrepreneurs, experts,
  companies, patrons and sponsors.
- **Conditions.** Three days, a large venue, autumn 2026;
  five territories on the floor, five cartographers and two game masters.
- **What the responsible will see.** Not agreed in the proposal. The
  house's reading: **results** — validated connections and sponsor leads,
  reported after the event.

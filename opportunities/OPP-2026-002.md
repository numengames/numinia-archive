# OPP-2026-002 — a large insurer's first day

> **Summary:** A large insurer could welcome its new employees with a
> first day that is both a live session and a virtual place they return
> to. A trial run of the sales process: a proposal of 8,000 EUR paid on
> signature, plus 20 % a year for maintenance, is drafted before any
> conversation with the client.
> **Epistemic:** Is this opportunity worth pursuing, and what is still
> unknown about it — nearly everything the client has not yet said?
> **Pragmatic:** Hold the first conversation before sending anything;
> use this record to test the moulds and the pipeline tool end to end.
> **Audience:** Sales · Oracles

## Need

Written from the Oracle's brief, not from the client. Every line is the
house's hypothesis until the first conversation confirms or replaces it.

- **Objectives.** A new employee ends the first day knowing the company,
  its people and how work is done there — having walked it, not heard it.
  *Unconfirmed: the client's own words are missing.*
- **Learners.** New employees, by intake. How many per intake, how many
  intakes a year, and from which devices they enter: unknown.
- **Conditions.** A live session on the first day, and a virtual place the
  new employee can return to afterwards. Where, when, and what support the
  client gives: unknown.
- **What the responsible will see.** Not yet agreed. The house proposes
  **behaviour** — the questions a new employee still has to ask in the
  first month — as with the retailer's first day; the client chooses.

## Notes

- 2026-09-28. **This is a trial.** The Oracle asked for it to see the
  moulds, the record and the tool work on a real kind of buyer. It holds
  the house's price but no word from the client; the stage stays `lead`,
  and the proposal stays `draft`, until a first conversation happens. If
  none does by the next date, the record closes as lost for `silence`, or
  is withdrawn as opened by mistake — the Oracle's call.
- The price is the Oracle's: 8,000 EUR before tax, paid in full on
  signature; maintenance, 20 % of that — 1,600 EUR a year before tax.
- **Not in the offer yet.** `OPS-012` defines Training as the virtual
  place; the **live session** on the first day is not described there.
  Either the offer record grows a line for it, or the live part is quoted
  under the events offer when one exists. The Oracle decides before this
  proposal is sent.
- The fit question: yes on its face — a first day is the offer's own first
  case. What would make it no: a client who wants a one-off party rather
  than a procedure their people rehearse.

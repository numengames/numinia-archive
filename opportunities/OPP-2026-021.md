# OPP-2026-021 — The Community of Madrid's grant for creating a video game

> **Summary:** A grant of up to 31,250 € to make the prototype of one narrative, original, unpublished video game, for a micro or small company with a seat in the Madrid region. 500,000 € for sixteen grants in 2026; fifteen working days to apply, around March. Paid once, after justification. Chance medium: everything fits; the certificates are still to get.
> **Epistemic:** Can the house take this money, and carry the work until it is paid?

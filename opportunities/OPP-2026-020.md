# OPP-2026-020 — a non-profit startup accelerator in Madrid

> **Summary:** The video game campus tender of a municipality of 3.3 M
> people has an accelerator lot — startups in video games,
> esports, applied technology and gamification — that neither the house nor
> this accelerator can bid for alone. Together, behind a large bidder that
> brings the turnover, the past works and the ISO certificate, they can be
> the programme: the accelerator runs it, the house brings 3D world
> workshops, gamification and game-design mentoring.
> **Epistemic:** Does the accelerator go for the lot, with whom, and is
> there a place in that team for the house — named in the offer?
> **Pragmatic:** One message this week; the partnership closed before the
> last week of October, because the bidder must name its subcontractors
> and their share in the offer, which closes 3 November.
> **Audience:** Sales · Oracles

## Need

- **What the lot buys.** Lot 4 of the campus tender: the acceleration
  programme of the campus for companies in video games, esports, applied
  technology and gamification, 36 months plus two of extension; estimated
  value of the lot 6,227,433 € before tax (the notice, per lot).
- **What it asks of the bidder.** Turnover of at least 2,235,663 € in the
  best of three years, similar works of at least 1,043,310 €, ISO 9001;
  the tender is harmonised, so the exemption of LCSP art. 90.4 for young
  companies does not apply. Neither the house (under 50,000 € a year) nor
  the accelerator (a non-profit association; turnover not verified, an
  aggregator's estimate is well under a million) reaches it, alone or
  together.
- **Where the two fit.** As subcontractors declared in the offer of a
  bidder that does reach it. Subcontracting is allowed in this tender; the
  bidder names the part, the amount and the subcontractor (the notice).
  The judged quarter of the score (25 of 100 per lot) is where a known
  accelerator and a game studio add points.
- **Who could lead.** Those running the campus under the current contract,
  which ends and is being renewed: the joint venture that runs the
  development centre and its accelerator, the company that runs
  communication, museum and events, and the consultancy that runs the
  technical office (awards of file 300/2021/00680, public).

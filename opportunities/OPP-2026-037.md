# OPP-2026-037 — A municipality of 90,000 in the Madrid region: its schools' science fair

> **Summary:** Organise the council's school science fair: five school days (16–20 November 2026, and again in the 2027/28 year), 100 pupils a day from the last years of primary to the second of secondary, a welcome talk and four workshops at once — experimental science, robotics and programming, astronomy, mathematics. 31,854 € before tax for two editions. No solvency is asked; it pays one invoice after each fair; it needs a teaching team the house does not have. Chance medium, with a partner. Offers close 16 October.
> **Epistemic:** Can the house close, within a week, a partner that brings the teaching team, and carry the fair until it is paid?
> **Pragmatic:** Decide by 9 October: a partner for the teaching team, or let it pass. ROLECE is applied for.
> **Audience:** Sales · Oracles

## Need

The council buys a five-day science fair for its schools, run by the contractor end to end: the image, contact with the schools, materials and robotics kits, the welcome talk, the four workshops, and a final report. It is the house's domain — science and education workshops and events (`OPS-018`, what the house makes) — and three of the four award criteria are formulas the house can win on: price 48, extra workshop proposals 30 (four per theme for the maximum), one extra school day 22. The award has no technical memory: a better idea scores nothing, only the formulas do.

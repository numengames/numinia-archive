# A voyage through the ecosystem — for a digital-economy association's forum

> **Summary:** Live gamification for a one-day forum of 150–200 attendees:
> six navigation roles, five territories, a crew of three cartographers
> and two game masters, four deliverables after. 2,000 EUR + tax, about
> 10 EUR per attendee.
> **Epistemic:** What was offered to the forum, and on what terms?
> **Pragmatic:** The archive's rendering of the proposal sent; check a
> delivery or an invoice against it.
> **Audience:** Sales · Oracles · the client

*Transcribed into the mould from the designed original sent to the client.*

## 1. Objectives

Turn the attendees into a community of exploration able to detect trends,
contrast perspectives and build a shared orientation, so that the forum
keeps, after the day, the intelligence its conversations produced.

For 150 to 200 attendees of a one-day international forum on responsible
technology, September 2026.

## 2. Why us

- **The method.** Roles and Territories on a navigation metaphor, built on
  behavioural design and the Octalysis framework.
- **Who runs it.** Three cartographers and two game masters.
- *Not in the original: an earlier case with the client unnamed.*

## 3. How it teaches, and how it measures

Six roles — **Spyglass**, **Sextant**, **Compass**, **Helm**, **Logbook**,
**Anchor**. Five territories:

| Territory | What attendees do | What it yields |
|---|---|---|
| **The Lighthouse** | Contribute emerging signals on a large board from their role | signals |
| **The Port** | Those who built a Route join closed conversations with experts on the same theme | convergence |
| **The Atlas** | Bring arguments to the ecosystem's big debates on a board, for tokens, and build the event's map | trends |
| **The Shipyard** | Turn tokens into navigation charts with specialised knowledge | intelligence |
| **The Trenches** | Leave open questions to find the ecosystem's most relevant uncertainties | uncertainty |

**Level:** results — **Indicator:** *not stated in the original.*

**What each attendee leaves with:** validated connections, actionable
intelligence, orientation.

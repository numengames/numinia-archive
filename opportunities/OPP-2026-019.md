# OPP-2026-019 — a regional digital agency, a simulator for a marble trades school

> **Summary:** Sold by aggregators as "a multimedia platform on the marble
> trades with 3D models"; the terms buy a commercial multi-machine
> simulator — forklift, loader, gantry crane — as a permanent licence with
> a copy-protection key, a laptop, a 4K screen, joysticks, a steering wheel
> and an industrial crane pendant, installed in 20 days. 21,039 € before
> tax. Declined: the house would only pass on another maker's product.
> **Epistemic:** Why was this tender declined, so the next sweep does not read it again?
> **Pragmatic:** Read it before trusting an aggregator's summary of a "simulator".
> **Audience:** Sales · Oracles

## Criteria

Read on 1 October 2026 from the justification memo, the administrative
terms' Annex I and the technical terms, downloaded through the regional
portal's search service.

| Criterion | The call asks | The house | Meets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fit | a commercial multi-machine simulator: permanent licence, copy-protection key, laptop, 4K screen, joystick, steering wheel, crane pendant, touch monitor (Annex I §1) | the house does not make or resell simulators or hardware | no |
| Solvency | none: an abridged simplified procedure exempts it (LCSP art. 159.6) | — | yes |
| Award | price 70 points, warranty 30 (36 to 60 months); no judgement criteria (Annex I §7) | only price and a longer warranty score | no |
| Time | delivery and installation within 20 days of signing, a training course on site in Almería (Annex I §3) | — | check |
| Cash | one payment after acceptance: 30 days to accept, then payment; the hardware is bought first (Annex I §11) | the house cannot pre-finance hardware | no |
| Platform | offers through the region's own portal, SiREC, not the state platform (Annex I §5) | no account there | check |

## Notes

- 2026-10-01. The lesson that became a rule of the record standard: the
  aggregator's summary invented the object. The code 34150000-3 "Simulators"
  covers a software simulator and an industrial one with its hardware
  alike; only the technical terms tell them apart.
- The regional portal is an application with no content in its pages; its
  documents were reached through its search service (the method is in the
  sales kit's README).

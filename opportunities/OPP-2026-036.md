# OPP-2026-036 — Creative Europe MEDIA: developing a video game or an immersive experience

> **Summary:** Up to 200,000 € as a lump sum to develop an original video game or interactive immersive experience, from the idea to a playable prototype; 70 % paid in advance; a company may apply alone. 13.5 M € in the call; it closes on 2 February 2027. The entry ticket: the house must itself have produced a finished game that was sold, with revenue, since 1 January 2024. Chance medium: everything else fits.
> **Epistemic:** Can the house show a finished game of its own, sold, before the call closes — and which new work would it present?
> **Pragmatic:** Republish one of the house's graphic adventures with a price or ads now; register the company on the portal; choose the work.
> **Audience:** Funding · Oracles

## Need

The call funds the development phase — from the first idea to the first prototype (first playable, vertical slice, alpha or demo) — of video games and interactive narrative immersive experiences with original content and commercial ambition, on any platform. It is the one Creative Europe line built for a studio like the house: no co-producer, no slate, no film. It found its way here through the opportunity watch, which first set it aside for "no published game"; the Oracle reopened it, since the house's team has published many graphic adventures online, taken down since and republishable.

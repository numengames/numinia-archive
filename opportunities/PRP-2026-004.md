# An experiential side event, in three depths — for a startup and investment event

> **Summary:** Roles and territories as a side event for a 3,000-attendee
> startup event: three tiers (Essential, Signature, Legacy), two delivery
> models (co-production, turnkey), paid by milestones. Recommended:
> Signature in co-production, 29,000 EUR, 23,200 EUR at the launch rate,
> + tax.
> **Epistemic:** What was offered to the event, and on what terms?
> **Pragmatic:** The archive's rendering of the proposal sent; the first
> event proposal with tiers — the shape of a menu.
> **Audience:** Sales · Oracles · the client

*Transcribed into the template from the designed original sent to the client.*

## 1. Objectives

Let anyone dare to approach anyone: every attendee shows what they seek
and what they bring, so conversations start faster and continue the
Tuesday after. The organiser keeps an asset — intelligence it can show
sponsors — that makes every sponsorship worth more next year.

For an event of over 3,000 attendees and over 100 investors and partners.

## 2. Why us

- **The method.** Roles and Territories, a proprietary system, not a
  supplier's package.
- **Narrative, AI and blockchain** combined; a resident artist adds a
  cultural layer.
- **Who runs it.** Four to eight cartographers, by tier.
- *Not in the original: an earlier case with the client unnamed* — the
  launch rate asked for the right to document this one.

## 3. How it teaches, and how it measures

Six roles — **Explorer**, **Builder**, **Booster**, **Interpreter**,
**Connector**, **Curator** — each attendee wears three: what they seek and
what they bring. Five territories:

| Territory | What attendees do | Turns … into … |
|---|---|---|
| **The Agora** | A large screen shows a Living Encyclopaedia: the event's activity, live | a living memory |
| **The Forge** | Write real challenges on a board; anyone adds ideas, solutions, contacts | problems into solutions |
| **The Bazaar** | A board of offers — mentoring, intros, feedback; each validated contribution earns a coin to claim another | capacities into opportunities |
| **The Observatory** | Cartographers gather signals from every territory; AI turns them into public hypotheses and conclusions, live | conversations into intelligence |
| **The Cabinet** | A resident artist creates a digital work live; whoever contributes and has a wallet receives a fragment | intelligence into culture |

**Level:** results — **Indicator:** *not stated as such in the original*;
it argues sponsor renewal: data on real connections and engagement for the
organiser to show its sponsors.

**What each attendee leaves with:** validated connections, actionable
intelligence, a fragment of the collective work.

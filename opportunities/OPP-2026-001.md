# OPP-2026-001 — a national police force's training academy

> **Summary:** An instructor at a national police academy, met at an event,
> wants trainees to rehearse a crime scene — which evidence, what to do
> with each, in which order — before a real one. A demo is built and a
> proposal of 14,500 EUR drafted; the first meeting shows both.
> **Epistemic:** Is this opportunity worth pursuing, and what is still
> unknown about it — who decides, and from which budget line?
> **Pragmatic:** Prepare the first meeting: what to show, what to hand
> over, what to ask.
> **Audience:** Sales · Oracles

## Need

Written from the Oracle's account of one informal conversation at an event;
to be confirmed with the client at the first meeting.

- **Objectives.** A trainee arriving at a crime scene knows which pieces of
  evidence are there, what is done with each one, and in which order the
  protocol says it is done — before doing it on a real scene.
- **Learners.** Future officers at the academy, in classes of ten to twenty;
  how many classes a year is unknown. They will enter from the academy's
  computers, from their own phones, and from headsets the client will value
  most.
- **Conditions.** During the academy's training, as one more format beside
  the ones they already use for the same purpose — which they are not fully
  satisfied with. Free use by the academy for the period agreed.
- **What the responsible will see.** Level: **learning** — sequence errors in
  the scene, each trainee's first walk against their last, which the space
  records on its own. Behaviour — errors in the academy's own practical
  exercises after the rehearsal — is the next level, if they measure it.

## Notes

- 2026-09-28. The fit question: yes — a sequence of acts on objects in a
  place, expensive to learn on a real scene. The house built a ten-to-twenty
  minute demo of one crime scene before any meeting, so the first meeting
  can show it and hand over the proposal at once.
- Who decides and from which budget line is not known. Public procurement:
  the price is set under the minor-contract threshold (15,000 EUR before
  tax) so that a direct award is possible; the annual maintenance is offered
  as a separate optional line for the same reason. To confirm at the meeting.
- Expansion, not in this proposal: several crime scenes as a series; live
  escape-room sessions for groups at the academy, joining the digital
  rehearsal to an analogue one.
- The proposal is drafted beside this record as PRP-2026-001, in English,
  the house's original; the Spanish translation is what the client receives.
  It is drafted, not sent: the stage stays `lead` until the meeting, and the
  transition to `proposed`, with the sender's name, will be the evidence
  that it was read and approved.
- 2026-09-29. The house works in the open, and the proposal now says so in
  its last section. The academy stays by sector until the proposal is
  handed over and the academy does not object; then its name and
  `disclosure: open`. A public buyer is named on the same terms as any
  other; its own obligation to publish an awarded minor contract (object,

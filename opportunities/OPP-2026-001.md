# OPP-2026-001 — a national police force's training academy

## Need

Written from the Oracle's account of one informal conversation at an event;
to be confirmed with the client at the first meeting.

- **Objectives.** A trainee arriving at a crime scene knows which pieces of
  evidence are there, what is done with each one, and in which order the
  protocol says it is done — before doing it on a real scene.
- **Learners.** Future officers at the academy, in classes of ten to twenty;
  how many classes a year is unknown. They will enter from the academy's
  computers, from their own phones, and from headsets the client will value
  most.
- **Conditions.** During the academy's training, as one more format beside
  the ones they already use for the same purpose — which they are not fully
  satisfied with. Free use by the academy for the period agreed.
- **What the responsible will see.** Level: **learning** — sequence errors in
  the scene, each trainee's first walk against their last, which the space
  records on its own. Behaviour — errors in the academy's own practical
  exercises after the rehearsal — is the next level, if they measure it.

# A Renaissance workshop for the ecosystem — for a technology community's conference

## 1. Objectives

Turn the attendees into a community of exploration able to detect trends,
contrast perspectives and build a shared orientation — so that the
conversations of the event survive the Monday after, and sponsors pay for
connections that close business rather than for visibility.

For over a thousand attendees — developers, entrepreneurs, experts,
companies, patrons, sponsors and members of the community — at a
three-day conference in autumn 2026.

## 2. Why us

- **The method.** Roles (how you take part) and Territories (where you
  take part), a proprietary system built on behavioural design and the
  Octalysis gamification framework.
- **Who runs it.** Five cartographers facilitate the dynamics and turn the
  contributions into shared maps; two game masters direct the whole
  experience, the timing, the transitions and the incidents.
- **The house.** Numen Games works where gamification, narrative design
  and AI meet; the same method is applied to organisations and
  communities.
- *Not in the original: an earlier case with the client unnamed.*

## 3. How it teaches, and how it measures

Six roles — **Lens**, **Compass**, **Hammer**, **Torch**, **Link**,
**Lever** — chosen by each attendee at the entrance and worn on the
credential. Five territories, each with its own mechanic and its output:

| Territory | What attendees do | What it yields |
|---|---|---|
| **The Atrium** | Make the community visible on a physical and virtual map while AI charts the ecosystem live | signals |
| **The Studio** | Pose sketches and turn contributions into works | ideas |
| **The Workshop** | Put their capacities in circulation and exchange them | exchange |
| **The Forum** | Face each other in debates on AI-proposed topics | criteria |
| **The Gallery** | AI turns completed works into pieces attendees acquire with the event's tokens | works |

Every territory shares a flag, a cartographer's desk, an interaction island
and a characteristic object; a credential and a quick guide are handed at
the entrance.

**Level:** results — **Indicator:** *not stated in the original.* The
house's reading: validated connections and sponsor leads, reported in the
intelligence report.

**What each attendee leaves with:** validated connections, actionable
intelligence, orientation; the works they took part in.

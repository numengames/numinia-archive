# The first day, before the first day — for a large insurer

> **Summary:** A first day in two parts: a live welcome session, and a
> virtual place of the company that new employees walk from a phone or a
> computer and return to during their first month. Judged at behaviour —
> the questions still asked in the first month. 8,000 EUR before tax, paid
> on signature; maintenance 1,600 EUR a year. Valid until 28 October 2026.
> **Epistemic:** What exactly is offered to the insurer, and on what terms?
> **Pragmatic:** A trial draft: read it against the proposal standard; do
> not send it before the first conversation replaces its hypotheses.
> **Audience:** Sales · Oracles · the client

*Trial draft. Written in English, the house's original; the client would
receive the Spanish translation. Not sent: its objectives are the house's
hypothesis until the client says them in their own words.*

## 1. Objectives

When the first day is over, a new employee knows the company — its
people, its places, how work is done there and whom to ask — because they
have walked it, not because they were told it. In the weeks after, they
go back to the same place whenever a question comes up.

For each intake of new employees, entering the virtual place from their
own phone or a computer, with a link and nothing to install. *How many per
intake, and how many intakes a year: to be confirmed with the client.*

## 2. Why us

- **We have done this before.** A large retailer's first day, rehearsed
  before the first day: lockers, contract, floor rules, the till — judged
  by the incidents of the first week. Client unnamed until it agrees to be.
- **The same method, another procedure.** A crime scene rehearsed by a
  police academy's trainees, walked from computers and headsets.
- **Who builds and runs it.** Numen Games designs places where a procedure
  is learnt by walking it — narrative and game design, 3D production,
  browser-based delivery — and its game masters run the live session.
- **What the technology asks of you.** A browser and a link: no software,
  no licence, no installation.
- **What we need from you.** Your first day as it is today — what a new
  employee must know, see and do — and one person from onboarding who can
  answer questions while we build.

## 3. How it teaches, and how it measures

One first day, three moments:

| Moment | What the new employee does | What is checked |
|---|---|---|
| **The live welcome** | Meets the intake and the company in a session run by our game masters: challenges in teams about the company, its values and its people | who took part; what each team solved |
| **The place** | Walks the virtual place of the company: its areas, its people, its rules, whom to ask for what | which rooms were walked, what was found |
| **The first month** | Returns to the place when a question comes up; a guide answers the first month's questions | the questions asked, and when |

**Level:** behaviour — **Indicator:** the questions a new employee still
has to ask their manager or onboarding in the first month, for intakes
that walked the place against earlier intakes. *Proposed by the house; the
client chooses the level before anything is built.*

**What each new employee leaves with:** their record in the place — what
they walked, what they found, the questions they asked.

## Before you agree

- **Title and objectives:** above.
- **Prerequisites:** none for the new employees. For the client: a browser;
  a description of today's first day; a person from onboarding during the
  build; a room and a date for the live session.
- **How it is delivered:** one live session on the first day of an intake;
  the place by a link, valid for twelve months.
- **How it is assessed:** by the questions asked in the first month,
  recorded by the place and compared with earlier intakes; no examination
  by us.
- **How it ends:** at twelve months the links expire unless maintenance is
  running; the client keeps every record produced.

## The three questions

- **What does whoever pays take away?** A first day their new employees
  walk — a live session and a place of the company to return to — with a
  record of each one's walk, for twelve months.
- **Where is it written?** Section 4, *Delivered*.
- **Can the whole price be seen?** Yes: section 4, tax included, and the
  yearly maintenance with its tax.

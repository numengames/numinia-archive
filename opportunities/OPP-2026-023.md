# OPP-2026-023 — The Ministry of Culture's grant for innovation in the cultural and creative industries

> **Summary:** A state grant of up to 50,000 € for for-profit micro and small companies that modernise a cultural sector; paid in full in advance. Fifteen working days, around April. Chance medium: the video game is not one of the listed sectors, so the project must be framed as cultural heritage or audiovisual arts.
> **Epistemic:** Can the house take this money, and carry the work until it is paid?
> **Pragmatic:** Prepare the next step, or decide not to apply.

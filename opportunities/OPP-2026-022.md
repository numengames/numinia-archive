# OPP-2026-022 — The Ministry of Culture's grant for video games and digital creation

> **Summary:** A state grant of up to 80,000 € for producing a video game or immersive experience of cultural interest, at most 80 % of its budget; paid in full in advance. The 2026 call is planned for the last quarter and not yet out. Chance medium: the house fits, but about one application in ten was funded in 2024 and the 2026 budget falls to 2 M €.
> **Epistemic:** Can the house take this money, and carry the work until it is paid?
> **Pragmatic:** Prepare the next step, or decide not to apply.
> **Audience:** Funding · Oracles

| Requirement | The call asks | We have | Meets |

# OPP-2026-025 — a capital city's integrated training centre for police and emergency services

> **Summary:** The training centre of a capital city's municipal police and emergency services. It bought a firearms simulator in 2025; procedures such as securing a scene or keeping the chain of evidence are not covered. The house offers a rehearsal space under the minor-contract threshold.
> **Epistemic:** Is this opportunity worth pursuing, and what is still
> unknown about it?
> **Pragmatic:** Prepare the next step, or decide to drop it.
> **Audience:** Sales · Oracles

## Need

Not yet heard from the client. What the house believes it could be: rehearsing a procedure the firearms simulator does not cover — securing a scene, the chain of custody of evidence, a first response — before the street. The four things are written at `analysed`, after the first conversation.

## Notes

- 2026-10-02. Researched from public sources only — the centre's annual
  study plan for 2026, its awarded minor contracts of 2025 and 2026 in the

  centre's own budget programme. The channels to reach it are on the
  house's target list, outside the archive.

# Numinia is the operating system, narrated

> **Summary:** Numinia is not a fiction beside the work. It is the same
> operating system Numen Games runs, stated as a city so that it can be
> understood and entered.
> **Epistemic:** Why this system is narrated at all, and what that obliges —
> the one principle every other canon here derives from.
> **Pragmatic:** Read this before asserting what Numinia is, or before
> inventing a structure, a name or a ritual that the system does not have.
> **Audience:** Everyone

**Binds:** every document, artefact and agent that speaks in Numinia's name.
**Does not bind:** who does what, how the brand sounds and looks, or why the
three levels relate as they do.

---

## 1. The definition

Numen Games is a company that builds a way of working. Numinia is that way of
working, told as a city.

The two are not a product and its marketing. They are one thing seen from two
sides: the germinal motive that wanted an organisation, the regulatory model
that gave it a shape, and the narrative projection that makes the shape
inhabitable. That relation is developed in its own canon; what this one fixes
is its consequence — **the narration is not decoration over the system, it is
the system's form of appearing**.

So Numinia is a real operating environment with a fictional surface, and every
element of it holds on both sides at once. A qualification is a guild, and it
is what a person is trained in. A field of work is a faction, and it is a
department that reports to no one. An act of work is a mission, and it is a
card in the archive. Neither half is the true one.

This is why we say that in Numinia **to play is to operate, and to operate is
to play**. Not because work should be made to feel lighter, but because a
structure that can be narrated is a structure someone can hold in their head,
and a structure someone can hold in their head is one they can act in without
being told what to do.

---

## 2. The governing principle

**Every structure of this system exists twice — as a function and as a name in
the world — and neither version may say something the other denies.**

Three obligations follow from that sentence, and they are the whole of this
canon's authority:

**A name without a function is decoration.** A guild, a rank, a ritual or a
place that corresponds to nothing anyone actually does is removed, not kept
for atmosphere. The fiction is not allowed to grow on its own.

**A function without a name is unfinished.** Something the system genuinely
does, and that has no place in the narrated city, is a gap in the city — not
proof that the city is a pretence.

**When the two disagree, the function decides and the name is rewritten.** The
world adapts to what is true. A document is never made true by adjusting
reality to match it.

---

## 3. What follows from it

**Roles are functional, not attributed.** A role in Numinia is not a label a
person carries; it is what they are doing right now, which arises from their
profile, the need of the moment and the context. A paperweight drives a nail
when the occasion calls for it. Here, to do is to be — which is why the same
person can lead one piece of work and review the next, and why neither is a
promotion.

**The system forgets on purpose.** What is narrated must remain readable, so a
corpus that only adds is out of compliance with this canon: records roll up by
period and what they taught survives the documents that carried it. Being
contained is part of being intelligible.

---

## 4. What this canon does not define

**Who does what, and what things are called.** The guilds, the factions, the
ranks, the units of activity and the mapping between in-world names and
operational ones are fixed elsewhere. This canon states that they exist and
why; it does not enumerate them.

**Why the three levels relate as they do.** The germinal motive, the
regulatory model and the narrative projection, with the semiotic reasoning
behind them, have their own canon. Two names circulate for the middle level
and both are correct: say **regulatory** when the argument is about what
governs, **functional** when it is about what runs. *Operating system* names
the whole, never one level.

**How Numinia sounds, looks and celebrates.** The voice, the palette, the
values, the symbols and the calendar of the culture belong to the canons of
brand and of visual identity.

**The rituals.** What a ritual of Numinia is, how it is convoked, prepared and
recorded, and which ones are currently held, is a protocol. Their hours are in
the calendar; an archive that writes down a meeting time is writing down the
thing that expires first.

**How a person enters or leaves.** Joining and offboarding are a path someone
walks, and a path is a protocol.

# Friends who play, build and learn

> **Summary:** Numinia was born of childhood dreams, in the decade the city
> was called back, among friends who had played and learnt together for half
> their lives. It was a game before it was a company, and that is why the
> company is a game.
> **Epistemic:** Where do we come from?
> **Pragmatic:** Tell where the house comes from in one breath — origin,
> present, future — and know which of the three a piece is speaking of.
> **Audience:** Everyone

**Binds:** every piece that tells where Numinia comes from.

---

## Origin, present, future

**Origin.** Inspired by childhood dreams, Numinia began in 2020, the decade
the city was called back: a quest to level up organisations through play,
born at a role-playing table, with the wish to leave a better world to the
ones who come after.

**Present.** We build experiences that keep changing, and we research in the
open with whoever wants to join. That keeps us nimble, and keeps us
learning.

**Future.** A city where people, digital agents and the things they make
share one game, where capitalism has a heart and growth comes from the
community.

## Who we are

**Friends.** The Oracles are bound by family and friendship, and by more
than fifteen years of adventures and learning shared. That bond comes first.

**Adventurers.** They share a passion for making this world better for those
who come next, and a tradition of learning through play — role-playing above
all — which is in the house's DNA and in everything it makes.

**Learners.** Each has spent a working life founding things, often at the
edge of technology and innovation, and as often telling stories and
explaining them.

So the order is play, build, learn and divulge — and it matters. Who each
Oracle is lives in their own card, not here.

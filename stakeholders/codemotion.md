## Who they are

A conference for developers, technical leads and digital profiles. Its Madrid edition is held at La Nave; the next one is on 12 and 13 May 2027. Between editions it runs #CodeMeet meetups, several of them on AI agents.

## How they can take part

A stage: a place to show how the house works with digital agents, in front of the people who build software.

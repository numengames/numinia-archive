## Who they are

Adigital, the Asociación Española de la Economía Digital, gathers more than 450 companies and carries the regulatory and technology agenda of Spain's digital economy. It publishes the yearly *Economía digital en España* report.

## How they can take part

An association of companies: its members are possible clients, and its events are places to show the work.

## Who they are

A non-profit initiative for early-stage technology entrepreneurs in Madrid, founded in 2009 when the word *startup* was barely heard in Europe. It runs pre-acceleration programmes, among them its Startup School and W4C for women in cybersecurity.

## How they can take part

A community of founders, and a programme whose participants can meet the house's way of working.

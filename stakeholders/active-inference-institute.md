## Who they are

A volunteer-led, non-profit open-science institute for education, research and applications of Active Inference — Karl Friston's account of how living systems perceive, act and learn by minimising surprise about their world.

## How they can take part

A source of ideas. Active inference already grounds the house's positioning (`OPS-011`) and its vocabulary (`STD-026`), and the Institute's material is listed among the sources still without a home (`DBT-023`).

# Stakeholders — every one who takes part in the system

> **Summary:** One card per stakeholder outside the house: an organisation, a public body, an event. The people and the digital agents of the house are stakeholders too, and their cards already live in `agents/`.
> **Epistemic:** Who takes part in Numen's system, whatever part they play.
> **Pragmatic:** Find who someone is before reaching them, and file a new one in the same shape.
> **Audience:** Agents · Oracles

---

A stakeholder is anyone who acts in the system: a person, a digital agent, an
organisation, a public body, an event. All of them are the same kind of thing;
what changes is the **role** each can play — a source, a peer, a community, an
association, a public body, a stage. One stakeholder can play several.

The cards are public, so they say only what each organisation publishes about
itself and how it could take part. They do not say what the house has agreed,
sold or talked about with anyone: that is an opportunity's record
(`opportunities/`, `STD-039`), where an organisation is named only once it has
agreed. Nobody's name is in a card.

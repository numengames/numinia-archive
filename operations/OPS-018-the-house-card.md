# OPS-018 — The house's card

> **Summary:** What Numen Games S.L. holds when a public body — a buyer
> with a tender or a funder with a grant, a loan or a prize — asks who it
> is: how big, how old, where, what it has done, which registers,
> certificates and cash it has, and what it does not have yet. Each line
> says what calls usually ask, what the house holds, whether that meets
> it, and what would unlock the gap. Every call is read against this card,
> requirement by requirement; a call that fails here is not recorded, and
> what it taught comes back here.
> **Epistemic:** What does a call usually ask, what does the house hold today, and what would unlock each gap?
> **Pragmatic:** Read it beside the terms or the bases before writing a
> record's criteria table; update it the day a certificate, a register, a
> year's accounts or a lesson changes it.
> **Audience:** Sales · Oracles

---

## Context

A public body asks before it reads the offer or the project. A buyer asks
that the bidder may contract, can pay its way and can do the work — and, in
the simplified procedures, that it is in the bidders' register (LCSP arts.
65, 87, 90, 159). A funder asks who may apply: size, age, seat, sector,
being up to date with the tax agency and Social Security, and how much
state aid the applicant already carries (Law 38/2003, art. 13). A call is
lost before it is read when one of these fails. This card is the house's
side of that check, written once, so no call is weighed from memory.

Spanish calls first — the State, the Community of Madrid, the town — then
the European ones.

## The card

The rows run in the order in which they sank tenders in the deep reading
of 1 October 2026, then the rows grants ask. The requirement's name is the
one a record's criteria table uses. State: **yes** (held, the evidence
named), **no** (not held), **check** (not yet known or not yet loaded).

## What the reading of 1 October 2026 taught

A sweep of the open Spanish tenders and calls, each read from its own
terms, taught the card these lessons.

- **Turnover sets the size.** The authority may ask up to one and a half
  times the value, so with under 50,000 € a year a contract above about
  33,000 € is in reach only with a partner whose solvency is added.
- **A required team sinks a house with no employees.** Named profiles with
  degrees and years of experience are met only by a partner or by staff
  hired for the contract.
- **Paid all at the end is pre-financing.** One payment after acceptance
  means carrying the whole work first; a call that pays in stages or in
  advance fits the house's cash.
- **Past works may be asked after all.** The protection for companies under
  five years (LCSP art. 90.4) holds only below the harmonised threshold and
  only for contracting authorities; above it, or from a public company that
  is not one, past works are asked.
- **ROLECE is asked in the simplified procedures.** The application filed
  before offers close is enough.
- **Liability insurance is common**, between 60,000 € and 600,000 €, and is
  sometimes accepted instead of turnover.
- **Large contracts ask ENS or ISO.** Only a partner who holds them opens
  those.
- **Tax and Social Security certificates and a 5 % guarantee** are asked of
  whoever wins; both are arranged before filing, never after the award.
- **What they really buy is in the technical terms, never in the title.** A
  "multimedia platform with 3D models of the marble trades" was a
  commercial forklift, loader and gantry-crane simulator, licence and
  joysticks included. The documents are read in this order: the
  justification memo, the administrative terms' table of characteristics,
  then the technical terms. Where the authority uses its own portal rather
  than the state platform — Andalusia's SiREC, for one — the offer is filed
  there, and the documents are reached through its search service; the
  method is in the sales kit's README.

## What the card leaves within reach

Read from the law, against the card:

- **Minor contracts, under 15,000 €** — no notice and no solvency: the
  authority asks and awards directly (LCSP art. 118). Won by being known to
  the technician before the need is written.
- **Abridged simplified procedure, under 60,000 €, not intellectual
  services** — no solvency to prove; ROLECE or its application; price and
  formula criteria only (LCSP art. 159.6).
- **Simplified procedure, under 35,000 €** — no solvency unless the terms
  say so (RGLCAP art. 11.5). In reach with ROLECE.
- **Calls for micro and small companies with a Madrid seat**, for profit,
  without a minimum age or staff, paid in advance or bridged — in reach.
- **Out of reach today** — a tender asking ENS, an ISO certificate, a team
  or a turnover above 50,000 € the house cannot add from a partner; a call
  asking a published game with sales, five years of trading, a non-profit
  applicant, or a capital the house does not have.

## What the house makes, and what it does not

**Makes:** 3D web worlds with AI agents (on Hyperfy, not Unity or Unreal);
gamified training and onboarding; culture, heritage and education workshops
and events; game design and narrative.

**Does not make:** hardware, or another maker's product passed on; desktop
industrial simulators with replicated controls; generic courses or videos;
running a centre; children's entertainment; corporate websites; supplying
staff; health or psychological services.

A sweep skips a notice whose title matches one of these, unless its
technical terms say otherwise. The pipeline tool reads this table.

## To get, in order

1. **ROLECE** — file the application now: it opens every simplified
   procedure, and the filed application is enough until it is granted.
2. **Tax and Social Security certificates** — every tender and every call
   asks; renew every six months.
3. **The representative's electronic certificate** — to sign offers and
   applications on the platforms and e-offices.
4. **The filed accounts for FY2024 and FY2025** — turnover, capital and
   equity are proved from them.
5. **The certificate of the one past work** — from the client, with amount
   and date.
6. **Professional liability insurance** — some terms accept it instead of
   turnover.
7. **The partners' ages and who leads** — two facts that open or close the
   youth and women's calls.
8. **The grant equivalent of the ENISA loan** — from ENISA; it counts
   against the de minimis ceiling.
9. **A partner with solvency, a team or a certificate** — for the tenders
   above about 33,000 € or asking what the house lacks; named per call,
   in a partner record, never in this card.
10. **A partner who makes hardware or physical structures** — for the
    tenders whose physical part is secondary (a model, a cabinet, a stand);
    found before deciding to bid, never after.
11. **A playable prototype of Numinia** — it lifts the score of every video
    game call.
12. **A European partner** — the day the house turns to European calls.

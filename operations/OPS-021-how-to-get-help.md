# How to get help from us

> **Summary:** The doors through which anyone gets help from Numen Games
> and Numinia, each with what it is for and whether it is open today:
> Discord and email are open, the data address is open, an agent that
> answers at any hour is coming.
> **Epistemic:** Where does someone with a question, a problem or a request
> go, and does anyone answer there?
> **Pragmatic:** Point a person to the right door, and keep the help page on
> numinia.com saying only what is true.
> **Audience:** Everyone

**Binds:** nothing. A register records; the help page on numinia.com/support
copies it.

## The doors

| Door | For | Address | Today |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Discord** | Questions, bugs, ideas, playing with others; a problem of conduct goes to a Sentinel there | [discord.gg/ASwwdd24pp](https://discord.gg/ASwwdd24pp) | Open |
| **Email** | Anything else; a person answers | hola@numengames.com | Open |
| **Your personal data** | Seeing, correcting, deleting or taking away what we hold about you | legal@numengames.com | Open |
| **An agent at any hour** | A first answer when no person is awake | — | Coming |

## Support is not help

The coffee cup at the foot of every site does not lead here. It leads to the
page where people back Numinia with money, numinia.com/back. The word
*support* belonged to both for a while, and a person looking for help found
a price list. Now help is help, at numinia.com/support, and backing is
backing.

## What is deliberately absent

No form. A form promises a ticket and a tracked answer, and nothing behind
it tracks one yet. When something does, it enters this table with its
status. The contact form on numen.games is not a ticket: it writes an email
to the address above.

# OPS-019 — The house's card for grants

> **Summary:** What Numen Games S.L. is when a public funder asks who may
> apply: its size, age, seat, sector, what it has published, how it stands
> with the tax agency, how much state aid it already carries, and how long
> it can wait for the money. Every call found is read against this card,
> criterion by criterion; what fails here fails there.
> **Epistemic:** For which grants, loans and prizes is the house eligible
> today, and which facts would change the answer?
> **Pragmatic:** Read it beside the call's bases before writing a grant's
> criteria table; update it when a fact below changes.
> **Audience:** Funding · Oracles

---

## 1. Context

A grant is not a sale: there is no client and no proposal, only a funder,
a call with its bases, and a list of who may apply. The General Subsidies
Law refuses anyone not up to date with the tax agency and Social Security,
and pays after the work is justified unless the bases allow an advance
(Law 38/2003, arts. 13 and 34). Most calls then add conditions of size,
age, seat and sector. This card holds the house's answer to each, once.

Spanish calls first — state, the Community of Madrid, the town — then the
European ones.

## 3. What the card leaves within reach

- **Calls for micro and small companies with a Madrid seat**, for profit,
  without a minimum age or staff — in reach.
- **Calls paid in advance** — in reach of the house's cash; calls paid only
  after justification need a bridge (a loan against the granted call).
- **Calls asking a published game with sales, five years of trading, or a
  non-profit applicant** — out of reach.
- **Calls sized by capital or equity** — out of reach while the capital is
  5,512.40 € and the equity is not loaded.
- **Youth and women's calls** — in reach only if the facts marked check
  above say so.

## 4. How a call is read against it

1. Open the call's extract in the official gazette and its bases.
2. Write one row per condition in the grant record's criteria table: what
   the call asks, what this card says, yes, no or check.
3. The chance follows from the rows, by the register: any **no** is low or
   none; any **check** keeps it below high. Write how the call pays — in
   advance, or after justification — beside it: it decides whether the
   house can carry the work.
4. A **check** that decides the chance is a task with a date: the record's
   next action.

## 5. To get, in order

1. **Tax and Social Security certificates** — every call asks; renew every
   six months.
2. **The filed accounts for FY2024 and FY2025** — turnover and equity.
3. **The partners' ages and who leads** — two facts that open or close the
   youth and women's calls.
4. **The grant equivalent of the ENISA loan** — from ENISA; it counts
   against the de minimis ceiling.
5. **A playable prototype of Numinia** — it lifts the score of every
   video game call.
6. **A European partner** — the day the house turns to European calls.

# Positioning and market

> **Summary:** What Numen Games says to a buyer: the problem it names, the
> solution it offers, who it is for, and the words used to say so. Carried
> out of `CAN-002`, where it had been sitting as canon.
> **Epistemic:** The commercial framing, separated from what the company
> believes — belief is canon, positioning is a bet that gets revised.
> **Pragmatic:** Reach for it when writing a pitch, a deck or a landing page.
> **Audience:** Oracles

**Binds:** nothing. A register records; the canon of brand states what binds.

## The problem and the solution

| | |
|---|---|
| **Problem** | Organisational models are becoming obsolete in the digital age, producing distortions such as existential monotony, which kills creativity and the human spirit. In the digital era organisations face resistance to change, lack of innovation, limited personal and collective development, and a narrow focus on social impact. |
| **Solution** | Leveling up work: games spark human organisations into vibrant, creative and collaborative adventures. An update to the work approach — antifragility, data sovereignty, zero-knowledge privacy and active inference, with gamification, adaptability, innovation and social impact. |
| **Concept (USP)** | **The game to work better.** Redefining how organisations work, using gamification and digital goods to transform their essence. |

## Context

We moved from a static, mouse-driven Web 1.0 to a touch-driven, content-creating
Web 2.0, and are moving to a web where we go beyond the screen: better
visualisation and persistent digital objects.

From the rigid confines of industrial work to the freedom of the digital age,
how organisations operate has shifted. The modern era demands workplaces as
dynamic and diverse as the people in them — a demand made by a generation
raised on multiplayer games and science fiction, who expect work to be an
adventure they embark on rather than a place they go.

## The words

**Elevator pitches.**

| Length | Text |
|---|---|
| 15 words | Empower and transform organisational culture with engaging experiences that foster fun and drive impactful social change. |
| 11 words | Foster organisational transformation and social change through engaging, fun experiences. |
| 8 words | Cultivate fun, transformative experiences for organisational and social change. |
| 7 words | Create fun, drive social change in organisations. |

**Go-to-market statement.** For proactive agents in organisations, who work
better while playing, Numinia is the game that remixes knowledge and turns
work into a game — unlike the old ways of companies, Numinia enlightens human
relationships and growth.

**Core language.** Transformative work experience · innovative approach ·
educational resources · decentralised technologies · legal by design ·
interactive learning · play · fun · immersive · engaging.

**What we are, for whom.** Games, education and an organisational incentive
system, for people in organisations and on the metaverse: technology pioneers,
gamers, metaverse lovers.

## Product shape

| | |
|---|---|
| **MVP** | An escape-room experience on the metaverse, chargeable at 1 USD per adventurer, that is fun. |
| **MLP** | A full free CC0 version an organisation can adopt, that makes it better for the humans who interact with it. |
| **Revenue models** | Hosting · building experiences · subscription · microtransactions on art and digital goods · season passes |
| **Support** | Digital agents for 24/7 first response · Discord for community support · forms for tracked requests |
| **Strengths** | Purpose · the human team · epistemic consistency · the size of the video-game market |
| **Weaknesses** | Scalability · low adoption · friction to change · regulatory uncertainty · lack of technological maturity |

Acquisition, activation, retention, referral and revenue are the funnel stages
tracked.

## Research

Three lines, run as continuous inquiry rather than as a programme with an end:
**active inference**, to predict and adapt game mechanics to behaviour;
**complex problem solving**, applying CPS frameworks to role-playing scenarios;
and **AI experiments in the metaverse**, with AI-driven characters and
scenarios in virtual and mixed reality.

## What is deliberately absent

Market sizing, revenue targets, headcount and dated milestones were carried in
`CAN-002` and are not reproduced here. They were stale on arrival — a market
table with unfilled placeholders, and a 2026–2027 objective list whose first
milestone was dated 2024. Figures belong where they are measured and revised,
not in the archive (`ADR-052`).

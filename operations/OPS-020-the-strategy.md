# The strategy: a city that plays its work

> **Summary:** How Numen Games means to get from where it is to its purpose:
> a living, open city where organisations and players work inside one game,
> organised by guilds and factions, co-writing its story — and, as the next
> milestone, a full free version any organisation can adopt.
> **Epistemic:** Purpose says why, culture says how we live; this says which
> way we are walking. Strategy is a bet, so it is a record and gets revised.
> **Pragmatic:** Reach for it when deciding whether a piece of work moves the
> house towards its next milestone, or away from it.
> **Audience:** Oracles · Agents

**Binds:** nothing. A record states the bet; the canon states what binds.

## The plan

Numen Games builds a living, decentralised internet: a gamified structure in
which education, exploration and creative collaboration are one game.
Organisations and players live in it as citizens. The city is organised in
districts aligned with factions, and its knowledge is spread among many
rather than held by one, so the experience stays fresh and keeps changing.
Citizens are not participants but co-authors of a story that never stops
evolving.

Every part of that plan already has a home in the archive:

| The plan says | Where it lives |
|---|---|
| A living, decentralised internet | The archive is the organisation: plain text, public, and whoever reads it can act in it |
| A gamified structure that blends education, exploration and collaboration | Numinia is the way the house works, told as a city |
| Districts aligned with factions | What you know is a guild, where you apply it is a faction; every document carries its territory |
| Decentralised knowledge | Decision is decentralised by design |
| Citizens as co-authors | The remix pillar; people and agents change the text |

The metaverse is not scenery in this plan. It is one of the places where the
city is played, as the organisation is another; the same rules hold in both,
on paper, in person and on a screen. It is early: today the metaverse is an
experiment for the earliest adopters. It will end up touching the whole
digital landscape, as email did, as peer-to-peer did, as the web did.

## Where we play

The industry is the meeting of two that rarely meet: the people side of
organisations, and video games. Together they offer a framework that breaks
with the inheritance of the Second Industrial Revolution, opens a path to the
digital one, and makes human relationships better on both sides.

## The next milestone: the MLP

The next milestone is the minimum lovable product, not an alpha: **a full,
free, public-domain version that any organisation can adopt, and that makes
it better for the humans who work in it.**

The minimum viable product came first — an escape room on the metaverse that
a player paid a dollar to enjoy — and it proved that play could be charged
for. The lovable product proves something else: that an organisation would
choose to live inside the game.

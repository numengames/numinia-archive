## Why draft

The bytes are verified (see `CHECK.md`) and the licence is inside the file. What is not settled is the card itself: what a history must contain, which forms an avatar has beyond its model (a portrait, a voice), and who admits an entity and how. Until an admission procedure exists, no card is `active` — the Oracle's ruling on 2026-09-20. This card moves to `active` the day that procedure admits it.

## Description

A humanoid avatar in VRM format, about 1.25 MB: a round green body with a brown pit for a head, rigged for the standard VRM humanoid skeleton, so any world or client that speaks VRM can wear it. Modelled by Polygonal Mind and released as number 88 of their *100 Avatars* round.

The file declares its own terms inside its metadata: title `88_Avocado`, author `Polygonal Mind`, licence `CC0`, commercial, violent and sexual usage allowed. That is the reason this card exists: the Avocado is the one legacy resource whose bytes say what its catalogue said.

## History

The Avocado was uploaded to the Numinia digital-goods MVP on 2026-03-30 as one of the first avatars offered to citizens. The MVP catalogue labelled it CC0 and stored it in an R2 bucket; the label was a catalogue default, not a verified claim, and the file carried no recorded hash.

On 2026-09-18 the archive reviewed all 32 legacy resources (the record is `system/SYS-005-digital-resource-register.md`). The Avocado's bytes matched Polygonal Mind's official VRM exactly, the embedded metadata said CC0, and the rightsholder's release v24.02.1 expressly offers the avatars under CC0 — three sources agreeing. An older notice in the same upstream repository says CC-BY-4.0 and asks not to resell unmodified avatars; that inconsistency is disclosed there and this card relies on the specific, later CC0 offer. The same day the unchanged file entered the resource depot `numengames/numinia-assets`, where its first copy above lives.

No one in Numinia has yet worn it in a world. When someone does, that is where this history continues.

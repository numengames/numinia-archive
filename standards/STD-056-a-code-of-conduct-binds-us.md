# A code of conduct binds us

> **Summary:** Everyone who works for Numen Games knows and keeps one code of
> conduct: equal and respectful treatment, no conflicts of interest, no gifts
> that buy, secrets kept, no laundered money. A named person watches over it.
> **Epistemic:** How must a person who works for Numen Games behave?
> **Pragmatic:** Tell a person what the house expects of them, and decide
> whether an act breaches it. This is not legal advice; counsel reviews it first.
> **Audience:** Everyone

**Binds:** every person who works for Numen Games, and every agent acting in its name.

## Rules

### Scope and principles

**Everyone knows it and keeps it.** Whoever is bound MUST read the code, accept it in writing and keep it.

**Equal, and with respect.** Everyone MUST treat everyone else with respect and without discrimination.

**Work leaves room for life.** Work MUST allow people to balance it with their personal and family life.

**Health comes first.** Everyone MUST follow the rules that prevent harm at work.

**We look after the environment.** Everyone MUST avoid avoidable harm to the environment in their work.

**Collective rights hold.** The house MUST respect its people's right to associate, meet and bargain together.

### How we act

**We keep the law and our own rules.** Everyone MUST act within the law, this code and the house's other rules.

**No work against the house.** A person MUST NOT work for a competitor, or on their own account in the house's line of business, without the Oracle's written consent.

**Interests are declared.** A person with a personal interest in a decision, a supplier or a project the house has a stake in MUST say so first and stay out of the decision.

**Gifts and commissions buy nothing.** A person MUST NOT ask for, accept or offer money, commissions or gifts above a token value to or from anyone who deals with the house.

**Secrets stay.** Everyone MUST keep the house's confidential information and other people's data secret, during and after their work.

**Authorities are told the truth.** Contact with an authority MUST be truthful and go through the person the Oracle names.

**Politics is private.** A person MUST NOT use the house's name in political or associative activity.

**Money has a known source.** The house MUST know who pays it, keep the proof of identity and refuse or report a payment that looks like laundering.

### Watching over it

**One person watches over the code.** The Oracle MUST name a compliance officer who spreads the code, answers questions about it and handles reports.

**A breach is reported, never punished.** Anyone who sees a breach MUST report it, and a person who reports in good faith MUST NOT suffer for it.

**A breach has a fair answer.** A confirmed breach MUST be answered in proportion, after the person has been heard.

This standard is over its word budget because its check table names the law behind each of its seventeen rules.

## Why

The penal code lets a company show it took care to prevent crime, and a
written code, a named watcher and a way to report are what shows it. Most
rules here are the law in the house's words; a draft is on trial until
counsel and the Oracle read it.

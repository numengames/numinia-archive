# The stages of a grant

> **Summary:** The states a call for public money passes through, from
> foreseen to paid or closed: what each means and what puts a record
> there; the kinds of money a funder gives; how it pays; and the closed list
> of reasons a grant does not happen. A grant is not a sale — no client,
> no proposal — so it has its own stages. The house's chance is the one
> scale tenders use, in the stages of a sale. The grant record cites these
> values; the funding tool reads them here and nowhere else. A register
> records: nothing in it binds by itself.
> **Epistemic:** Which stages does a grant pass through, and what moves it?

## The stages

| Stage | Means | Evidence that puts a record here | Open |
|---|---|---|---|
| `foreseen` | the call is expected: last year's, or a funder's plan of subsidies | the record exists, its days marked estimated | yes |
| `open` | the call is published and accepting applications | the extract in the official gazette | yes |
| `applied` | the application is filed | the e-office's receipt | yes |
| `granted` | the funder resolved in the house's favour | the resolution, the amount granted | yes |
| `justified` | the work is done and its justification filed | the justification's receipt | yes |
| `paid` | the money is in the bank | the payment, handed to the ledger | no |
| `denied` | the funder resolved against the house, or excluded it | the resolution and a reason below | no |
| `declined` | the house chose not to apply, or let the day pass | the closing date and a reason below | no |

A record moves forward one stage at a time, and from any open stage to
`denied` or `declined`. `paid`, `denied` and `declined` are closed; a call
repeated next year is a new record that names the old one. Every open stage
waits on a day — the call's opening, its closing, the resolution, the
justification — so a grant is overdue when its next date passes, never
stale.

## What the funder gives

| Instrument | Means |
|---|---|
| `grant` | money that is not paid back, if the work is done as promised |
| `loan` | public money lent on soft terms: paid back |
| `prize` | money for what was already done; no work to justify after |
| `programme` | services, not money: incubation, mentoring, space, credits |

## How it pays

| Payment | Means |
|---|---|
| `advance` | all or part before the work; the record's `advance` says which share |
| `on-justification` | after the work is done and justified; the house carries it until then |
| `in-kind` | no money changes hands |

The General Subsidies Law pays after justification unless the bases allow
an advance (Law 38/2003, art. 34.4); the bases decide.

## Reasons a grant does not happen

| Reason | Means |
|---|---|
| `not-eligible` | a condition of who may apply fails |
| `not-a-fit` | the call funds something the house does not make |
| `no-cash` | the house cannot carry the work until it is paid, or put in its share |
| `timing` | the day came before the dossier could |
| `outscored` | the funder scored other applications higher |
| `excluded` | the application was excluded, or the call withdrawn |
